# Dynamics of Output Tendency in the Biological Human Individual

## 0. Position and Assumptions

The object of this paper is the [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) bound by the physical exclusion described in Premise 5. Such an [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) is a biological human and a [Self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) in the upstream sense: one who can report on itself and can make a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) of itself. Its subject is what can and cannot be said about such an [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) between input and [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28).

<note>

*What is studied here is simply one person: one body, which can be in only one place at a time, with limited strength and attention. The researcher first decides whom to treat as one individual, and only then speaks about that person's affairs.*

*The whole paper on one page (plain language): when a person acts, there are only three things: "does the person want it", "can the person do it", and "what becomes of the person afterwards", and none of these can be inferred from another. What is wanted (weight) cannot be seen, and can only be summarized into a tendency from what has been done (records); whether it can be done depends on the threshold, that is, whether the needed conditions are all in place; what follows depends on the fact that an output changes the outside world, and the outside world's response then changes the next round's threshold and weight. Surviving and having children are facts that the researcher checks against a standard the researcher has first set; they are not this person's purpose. Every decision is impossible to calculate beforehand and impossible to take back afterwards, so this paper does not speak of risk, and cannot use outcomes to judge decisions; categories such as "eating", and opportunity cost, are all the researcher's distinctions or calculations. The two cases (ten synthetic people, and Lincoln) are used only for comparison, not as evidence.*

</note>

The [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) has only three [questions](concepts.md#c01), called the [Want-Can-Outcome Questions](concepts.md#c01): what I want, which corresponds to [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) and [output tendency](concepts.md#c02), cannot be read and can only be treated statistically; whether I can do it, which corresponds to the [threshold](concepts.md#c03); and what I become after doing it, which corresponds to feedback, end point, survival and reproduction. What can be said about the first question under the [Law of the Black Box](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c25) is limited by nature, so this paper has few propositions and puts weight on "what cannot be inferred"; this is a property of the [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) level, not an omission.

<note>

*To know what a person wants, we can never see into the mind, and can only see what the person did. So very little can be said about the first question; the other two have something to look at and can be discussed more. This is why this paper is mostly about "this cannot be inferred".*

</note>

This paper uses only two quantities:

- **[Weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06)**: determined by the [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) that has [arrived at](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) the [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20), possibly different at every [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09), and unobservable.
- **[Records](concepts.md#c06) and [output tendency](concepts.md#c02)**: [records](concepts.md#c06) are the researcher's registrations of the [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) that actually occurred within the time window; a [tendency](concepts.md#c02) is the researcher's generalization of the shapes that recur in the [records](concepts.md#c06), and must be stated together with the [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23), the time window, the classification rule, and the [threshold](concepts.md#c03) conditions.

<note>

*"Weight" cannot be seen; it is determined by everything in the person's past. All we can see is what the person actually did (records), and the "this person probably often does this" (tendency) summarized from the records. A tendency is the researcher's generalization; it is not a peek into the person's mind.*

</note>

Traditional theories explain people with desire, need, motive, utility, preference, value, and goal; these words presuppose that the researcher knows what a person thinks inside. By the [Law of the Black Box](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c25), this paper does not use the whole group, and does not rewrite them word by word. The [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) certainly has judgments; it is only that the researcher cannot read the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06), so the researcher can only work statistically and cannot pretend to know. Nor does this paper treat "cost" as a quantity of the [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20): what counts as a sacrifice is itself the researcher's [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18); this paper records only the [threshold](concepts.md#c03), the starting point, and the end point. The researcher may separately carry out a comparison calculation of [opportunity cost](concepts.md#c05), whose status is given in 3.4.

<note>

*Example and counterexample. I eat every few hours; this is a tendency, can be counted, and can be found in most people. "Because I have a desire to eat" is an assertion about the mind that no one can confirm. Counterexample: seeing someone who has not eaten for a day, we cannot infer that he does not need to eat; he may simply have no food (the threshold is not met).*

</note>

Conclusions fall into three classes: the constraint level (relying only on the four laws, the [Unconfirmability of Drives](../paper_ae79e938_76e1_4bf9_b2ab_995c03e16c9e/concepts.md#c03), and the scope settings), the conditional level (which also uses empirical [antecedents](concepts.md#c08), stated explicitly in the text), and research questions. Cases serve only for calibration and are not sources of derivation. Persons A through J are all synthetic [individuals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20).

<note>

*Conclusions are divided into "always holds", "holds under some extra condition", and "not yet known", each marked as such. Cases are only used to compare against intuition and cannot serve as evidence. The ten people from A to J were made up by me.*

</note>

## 1. The Laws and Premises Used in This Paper

**Inheritance.** The [Law of Flux](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c24), the [Law of the Black Box](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c25), the [Law of Attrition](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c26), the [Law of Non-Restoration](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c28), and the [Unconfirmability of Drives](../paper_ae79e938_76e1_4bf9_b2ab_995c03e16c9e/concepts.md#c03) are carried over from upstream and not proved again. The direct use of the [Law of Non-Restoration](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c28) in this paper is this: an [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) that has occurred enters the [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13), only increasing and never decreasing. The [Forced Wager](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c11) (every [decision](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c06) cannot be calculated beforehand, cannot be taken back afterwards, and cannot be avoided), the [Wall of the Undetermined](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c10) (statistics give only an estimate of [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) and cannot give prohibitions; a prohibition obtained from statistics is only an [empirical prohibition](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c51) and must be marked as such), the [Wall of Provenance](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c08) (a person cannot hold the whole of his own past [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13)), and the distinction between [exit](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c43) and [death](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c22) are likewise carried over and not proved again.

<note>

*What upstream has already proved is used directly here and is not argued again. The key points: when making decisions one cannot see the future clearly or the past completely; there is no going back to redo; and "no longer being a vortex" and "being said by someone to be dead" are not the same thing.*

</note>

**Premises (scope settings and empirical [antecedents](concepts.md#c08)).**

- **Premise 1 ([Cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)).** The researcher specifies the [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) boundary, the time window, the round length, and the dimensions of the [records](concepts.md#c06), and reports who made this division.
- **Premise 2 (a [state](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c02) and a [resource](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03) are the researcher's classification).** When a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) places an item of [record](concepts.md#c06) on the [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20), it is recorded as a [state](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c02); when a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) places it on the outside world, it is recorded as a [resource](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03). This is the researcher's [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) and has no absolute definition. Physical strength may be recorded in either column; different researchers may obtain different classifications, and they do not contradict one another.
- **Premise 3 ([Threshold](concepts.md#c03)).** The set of [antecedents](concepts.md#c08) required for an [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) [path](concepts.md#c07) to hold is called the [threshold](concepts.md#c03) of that [path](concepts.md#c07), and each item in it is called an [antecedent](concepts.md#c08); a [path](concepts.md#c07) is a class of [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) sequences drawn by the researcher. An [antecedent](concepts.md#c08) may be bodily quality, knowledge, time, position, external conditions, tools, and [resources](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03); which column it is recorded under is stated by the researcher and is not classified in advance.
- **Premise 4 (only three things are observed).** All that the researcher can [record](concepts.md#c06) is: the [threshold](concepts.md#c03) of a [path](concepts.md#c07), the [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) that actually occurred within the time window, and the [state variables](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c02) and [resource variables](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03) at the start and end of the time window. The difference between start and end is only the difference between two [records](concepts.md#c06), and is not called cost or loss.
- **Premise 5 (empirical [antecedent](concepts.md#c08)).** Such an [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) cannot be in two places at once in the same round, and its strength and attention are limited in total, so some [paths](concepts.md#c07) exclude one another. This is not derived from the four laws.

<note>

*These five are the foundation of this paper. First, the researcher must state how the cut is made. Second, "state" and "resource" are only columns used when keeping the books, and strength may be put in either. Third, the conditions needed to do something form a set, and this set is the "threshold". Fourth, all we can see is the threshold, the start, and the end; we cannot see "how much was paid". Fifth, a person can do only one main thing at a time; this is empirical and is not derived. That what has been done cannot be taken back is already accounted for in the inheritance above.*

</note>

**Rules derived in this paper.**

1. **[Path](concepts.md#c07) exclusion.** If any [antecedent](concepts.md#c08) in the [threshold](concepts.md#c03) is not met, the [path](concepts.md#c07) is infeasible in **that round**, which does not imply it is infeasible afterwards; if all are met, it only means the [path](concepts.md#c07) is feasible, not that it will occur. [Antecedents](concepts.md#c08) may change over time ([Law of Flux](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c24)): the [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) only grows forward, the [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) that [arrives at](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) the [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) differs at every moment, and the [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20)'s own [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) may also change the outside world, so the [antecedents](concepts.md#c08) must be checked again in every round; being met in this round does not imply being met in the next. (Constraint level)
2. **[Tendency](concepts.md#c02) is separate from events.** A high [tendency](concepts.md#c02) does not guarantee that the next occurrence will happen; at a [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) when not [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12), one occurrence cannot reconstruct the internal cause. (Constraint level)
3. **A self-report is an [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28).** "I want to eat" is one [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28), lossy, and cannot be taken as a complete reading of the inside; it has the same standing as other outputs. (Constraint level)
4. **Evidential status of [antecedents](concepts.md#c08).** Bodily and external conditions involve physical changes that can be directly [recorded](concepts.md#c06), and can be checked directly; knowledge is not defined separately, and means the [antecedents](concepts.md#c08) in the [threshold](concepts.md#c03) of a [path](concepts.md#c07) that cannot be checked directly, varying with the [path](concepts.md#c07) (having a cook's knowledge does not imply having medical knowledge), and can only be inferred from the [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) on that [path](concepts.md#c07), and must be marked "inferred from [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)" or "unknown", and is not assumed to be met. (Bookkeeping convention)
5. **An [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) changes what follows.** If an [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) [arrives at](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) the outside world, if the outside world responds, the response enters the [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13); an output that does not [arrive at](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) a given [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) is not in the [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) that arrives at that [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20). [Antecedents](concepts.md#c08) may vary with the [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) (Rule 1); if the outside world's response [arrives at](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) the [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) and has entered his [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13), the next round's [threshold](concepts.md#c03) (the [feasible set](concepts.md#c11)) may change accordingly; the [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) has gained one more entry, and the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) (the relative heaviness within the [feasible set](concepts.md#c11)) may also change accordingly; this may change the [records](concepts.md#c06) of later [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28), but within finite [records](concepts.md#c06) it is not guaranteed to be visible, and the value of the weight is not read off from changes in the [records](concepts.md#c06); so the [feasible set](concepts.md#c11) and the [tendency](concepts.md#c02) may vary from round to round and are not guaranteed to be stationary, nor to be [learning](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c04) or improvement. The two feedbacks must be written separately. An [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) can create the [antecedents](concepts.md#c08) of a later round (borrowing a book gives knowledge); the [antecedent](concepts.md#c08) of one [path](concepts.md#c07) can be the [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of another [path](concepts.md#c07), so feasibility has an order; some [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) may close certain [paths](concepts.md#c07); whether permanently or temporarily depends on the specific circumstances and the data, and is not inferred from the [Law of Non-Restoration](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c28): that what has happened cannot be taken back does not mean the [path](concepts.md#c07) is infeasible from then on, which also differs from being temporarily infeasible in this round. (That the [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) only grows and the outside response enters it is constraint level, from the [Law of Flux](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c24) and the [Law of Non-Restoration](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c28); [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) creating [antecedents](concepts.md#c08) and possibly closing [paths](concepts.md#c07) are conditional level, depending on experience and the specific data)
6. **Feasibility of each single [path](concepts.md#c07) does not imply that they can be carried out at the same time.** When several [paths](concepts.md#c07) are considered together, a physical exclusion check must be added. (Conditional level, using Premise 5)
7. **[Same Outcome from Different Paths](concepts.md#c04) is only an external classification.** Different [individuals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) follow different [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) histories and fall into the same class under the outcome variable, time window, and tolerance interval specified by the researcher; this "same class" is the researcher's classification; it is not what upstream calls [convergence](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c37) ([convergence](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c37) means the distance between [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) patterns shrinking on a specified dimension), and from the sameness of outcomes one cannot infer sameness of [path](concepts.md#c07), sameness of internal problem, or a common driving force, nor does it supply any frequency of occurrence. (Constraint level)
8. **At the [moments](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) when a [Wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) is not [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12), the [decision](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c06) behind every [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) is a [Forced Wager](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c11); when [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12), the result is already uniquely determined by constraints and is not covered here.** It cannot be calculated beforehand and cannot be taken back afterwards, and not producing an [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) is also one of the results that fall out in this round. So when not [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12), when an onlooker classes a single [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) as "safe" or "dangerous", this is the onlooker's own [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18), not a reading of the risk the party faced: how high the risk is is a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) made by the one looking, and the onlooker gets from the party only the party's [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) (including his self-report), cannot read how he sees the future, and has only their own judgment; whether a result is good or bad is a classification under some criterion, and it must be stated whose criterion it is, and a single result alone cannot decide whether the [decision](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c06) was good or bad; statistics can describe the [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) that have occurred; when used to speak of [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06), they give only an estimate, and statistics alone cannot give a prohibition; a "did not occur" seen in statistics is only an observational description, and only after it is calibrated across cases into a necessary condition is it called an [empirical prohibition](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c51), which must be marked as such and can be revised by new cases. This paper does not set up risk as a quantity of its own. (Constraint level, carried over from upstream)
9. **The [questions](concepts.md#c01) do not imply one another.** That I want does not imply that I can; that I can does not imply that I will do it; that I did it does not imply that the result is what I wanted. (Constraint level, the [Law of the Black Box](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c25) and the [Law of Non-Restoration](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c28); Premise 3, Rule 2)

<note>

*Among the nine rules, the first six say that "can it be done" and "is it wanted" must not be mixed; the seventh says that "the same result does not mean the same way of doing it"; the eighth says that "one cannot say after the fact whether this decision was safe"; the ninth puts the three questions together: want, can, and result cannot be inferred from one another. "Constraint level" and "conditional level" in parentheses say what each rule rests on.*

</note>

**Methodological prohibitions.** Do not write "in order to"; do not infer an internal driving force from a stable [tendency](concepts.md#c02); do not use goals, and judge good or bad only by the criteria the researcher specifies, stating who set them; do not treat externally displayed statistics as a reading of [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06); do not treat survival as optimal; do not presuppose, for any condition, that it is a [state](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c02) or a [resource](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03); do not infer from differences in end points a difference in choices at the start; do not infer the type of [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) from an increase or decrease in [resources](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03).

<note>

*This is a checklist for guarding against errors while writing: once the text says "he did it in order to ...", it is pretending to read his mind and must be changed.*

</note>

## 2. Records and Output Tendency

### 2.1 Records

[Records](concepts.md#c06) are the researcher's registrations, under the specified [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23), of the [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) that actually occurred within the time window, and also of the [state variables](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c02) and [resource variables](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03) at the start and end of the window (the [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) within the window and the start and end states and resources of Premise 4): what happened, when, where, how many times, and at what intervals. Only what has happened is registered; the registration is the researcher's selection, and [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) that are not registered remain in the [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) and may continue to affect the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06). A self-report is registered as one [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28), with the same standing as the other [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28).

<note>

*A record is a running account: when, where, and what was done. The account is kept by the researcher, and what is not entered did not necessarily fail to happen. What he said is also entered as one line, but it has the same standing as what he did, and is not compared for being more "true".*

</note>

### 2.2 Tendency

An [output tendency](concepts.md#c02) is a generalization of the **recurring shape** in the [records](concepts.md#c06), and must be stated together with four items: the [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23), the time window, the classification rule, and the [threshold](concepts.md#c03) conditions.

<note>

*A tendency is not an "inner" trait of a person; it is only a regularity the researcher sees in the running account, and the conditions under which the regularity was seen must be written out clearly.*

</note>

- It can be compared across [individuals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) and across time windows. "Most humans eat at intervals" is one example of such an empirical description; whether it holds depends on the [records](concepts.md#c06) under the specified time window and classification.
- Its relation to [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) can only be an **estimate**: seeing eating recur, one may estimate that the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) of this kind of [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) is heavier under these conditions, but cannot read off the value of the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06).
- It must be stated together with the [threshold](concepts.md#c03) conditions. If a necessary [antecedent](concepts.md#c08) of an eating [path](concepts.md#c07) (for example, food) is not met and one does not eat, the [threshold](concepts.md#c03) is not met, and this cannot be taken to mean the [tendency](concepts.md#c02) is low; whether food is a necessary [antecedent](concepts.md#c08) is stated by the researcher for that [path](concepts.md#c07).
- **Categories are the researcher's [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18).** The categories a [tendency](concepts.md#c02) uses, including words that seem natural such as "eating", are all the researcher's classification, and are not boundaries carried by the behavior itself. Grouping the behavior of different [individuals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) and different populations into one class is something the researcher does; "the same [tendency](concepts.md#c02)" is said under the categories the researcher has set, and does not imply that they are doing the same thing or have the same thoughts about it. If the observed [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20)'s own words are adopted, it must be stated whose classification is adopted. If the classification rule is changed, a common shape may appear or disappear.
- **The same [tendency](concepts.md#c02) is not [convergence](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c37).** [Convergence](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c37) requires a separately specified dimension and distance before one can say that the distance shrinks or does not shrink.

<note>

*"Eating" differs among peoples in what is eaten, how, and where, and in what people think about it. We merely call all these behaviors "eating". So saying "people on both sides love eating" only means "the researcher grouped these behaviors into one class", not that they are doing the same thing.*

</note>

### 2.3 Three Things That Cannot Be Inferred in Reverse

| Cannot infer | Reason |
|---|---|
| A high [tendency](concepts.md#c02) → the next occurrence surely happens | [Law of the Black Box](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c25): when not [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12), only the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) can be inferred |
| It occurred → reconstruct the internal cause | If the content of this [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) is a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18), it is only the landing point of one [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) |
| A stable [tendency](concepts.md#c02) → an internal driving force exists | [Unconfirmability of Drives](../paper_ae79e938_76e1_4bf9_b2ab_995c03e16c9e/concepts.md#c03); a [tendency](concepts.md#c02) is the shape of the [records](concepts.md#c06) |

<note>

*Examples and counterexamples. Counterexample 1: not having eaten for three days does not imply he no longer wants to eat; there may be no food. Counterexample 2: having eaten a lot does not imply that he "wanted it badly". Counterexample 3: statistics saying humans love eating cannot be used to demand that a particular person must eat.*

</note>

### 2.4 Variation and Limits

The same [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) may act differently on the same [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) today and tomorrow ([Law of Flux](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c24)). After the [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) is updated the [tendency](concepts.md#c02) may change, but the change is not guaranteed to be [learning](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c04), still less an improvement. If the researcher's observation [arrives at](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) the observed [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20), it enters his [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13); observation is not transparent.

<note>

*The same sentence said today and said tomorrow does not affect the same person in the same way. A person who has changed has not necessarily improved or learned something. And once the researcher asks or looks, that act itself enters the person's history.*

</note>

### 2.5 Decision and Risk

Traditional theory uses risk to distinguish "near-certain success" from "nearly certain death". This distinction requires knowing the distribution of results beforehand, but according to the [Forced Wager](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c11) (a [Wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) at a [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) when it is not [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12)), the future at the moment of acting cannot be seen, the [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) itself cannot calculate it accurately, and the researcher can even less read how he sees that future. "How high the risk is" is only a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) made by whoever is looking at the situation: what an onlooker gets is their own judgment, not the risk the party faced; the party's own account afterwards is also just one [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28). Afterwards the person has no complete vantage point from which to evaluate the [decision](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c06): by the [Wall of Provenance](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c08), although that stretch of [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) is still there, it cannot be held in full, and any after-the-fact reproduction or evaluation made by the researcher or the party is a new [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) made now, which is a new [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28). So an after-the-fact evaluation is a new view, not a reading from that time. So this paper does not set up risk as a separate quantity:

<note>

*This paragraph first explains why risk is not discussed: risk requires knowing beforehand how results will be distributed, which we cannot do, so the table below lists only what cannot be said.*

</note>

| Cannot infer | Reason |
|---|---|
| A single [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) → this [decision](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c06) was safe or dangerous | "How high the risk is" is only a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) made by whoever is looking at the situation. An onlooker cannot read how the party saw the future at that moment, and gets only their own judgment; the party's later account is also just one [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28). If an onlooker makes an estimate, it must state whose estimate it is |
| A good or bad result afterwards → that [decision](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c06) was right or wrong at the time | Whether a result is good or bad is a classification under some criterion, and it must be stated whose criterion it is (the party's, an onlooker's, or one specified by the researcher; the three can disagree); for example, the same exam result is good news to someone who wants to change careers and bad news to someone who wants to continue studying. Even when the criterion is stated, a single result is only one landing and cannot by itself settle the quality of the [decision](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c06) |
| He chose a [path](concepts.md#c07) → he assessed the risk, or prefers risk-taking or is risk-averse | Choosing a [path](concepts.md#c07) does not reveal how he sees the future: the onlooker gets from him only his [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28), and a self-report is also just an [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) ([Unconfirmability of Drives](../paper_ae79e938_76e1_4bf9_b2ab_995c03e16c9e/concepts.md#c03)); he may also have had no other feasible [path](concepts.md#c07) ([threshold](concepts.md#c03)); on the same axis as the two rows above: an onlooker gets only their own judgment, and does not judge the risk for him |
| Other people's result [records](concepts.md#c06) on similar [paths](concepts.md#c07) → the risk this person faced at the time | Each person's [own segment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c17) may differ; those records cannot be taken as a reading of this person's risk, but if someone uses them to make an estimate, it must state whose estimate it is, the sample it rests on, and that it is not guaranteed |

<note>

*This table lists common mistaken inferences: a single result, good or bad afterwards, or other people's experience, cannot be used directly to speak of this person's risk at the time. The point is "who says so": standing beside him, what you know is how risky you think it is, not the risk he faced; and whether a result is good or bad must first be asked by whose standard.*

</note>

The everyday "high risk" can only serve as the researcher's after-the-fact description: under the specified classification and sample, the result [records](concepts.md#c06) of similar [paths](concepts.md#c07) are scattered. This description is not attributed to any particular [decision](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c06).

<note>

*You made a decision; at that moment no one knew whether it would succeed or fail, and neither did you. Looking at the result afterwards, one also cannot say whether the decision was good or bad: that depends first on whose criterion, and an after-the-fact evaluation is a new view, not a reading from that time. Here we do not discuss "how high the risk is", because that would require knowing the result in advance. Example and counterexample: in Chapter 3, B's stall sold nothing in the first week; one cannot say he judged wrongly, nor that running a stall is inherently dangerous; someone else may have sold very well the same week. Counterexample: A is employed and receives a weekly wage; one cannot say his original judgment was right; he could not calculate it then, and now the result has simply fallen out.*

</note>

## 3. Example: Ten People, One Month, Four Rounds

**[Cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23).** Ten people (A through J), a time window of one month, divided into four rounds of about one week each. The outcome classification "end-point cash [record](concepts.md#c06) is ten thousand more than at the start" is specified by the researcher and is only the researcher's [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18); it does not mean that anyone wants to earn. The five [paths](concepts.md#c07), the [antecedents](concepts.md#c08) of each [path](concepts.md#c07), the round-by-round [records](concepts.md#c06) of A, B, C and F (the [Four-Round Record Table](concepts.md#c10)), and the persons are all synthetic settings (including that the cash increases of A and F are equal and that the start and end [records](concepts.md#c06) record only total cash), not measurements. In each round we go through one cycle: [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28), [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at the outside world, the outside world's response, entry into history, and a recheck of the [threshold](concepts.md#c03).

<note>

*This chapter uses ten made-up people to see how "earning ten thousand" can be stated clearly through thresholds, records, and feedback. "Earning ten thousand" is a classification we set ourselves; it does not mean these people want to earn.*

</note>

### 3.1 The Two Feedbacks Written Separately

| Feedback | What it changes | How to write it |
|---|---|---|
| To the [threshold](concepts.md#c03) | The [feasible set](concepts.md#c11): whether it is physically possible; feedback may change it | Recheck the [antecedents](concepts.md#c08) each round, and record "infeasible in that round" |
| To the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) | The relative heaviness within the [feasible set](concepts.md#c11), which may change the [output tendency](concepts.md#c02) | Not guaranteed to be visible in finite [records](concepts.md#c06); write only the change in the [records](concepts.md#c06); do not read off a [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) value; it is not guaranteed to be [learning](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c04) or improvement |

Mixing them together confuses "unable to do" with "not inclined to do".

<note>

*After finishing something there are two kinds of consequence: one is that "what can and cannot be done next" has changed (threshold), the other is that "what is more wanted next" has changed (weight). The former can be seen and the latter cannot, so they must be recorded separately.*

</note>

### 3.2 The Thresholds of the Five Paths

| [Path](concepts.md#c07) | [Threshold](concepts.md#c03) (list of [antecedents](concepts.md#c08)) | Evidential status |
|---|---|---|
| Employment | An employer has appeared; a matching skill; able to [arrive at](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) the workplace at the specified time | The skill is marked "inferred from [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)" or "unknown"; the rest can be checked directly |
| Running a stall | Capital or goods; a site; time; knowing how to sell | Knowledge is marked "inferred from [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)" |
| Growing and selling crops | A field; seeds; farming knowledge; a crop that ripens within the time window, or something already in hand that can be harvested | See the [Four-Round Record Table](concepts.md#c10), C |
| Selling existing goods | Something to sell; a buyer has appeared | Can be checked directly |
| Robbery | A means of exerting force (a knife or sufficient strength); a target and a position that can be [arrived at](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10); overlapping time periods of both sides; knowledge of how to carry it out | Knowledge is marked "unknown"; the rest can be checked directly |

The above is called the [Five-Path Threshold Table](concepts.md#c12).

Whether an [antecedent](concepts.md#c08) is classed as a [state](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c02) or a [resource](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03) is never decided in advance; it is stated by the researcher when the books are kept. "Robbery" is the researcher's name for a sequence of [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28); the [records](concepts.md#c06) should be written as observable [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) (approaching with a knife, making a demand, taking cash), with a separate note that the researcher classed it as robbery and who made that classification. Whether each [path](concepts.md#c07)'s [antecedents](concepts.md#c08) are met is judged by the researcher from the specified data, with the basis stated; where the basis is insufficient, mark it "unknown".

<note>

*There are many roads to earning money, and each road works only after some conditions are gathered; that is the threshold. For example, robbery needs at least a means, a target, and time. Note that the word "robbery" is a label the researcher attaches; when keeping the books, first write the actions that can be seen, then note "this is what we classed as robbery". Whether he "has the skill" or "it can work" is likewise a judgment the researcher makes from the data, not the fact itself.*

</note>

### 3.3 Records of the Four Rounds (A, B, C, and F as Examples)

| Person | Round 1 | Change in the outside world caused by the [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) | Change in the [threshold](concepts.md#c03) from round 2 on |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Lives near the workplace, has the skill; employment is feasible; no field, so planting is infeasible; the [antecedents](concepts.md#c08) of robbery are not seen in the data and are marked "unknown" | Takes employment, [arrives at](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) the employer and the workplace; receives a weekly wage | Cash increases, and "has capital or goods" becomes met; employment is still feasible |
| B | Has a small amount of capital and a site; running a stall is feasible; skill unknown | Runs a stall, [arrives at](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) customers; sells nothing in the first week | Capital is used up and there are no goods, so "has capital or goods" is no longer met; running a stall is infeasible in round 2; switches to selling existing goods (its [antecedents](concepts.md#c08), "has something to sell" and "has a buyer", are not seen in the data and are marked "unknown"; he actually sold, so the [path](concepts.md#c07) was feasible in that round) |
| C | Has a field, seeds, and knowledge; the ripening period exceeds the time window, and planting that does not include sowing in a single round is infeasible | Sows (this is one [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)) | Crop growth is the outside world's response, and the crop is not ripe before round 4; the [antecedent](concepts.md#c08) of harvesting within the time window is still not met |
| F | Has a knife and sufficient strength; the [antecedents](concepts.md#c08) of robbery are all met except knowledge (unknown) | Approaches with a knife and takes cash (the researcher separately notes it as robbery) | Cash increases; the "position that can be [arrived at](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)" may change because of the other party's later [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28), and this paper records it only as a change in external conditions without asking the cause |

How to read it:

1. The [threshold](concepts.md#c03) of the same person differs in each round: because of round 1's [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) and response, B's [feasible set](concepts.md#c11) shrinks, while A's may expand.
2. C shows: planting is excluded by time in the case of "a single round without sowing", but sowing is itself an [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) and may change the later outside world; if the time window is lengthened, the exclusion list may be rewritten.
3. When the cash increases of F and A are equal, the total cash in the two [records](concepts.md#c06) at start and end alone cannot tell which [path](concepts.md#c07) each took; to distinguish them one can rely only on the [records](concepts.md#c06) of each round's [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28).

<note>

*Four people, four situations. A has a job and the skill, earns money, and even has spare capital to run a stall; B's stall failed, the capital is gone, and he has to sell things; C farms, but the crop is too slow and cannot be harvested within this month; F has a knife and strength and got the cash. The end point is either "more cash" or "not more", but the processes are entirely different, so one cannot look only at the end point.*

</note>

### 3.4 Several Paths Excluding One Another

By Premise 5, the total is limited, so some [paths](concepts.md#c07) exclude one another. This example specifies "full-time employment" and "running a stall at full effort" as each filling the whole week, so they are mutually exclusive in this example; if they were changed to part-time or partial time slots, whether they exclude each other would have to be checked [path](concepts.md#c07) by [path](concepts.md#c07) for overlap in time and position, and is not inferred directly from Premise 5. Nor can one strike at two places at once (this follows directly from not being in two places at the same time). In each round, after the single-[path](concepts.md#c07) checks, a physical exclusion check must be added. (Conditional level)

<note>

*A person can do only one main thing at a time, so in each round, besides checking one by one whether something can be done, one must check whether these things can be done at the same time.*

</note>

**[Opportunity cost](concepts.md#c05) is a comparison calculation by the researcher.** When a [path](concepts.md#c07) is chosen in a round, the other [paths](concepts.md#c07) that exclude it and whose [thresholds](concepts.md#c03) are met are thereby excluded, and this excluded set can be recorded. Under a valuation the researcher specifies (for example cash, time, survival probability), the researcher can compare the end point of the [path](concepts.md#c07) taken with the expected end points of the excluded [paths](concepts.md#c07), and this comparison is called [opportunity cost](concepts.md#c05). (Conditional level, using Premise 5)

- The valuation is chosen by the researcher; change the yardstick and the result differs, so it must be stated who chose it.
- An excluded [path](concepts.md#c07) did not occur, so its end point can only be an estimate, and the basis of the estimate (others' [records](concepts.md#c06), the same person's earlier and later rounds) must be written out; where there is no basis, mark it "unknown".
- Count only [paths](concepts.md#c07) whose [threshold](concepts.md#c03) is met and which were excluded. [Paths](concepts.md#c07) whose [threshold](concepts.md#c03) is not met are infeasible, not given up, and must not be merged in.
- It is not a quantity of the [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20), does not enter the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06), and is not used to explain motive: the valuation is the researcher's [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18), the excluded [paths](concepts.md#c07) did not occur and their end points can only be estimated by the researcher, and the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) cannot be read ([Law of the Black Box](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c25)), so this comparison cannot be a reading of the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06). One may say "under this valuation, A took employment, and the excluded stall is expected to be about some number", but may not say "A chose employment because his [opportunity cost](concepts.md#c05) was small".

<note>

*A person cannot work full time and also run a stall at full effort in the same week, so choosing one means giving up the other. What was given up can be compared and calculated, but that is the researcher estimating with the researcher's own yardstick; the road that was given up was never walked, and can only be estimated. One cannot therefore say that this person "calculated and so chose this one"; that would be reading his mind.*

</note>

### 3.5 End-Point Records and Interpretation

- The end point is only one [record](concepts.md#c06) after the last round, and its difference from the start is not called cost.
- A difference in end points cannot be read directly as a difference in choices: of two people with the same start, if early [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) receive different responses, the later [feasible sets](concepts.md#c11) may diverge ([Law of Non-Restoration](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c28)).
- Not earning does not imply not trying; trying is an [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) and can be recorded. Not trying is also one of the results that fall out in this round, and is not a blank (Rule 8).
- **[Same outcome from different paths](concepts.md#c04).** Suppose A took employment and B sold existing goods and both ended with ten thousand more in cash; if classed only by "end-point cash is ten thousand more", the two are the same class; by entry time, source, and the objects [arrived at](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10), they differ. The two need not regard themselves as doing the same thing (Rule 7).

<note>

*The same result does not mean the same way of doing it; a different result does not mean the choices differed at the start, because in between it may only be luck that differed. Not having earned may also mean having tried.*

</note>

### 3.6 Tendency and Threshold

Suppose the [records](concepts.md#c06) show that four of the ten people had employment [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) in every round; this is a [tendency](concepts.md#c02), with conditions attached: a one-month time window, and an employer having appeared. For the other six, one must check one by one in which round and which [antecedent](concepts.md#c08) was not met, or whether the [antecedents](concepts.md#c08) were met and the [path](concepts.md#c07) was feasible but did not occur; where this cannot be found out, mark it "unknown" and do not supply imagination. Three people have robbery [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28); this cannot be read as a high [tendency](concepts.md#c02), and one must first look at whether the other seven did not meet the [antecedents](concepts.md#c08) (for example, A has no means), or met them without it occurring.

<note>

*Of ten people, four always had work and the other six did not; one cannot directly say that "these six love work less", but must first check whether they had no employer or no skill, or had everything and simply did not take this road that round. By the same reasoning, three people robbed, and one cannot say they are "worse"; one must first see whether the other seven had no knife, or had a knife and did not rob.*

</note>

### 3.7 The Boundary of This Paper

If the outside world is a [natural environment](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c16) (weather, crop growth), its response belongs to this paper. If the outside world includes other people, their responses (handing over cash, resisting, retaliating) are recorded as the [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of others that [arrive at](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) this [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) only if they actually [arrive at](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) this [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25); whether an employer is there is an external state, not an [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28). This paper records both only as "changes in external conditions" without analyzing causes. The compatibility and [mismatch](../paper_ae79e938_76e1_4bf9_b2ab_995c03e16c9e/concepts.md#c08) between others' [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28), the separation of violence from mutual benefit, and the effect on the survival of the one robbed are not handled in this paper.

<note>

*Rain and crop growth are the responses of the natural environment and count within this paper. But how other people react (whether the one robbed hands over money or resists), this paper records only as "the conditions changed", without asking why, and still less analyzing the relationship between the two people; that is another topic.*

</note>

### 3.8 What This Example Does Not Say, and Limits of Use

Who is diligent, who is rational, what anyone acts for, which path pays, and who should choose how. That someone holds a knife and does not rob does not imply that he exercises restraint; that someone robbed does not imply that he lacks money. All the researcher has is each round's [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28), [thresholds](concepts.md#c03), start, and end.

<note>

*This example cannot be used to evaluate people; it can only be used to see how records are written.*

</note>

"One more ten thousand in a month" is a specified classification. If it is changed to one year, or to what is needed to maintain survival, the excluded lists and each round's feedback may differ. Each time it is used, the time window, round length, and classification must be stated.

<note>

*This example cannot answer: who is more diligent, who is smarter, which path pays most. It only demonstrates how to keep the books. Change the length of time, or change the definition of "success", and the result changes with it.*

</note>

## 4. Survival and Reproduction

Both are factual checks made after the researcher specifies a criterion; they are not consequences of the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06), nor are they the [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20)'s purpose. This paper does not know, and does not need to know, what the [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20)'s bodily condition is.

<note>

*"Staying alive" and "having descendants" are both cases where the researcher first sets a standard and then looks at whether it happened. They are not what this person wants, and they are not his purpose.*

</note>

### 4.0 The Individual Local Solution

The next-moment [state](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c02) and [resources](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03) of the specified [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) in its [derivation round](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c07), together with the relevant [records](concepts.md#c06) of [criterion exit](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09), if the [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) is listed as a [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) under the specified [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23) and these fields are recorded in that round's [solution](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c05), form the single-[node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) part of the upstream [solution](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c05). The above is called the [individual local solution](concepts.md#c09). This paper records only these fields. If the researcher wants to look at input (what was paid), a separate column must be set up and it must be stated who made the division; it must not be pretended that it has been obtained automatically from the [solution](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c05) (cost is not a quantity; see Section 0). Whether and when [criterion exit](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09) occurs is one dimension of the [solution](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c05); survival (4.1) is reading this dimension, not a separate set of data. The upstream [solution](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c05) does not presuppose reproduction as a dimension; reproduction is a determination made in this paper under a separately specified event criterion (4.2).

<note>

*At the end of each round, the researcher writes up the person's bodily condition at the next moment, the resources in hand, and whether the "exit" standard is met, as one record. As for "how much was paid", a separate column must be opened, and it must be noted who determined it.*

</note>

### 4.1 Survival

- **Criterion.** The researcher specifies what counts as [criterion exit](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09) (for example, a physiological threshold) and states who set it. A criterion is a definition.
- **Fact.** Whether [criterion exit](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09) occurs in a given round is decided by actual events. A fact becomes a [record](concepts.md#c06) only when the researcher registers it.
- **Record only presence or absence and the time point; infer no cause.** Whether he depends on equipment, is cared for by others, or what his body is like, if registered, appears only in the [threshold](concepts.md#c03) and in the [records](concepts.md#c06) of each round, what is not registered is marked unknown, and none of this changes the way of determination.
- **The three layers do not imply one another.** Upstream [exit](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c43) is a structural matter (no longer being a [Wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16)) and does not need anyone to determine it; [death](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c22) is one [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) in which someone marks it as "already [exited](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c43)"; [criterion exit](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09) is a factual check after the researcher specifies a criterion. The three can fail to line up: already [exited](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c43) with no one marking [death](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c22); [death](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c22) marked while still a [Wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16); meeting the criterion while structurally not having [exited](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c43). Structurally, after [exit](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c43) a new [entry](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c44) is possible; but whether [criterion exit](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09) is reversible depends on the criterion set (for example, a criterion of quitting a job may be reversible, and a criterion of a physiological boundary may be irreversible), so it records only presence or absence and the time point, as a bookkeeping convention, and is not treated in advance as a permanent end point.
- **Not a general ledger.** These [records](concepts.md#c06) are only the researcher's selection, under the specified [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23), of what has happened; a [record](concepts.md#c06) is not a complete settlement of the one recorded, and if the party makes a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) of himself, that [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) is also incomplete, so they are not a settlement of a whole life; a settlement requires a further [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) that takes a whole stretch as some result, and this [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) itself then enters the [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) and is not in its own material. The [records](concepts.md#c06) of survival and reproduction carry no criterion for evaluating a life of their own and do not evaluate a life; to evaluate or compare different [individuals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20), one must state whose criterion is used, and the [records](concepts.md#c06) do not supply it.

<note>

*Survival asks only one thing: by the standard the researcher set, did an exit occur in this round. Who the person is, why, and what held him up are not what this cell answers. "Structurally no longer a vortex", "said by someone to be dead", and "meeting the researcher's standard" are three different things, and they can fail to line up. And no one can write a summary of a person's whole life, because the summary is itself a new view. A record is the researcher's one selection, narrow and incomplete, and cannot serve as a settlement; to evaluate or compare, one must first say by whose standard.*

</note>

### 4.2 Reproduction

- **Recorded as an [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28).** Under the [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23) of this paper (Premise 1), a reproduction event is recorded as an [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of that [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20). This is a bookkeeping convention, not an ontological position; if the [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23) is changed to one that takes the two spouses as the unit, the same event can be recorded as the joint [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) of the [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of two [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25), and the two ways of recording do not contradict each other.
- **Accumulation is irreversible.** Reproduction events are a sequence of events that have occurred in the [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20)'s [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13), only increasing and never decreasing. An eighty-year-old who has no reproduction this year does not lose the children already had, nor are they cancelled by his [criterion exit](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09). "Yes or no" in a given round is only a slice of the sequence. (Constraint level)
- **[Record](concepts.md#c06) fields.** Time (including age at the time), geography (the position of the event, the positions of both parties), the other party (who, whether living together at the time; who made the attribution of "spouse" must be stated), the result (whether a child was born, and whether it later [arrived at](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) the person concerned), the [threshold](concepts.md#c03) of the round, the start, the end.
- **Actual reproduction is not replaced by other [records](concepts.md#c06).** A high [tendency](concepts.md#c02) does not imply that the next occurrence will happen (Rule 2), a self-report is one [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) (Rule 3), and an attempt is also an [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28), each being a separate [record](concepts.md#c06); the reproduction event itself is recorded as an [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28), so whether actual reproduction occurred depends only on whether this event occurred under the specified time window and event criterion. (Constraint level)
- **Boundary.** This paper records only the side of the [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20). The other party's [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13), how the pairing was formed, and the survival and further reproduction of the offspring are not handled in this paper. Offspring are [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) at their [entry](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c44), whose prehistory overlaps with that of the parents, and it does not follow that they inherit the parents' [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) or [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28). In a closed population (no outside supplementation, continuing across generations by internal reproduction), for the determination of the three results of member survival, actual reproduction, and offspring continuation, the [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) supplies only the [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20)-side [records](concepts.md#c06) of the first two; whether the three together hold, and what counts as sufficient, is not handled in this paper.

<note>

*Having a child is something that cannot be taken back once done. An eighty-year-old who has no child this year does not thereby have no children. The record must be detailed: when, where, with whom, and with what result. Wanting to, saying one will, and having tried are all not the same as actually having had one. As for the other party and the child, only the person's own side is recorded here.*

</note>

### 4.3 The Non-Implication List

| Cannot infer | Reason |
|---|---|
| A stable [tendency](concepts.md#c02) to eat → not eating necessarily leads to [criterion exit](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09) (when the criterion is the physiological boundary) | A [tendency](concepts.md#c02) is the shape of the [records](concepts.md#c06), not a necessary condition |
| Survival without eating → the generalization of that [tendency](concepts.md#c02) does not hold | Other [paths](concepts.md#c07) or external conditions may exist; a single case is not enough to overturn a generalization, one must look at the overall [records](concepts.md#c06) under comparable conditions |
| Survival → his [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) were better or optimal | Survival is only a fact under a criterion, and does not compare [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) |
| [Criterion exit](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09) → he chose wrongly, or lacked something | [Criterion exit](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09) has no necessary relation to what he wants; its relation to [resources](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03) depends on the criterion set (for example, if the criterion is set as bankruptcy, the relation is established by the definition of the criterion) |
| Reproduction occurred → he wanted to | [Unconfirmability of Drives](../paper_ae79e938_76e1_4bf9_b2ab_995c03e16c9e/concepts.md#c03) |
| No reproduction → he did not want to, or lacked something | Same as above; the [threshold](concepts.md#c03) may also not have been met |
| Survival in one round → survival in later rounds; reproduction in one round → reproduction in the next round | [Law of the Black Box](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c25); reproduction events that have already occurred are not cancelled (see 4.2), and what is said here is whether it occurs again in the next round |
| Survival and reproduction imply each other | This paper does not infer one from the other: the two determinations each have their own criterion and [records](concepts.md#c06) |

<note>

*Example and counterexample. An elderly person of eighty has no reproduction event this year and keeps surviving; his children from thirty years ago are still in the history. Counterexample: one cannot say, because he has no reproduction this year, that he has no children; nor can one say, because he survives, that his outputs are better than those of someone who died young.*

</note>

### 4.4 Conditioning on Existing Samples

When the sample is conditioned on survival, the sample contains the [individuals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) that have not yet undergone [criterion exit](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09). Whether [criterion exit](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09) occurs depends only on the criterion set by the researcher and on actual events (4.1), and has no necessary relation to whether his [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) were good or bad (4.3), so the performance of survivors cannot be used to judge good or bad; selecting samples conditioned on "survival" is where the sample boundary comes from. Of the [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) left by those who underwent [criterion exit](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09), those that have already [arrived at](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) others or remain in a [signal container](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c26) are still in the [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13); those that arrived at no one may be unobtainable by the researcher; one cannot claim that those who have disappeared cannot be studied. This is the researcher's sample boundary; it is not that the outside world has a selecting purpose ("filtering" is only a metaphor).

<note>

*If we study only those who are alive now, we have in fact already excluded "those who did not make it". This is a bias in how we sample, not nature "picking people". What those who have left leave behind is still in the history, and can be studied just the same; but what never reached anyone may be unobtainable by the researcher, though one cannot say that those who left cannot be studied.*

</note>

## 5. Grading of Conclusions

**Constraint level** (relying only on the four laws, [Unconfirmability of Drives](../paper_ae79e938_76e1_4bf9_b2ab_995c03e16c9e/concepts.md#c03), Premises 1 to 4, and what is carried over from upstream)

1. If any [antecedent](concepts.md#c08) of a [threshold](concepts.md#c03) is not in place, that [path](concepts.md#c07) is infeasible in that round; being in place only means feasible.
2. [Tendency](concepts.md#c02) and events are separate; a self-report is a lossy [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28).
3. If an [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) [arrives at](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) the outside and it responds, the response enters the [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) and may change the next round's [threshold](concepts.md#c03) and [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06); the two feedbacks are separate.
4. Reproduction and [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) that have already occurred only increase and never decrease; differences at the end point must not be used to infer differences in the choices at the starting point.
5. Survival records only presence or absence and the time point; it infers neither cause nor optimality.
6. A change in [resources](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03) does not imply the type of [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28): the same change in [resources](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03) can be reached by different [paths](concepts.md#c07) ([same outcome from different paths](concepts.md#c04)), and the two [records](concepts.md#c06) at the start and the end do not show the [path](concepts.md#c07) (Premise 4); moreover, a change in [resources](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03) is an outside [record](concepts.md#c06), while the type of [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) is the researcher's classification, so the two are on different levels (Premise 2). The classification into [state](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c02) and [resources](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03) is a [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23).
7. [Same outcome from different paths](concepts.md#c04) is only an external classification; it does not imply the same [path](concepts.md#c07) or a common drive, and it is not [convergence](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c37).
8. Actual reproduction looks only at whether the event occurred; it is not replaced by [tendency](concepts.md#c02), a self-report, or an attempt.
9. At the [moments](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) when a [Wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) is not [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12), every [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) is a [forced wager](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c11): a single [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) is not classed by an onlooker as safe or risky (how high the risk is is a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) made by the one looking), whether the result is good or bad requires stating whose criterion, and the [decision](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c06) is not evaluated on it; risk is not set up as a quantity of its own.
10. The [questions](concepts.md#c01) do not imply one another: want, can, and outcome are not inferred from one another.

<note>

*These ten conclusions hold no matter what the data look like.*

</note>

**Condition level**

| Conclusion | [Antecedents](concepts.md#c08) used |
|---|---|
| That a single [path](concepts.md#c07) is feasible does not imply that [paths](concepts.md#c07) can be carried out at the same time; an exclusion check must be added every round | Premise 5 (physical limits) |
| The list of [paths](concepts.md#c07) excluded in a given time window | The time window, round length, and classification specified by the researcher; decided by the data |
| Whether a specific [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) has each [antecedent](concepts.md#c08) | Decided by the data; knowledge is inferred from [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) |
| The comparison calculation of [opportunity cost](concepts.md#c05) | Premise 5; the valuation and estimation method specified by the researcher |
| An [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) creating [antecedents](concepts.md#c08) of a later round, possibly closing some [paths](concepts.md#c07) | Experience and the specific data; permanent or temporary is decided by the data, not inferred from the [Law of Non-Restoration](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c28) |
| Other people's result [records](concepts.md#c06) as a conditional estimate of this person's risk | The sample and estimation method specified by the researcher; must state whose estimate it is; not guaranteed |

<note>

*These need extra conditions to hold, such as "a person can do only one thing at a time" or "the researcher chose a particular ruler".*

</note>

**The status of the cases.** The cases in Chapter 3 (synthetic) and Chapter 6 (Lincoln) serve only for calibration and are not listed as a source of derivation for any of the above levels.

<note>

*Both cases are only examples for comparison, not evidence.*

</note>

**Research questions**

1. Under comparable conditions, how stable is the common shape of [output tendency](concepts.md#c02) across [individuals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20), and how does it change across rounds? For the same body of behavior under different classification rules, which common shapes remain stable and which disappear with the classification?
2. How much of the difference between [thresholds](concepts.md#c03) and the difference between end points can be explained by [path](concepts.md#c07) exclusion?
3. In settings where physical conditions can be stated, where the person's self-reported judgments of cost or consequence can be observed, and where actual [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) and results are tracked, under what conditions do outward self-reports and later [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) agree or deviate? One must distinguish observation gaps, [candidate](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c18) [paths](concepts.md#c07) that cannot be obtained, differences in [resource](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03) conditions, and differences in [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) information; where no self-report is seen, mark it "unknown"; deviation is not attributed to "irrationality", and agreement is not taken as reading the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06).

<note>

*These three have no answers yet; they need data and further study. The third one is a particular reminder: that what a person says matches what he does does not mean we have understood his mind; that they do not match does not mean he is irrational.*

</note>

## 6. Case (Calibration): Lincoln, from New Salem to Ford's Theatre

This whole chapter serves only for calibration and is not a source of derivation. The data come from public web pages, not original documents; where sources attribute the same matter differently, this is marked as the source's attribution and is not read off as a [record](concepts.md#c06). Chapter 3 is a synthetic example and this chapter is a real person; neither constitutes empirical support.

<note>

*This chapter uses Lincoln's life for comparison with the concepts above. The data come from introductions publicly available online, not from original historical sources, so they serve only as reference, not as evidence.*

</note>

**What happened.** In 1831, the twenty-two-year-old Lincoln went down the Sangamon River on a flatboat; the boat stuck on the town's mill dam, and so he came to New Salem for the first time, having been in Illinois for little more than a year. 〔Offutt〕, who had hired him to carry goods, ran a store there, which failed in less than a year, and Lincoln stayed in the town.

<note>

*An accident left Lincoln in New Salem.*

</note>

In 1832 he ran for the state legislature for the first time and lost. That same year he went into partnership with 〔Berry〕 and took over a general store on credit; in 1833 he again bought on credit the stock and premises of a store across the way. The store finally failed, 〔Berry〕 died, and a [debt over 1000 dollars](../ext/ext_3f133ee5_6685_4846_a0c1_f73221282d81/concepts.md#c01) was left to Lincoln (another source writes a [debt of about 1100 dollars](../ext/ext_27762a9b_b116_4d9b_85f8_9d460f8f1a9d/concepts.md#c01), listed side by side). This was a [debt paid off in 1848](../ext/ext_dba4a173_9078_4414_9f8b_b990559379cd/concepts.md#c01), the year he was a member of the U.S. House of Representatives.

<note>

*His first defeat, a store, and debt, which dragged on until 1848 before it was paid off.*

</note>

While the debt was still on him, he was [appointed Postmaster on May 7 1833](../ext/ext_dba4a173_9078_4414_9f8b_b990559379cd/concepts.md#c02), and supplemented his income by splitting rails, harvesting, doing odd jobs, and minding a store; the post paid a [postmaster income of about 55 dollars a year](../ext/ext_27762a9b_b116_4d9b_85f8_9d460f8f1a9d/concepts.md#c02). At the end of the year he borrowed books from the county surveyor to teach himself, and became a deputy surveyor.

<note>

*With the debt still there, he held several scrappy jobs at once, and moved into surveying by borrowing books.*

</note>

In 1834 and 1836 he was elected to the Illinois legislature. During the campaign he met 〔Stuart〕, a fellow candidate; 〔Stuart〕 lent him law books, which he studied on his own, without entering a law school. On March 24, 1836, a court certified his good character, and on September 9 he obtained [a license to practice law on September 9 1836](../ext/ext_d14ee3a0_732d_43b1_a11e_ccef396f1a38/concepts.md#c01), valid in the courts of the whole state, (another source records it as [a license to practice law on September 19 1836](../ext/ext_661ea447_93a6_4239_a564_08d70d5c9da1/concepts.md#c01), listed side by side). In the spring of 1837 he moved to Springfield and became 〔Stuart〕's junior partner, and afterwards formed partnerships in turn with 〔Logan〕 and 〔Herndon〕.

<note>

*From legislator, borrowed books, and license, to moving to Springfield to be a lawyer.*

</note>

In 1846 he was elected to the U.S. House of Representatives, serving 1847 to 1849. In 1854 his run for the Senate failed; in 1858 the Republican Party nominated him to run against 〔Douglas〕, the two held [seven debates in 1858](../ext/ext_661ea447_93a6_4239_a564_08d70d5c9da1/concepts.md#c02), and he lost, but the debates brought national fame. In November 1860 he was elected president. In 1865 he was [shot on April 14 1865](../ext/ext_661ea447_93a6_4239_a564_08d70d5c9da1/concepts.md#c03) at Ford's Theatre by 〔Booth〕, and his [death on April 15 1865](../ext/ext_48a45b8a_e208_4d98_adb4_cf779f8912e3/concepts.md#c01) came early the next morning, at the age of fifty-six.

<note>

*Then from congressman, through the debates of a lost race, to being elected president, and finally being assassinated.*

</note>

<note>

*Let me finish the story first: a young outsider stayed in a small town, opened a store and fell into debt, did odd jobs, postal work, and surveying, became a lawyer through borrowed books, served as a legislator, lost, was elected again, and was finally assassinated. This part uses no concepts at all; it just follows the data.*

</note>

**Bringing in the framework.** First choose the [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23): the [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) is Lincoln alone; the period is 1831 to 1865, divided into rounds at the turning points in the data, with rounds of unequal length, which is the researcher's division. The [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of employers, partners, voters, opponents, and the assassin are all recorded only as changes in the external conditions [arriving at](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) him, and their causes are not analyzed.

<note>

*First, how to cut: one person, thirty-four years, divided into segments at turning points; what other people do is always recorded only as "the conditions changed".*

</note>

Look first at **opening the store**. For the [path](concepts.md#c07) "open a store", the [antecedent](concepts.md#c08) visible in the data is "able to buy on credit", which is in place; he actually opened a store, so opening a store was feasible in that round. Its end-point [record](concepts.md#c06) is debt. Feasible does not mean a good result, and it does not imply a wrong judgment. Next, **running for office**. For the [path](concepts.md#c07) "run for legislator", he actually ran, so the [path](concepts.md#c07) was feasible in that round; **losing the election** is the end-point [record](concepts.md#c06) of this [path](concepts.md#c07), and it neither implies the [path](concepts.md#c07) was infeasible nor says why.

<note>

*He opened the store because he had credit to buy on (an antecedent visible in the data), and he did open it; he also actually ran for office; losing was only the result and does not show that the path did not work.*

</note>

Next look at **after the debt**. After the debt appeared, the [feasible set](concepts.md#c11) may have shrunk; the data show his [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) became several small [paths](concepts.md#c07) in parallel: postal work, odd jobs, surveying, and this paper does not infer a causal link between the two. This is an instance of feedback to the [threshold](concepts.md#c03): the previous round's [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) (buying on credit) may have changed the next round's [antecedents](concepts.md#c08). The [antecedents](concepts.md#c08) of the surveyor [path](concepts.md#c07) include knowledge; "borrowing books" is one [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28), and whether knowledge was thereby in place can only be inferred from later [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) (he became a deputy surveyor). This shows that [antecedents](concepts.md#c08) can be created by the previous round's [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28), but this rests on experience and data and is not inferred. How much he actually learned cannot be read directly.

<note>

*The borrowed books may have given him the knowledge for surveying; that is, what he did in the previous round may have created the conditions for the next. How much he learned can only be inferred from how he did later.*

</note>

The **lawyer [path](concepts.md#c07)**, in the data, shows three [antecedents](concepts.md#c08) (listed by the researcher, not taken as the complete [threshold](concepts.md#c03)): books (an external [resource](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03)), knowledge (accumulated through reading, and inferable only from later [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)), and a license (an external condition). According to the data, the three prerequisites were not acquired simultaneously; they were obtained one by one over the earlier rounds. Being elected legislator brought him to know 〔Stuart〕 and get the books, but this must not be written as "he became a legislator in order to borrow books", for that is a motive.

<note>

*The data show the three conditions for being a lawyer were obtained one at a time; but one cannot say he "became a legislator in order to borrow books", for that is a motive.*

</note>

In **1858**, he actually ran for senator, so running was feasible in that round; the defeat is the end-point [record](concepts.md#c06) of that [path](concepts.md#c07) and does not imply the [path](concepts.md#c07) was infeasible; the same round's [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) (the debates) brought "national fame", which is a favorable change visible in the data; whether it is an [antecedent](concepts.md#c08) of that [path](concepts.md#c07), the data cannot settle; in 1860 he actually won the presidency, so running for president was feasible in that round. The same defeat is an end-point [record](concepts.md#c06) on one [path](concepts.md#c07) and may be the source of a new [antecedent](concepts.md#c08) on another. To call it "wasted" or "paving the way" is in either case a later person's attribution.

<note>

*The same defeat is an end point on one road and a condition for the next step on another. To call it wasted or paving the way is a later person's view.*

</note>

Last is **1865**. The criterion takes a physiological boundary, and April 15 is recorded as [criterion exit](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09). The assassin's act is recorded only as another person's [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) [arriving at](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) this [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25); whether the two had a [dedicated history](../paper_ae79e938_76e1_4bf9_b2ab_995c03e16c9e/concepts.md#c05) and whether the act was compatible or a [mismatch](../paper_ae79e938_76e1_4bf9_b2ab_995c03e16c9e/concepts.md#c08) are not handled in this paper. "[Death](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c22)" is a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) marked by the researcher or by later people; this chapter uses [criterion exit](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09) and specifies the criterion, which can be out of step with structural [exit](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c43). He did not die of old age; this does not imply that there was anything wrong with the [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of the earlier rounds, nor that the survival [record](concepts.md#c06) is a "failure"; [criterion exit](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09) has no necessary relation to whether he chose rightly or wrongly (see the non-implication list in 4.3).

<note>

*Now walk through once more with this paper's notation: every choice is a path, a path has a threshold; one round's output changes the next round's threshold; losing an election is only the end-point record of a path, not a sign that the path was infeasible; and finally death records only the time point, infers no cause, much less that he did anything wrong.*

</note>

**What can be said and what cannot be inferred.** What can be said: the data show changes of occupation, place, debt, and license at each stage, corresponding to [antecedents](concepts.md#c08) being gained and lost; his [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) [arrived at](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) the outside, and the data show that after the outside's responses the [feasible set](concepts.md#c11) of the next round changed; no causal link is inferred. What cannot be inferred: why he opened the store, why he studied law, why he ran for office; the inner effect of debt and defeat on him; the details of income in each round (only scattered figures were read); the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) of any single [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28); evaluations such as "hard-fated" or "honest" (these are later people's attributions).

<note>

*All that can be said is "what he did, what he had in hand, how the outside responded"; why, what he thought, and whether he was ill-fated cannot be said.*

</note>

**Notes on the data.** Only summaries on public web pages and collection descriptions were read; neither full biographies nor original documents were read. Sources differ on dates and debt amounts, and these are listed side by side; what each source says is registered in a separate external stub. One slide caption states that his surveying instruments, horse, and saddle were auctioned off by the sheriff to pay the debt; this is a second-hand description and is not adopted in this chapter.

<note>

*The data are thin, and sources differ on dates and amounts, so I listed them side by side and did not force a choice.*

</note>

## 7. Failure Conditions

**Cases.** The cases in Chapter 3 (synthetic) and Chapter 6 (Lincoln) are both for calibration; they are not a source of derivation and do not constitute empirical support.

<note>

*Both cases are only for comparison.*

</note>

**Failure conditions.**

- When the four laws are overturned, the constraint-level conclusions that depend on them lose their source; losing the source only means the chain of derivation is broken, not that the proposition is false.
- If the [Forced Wager](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c11), the [Wall of the Undetermined](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c10), and the [Wall of Provenance](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c08) are overturned, Rule 8, Section 2.5 and item 9 of Section 5 lose their source; if the distinction between [exit](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c43) and [death](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c22) is overturned, the corresponding item in 4.1 loses its source; the rest is unaffected.
- If [Unconfirmability of Drives](../paper_ae79e938_76e1_4bf9_b2ab_995c03e16c9e/concepts.md#c03) is overturned, the conclusions that stable [tendency](concepts.md#c02), the presence or absence of reproduction, [opportunity cost](concepts.md#c05), and so on do not imply internal drives or motives lose their source; the rest is unaffected.
- Premises 1 to 4 are scope settings, not claims that can be overturned; changing them changes the scope this paper deals with, and then the conclusions obtained from them alone (without passing through any one law) lose their source and no longer apply under the changed scope.
- If Premise 5 is violated by reality (for example, a species that can act in parallel), only the condition-level conclusions that use it fail (Rule 6, the exclusion check in 3.4, and the comparison calculation of [opportunity cost](concepts.md#c05)).
- If the definitions of [record](concepts.md#c06), [output tendency](concepts.md#c02), [threshold](concepts.md#c03), or [same outcome from different paths](concepts.md#c04) are changed, the rules and sections that depend on them (Rules 1 to 4, 7, 9, and Sections 2.1, 2.2, 3.1, 4.1, 4.3) must have their sources rechecked.
- If the experience that "an [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) creates [antecedents](concepts.md#c08) for a later round and may close some [paths](concepts.md#c07)" does not hold, only the conditional-level part of Rule 5 fails, and the calibration narrative in Chapter 6 that depends on it must be rechecked.
- An observation opposite to a conclusion can only be attributed to an error at the starting point of the derivation or an error in the derivation; the latter is a correction, not a falsification.

<note>

*Failure conditions say: if one item does not hold up, which conclusions lose their basis along with it.*

</note>

**Not conclusions of this paper.** Family, network structure, the compatibility and [mismatch](../paper_ae79e938_76e1_4bf9_b2ab_995c03e16c9e/concepts.md#c08) of other people's [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28), violence and mutual benefit, the continuation of offspring, and the survival of the network are not handled in this paper. Data that were not obtained are not written as "nonexistent".

<note>

*When would this paper's conclusions fail to stand? If an upstream law is overturned, the conclusions that depend on it lose their source, but that does not mean they are wrong, only that they lose their basis. For a species in which a person can do two things at once, only the items that use "only one thing at a time" are affected. If an observation contradicts a conclusion, first check whether the starting point or the derivation went wrong; one cannot directly say the theory is overturned.*

</note>

## Appendix: Derivations

```text
Premises 1 to 5 as in Chapter 1.
Derivation 1 A threshold is a set of antecedents; if any antecedent is not in place, the path is infeasible in that round (Premise 3 + Premise 4; constraint level)
Derivation 2 All antecedents in place only means feasible; when not locked in, the next moment yields only the weight (Law of the Black Box; constraint level)
Derivation 3 High tendency does not imply that the next occurrence will happen; tendency is the shape of the records, not a reading of the inside (Law of the Black Box + Unconfirmability of Drives; constraint level)
Derivation 4 A self-report is one output, lossy, and not a complete description of the cause or of the inside (Law of Attrition + Law of the Black Box + definition of record; constraint level)
Derivation 5 If an output arrives at the outside and the outside responds, the response enters the history, and the history takes part in the next round's weight and may change the antecedents; the two may respectively affect the feasible set and the relative weighting within it, not guaranteed to be visible and not guaranteed to be learning (Law of Flux + Law of Non-Restoration; constraint level). Outputs creating antecedents and possibly closing paths are conditional level (depending on experience and data)
Derivation 6 Differences at the end point must not be used to infer differences in choice at the starting point: different early responses may make the feasible set bifurcate (Law of Non-Restoration + Derivation 5; constraint level)
Derivation 7 Reproduction events and other outputs that have occurred only increase and never decrease; the absence of an event in a round does not cancel existing events (Law of Non-Restoration; constraint level)
Derivation 8 Survival looks only at the criterion and actual events, not at causes (Premise 1 + the distinction between criterion and fact; constraint level)
Derivation 9 A single path being feasible does not imply that paths can be carried out at the same time; paths that are excluded but whose thresholds are in place can serve as the researcher's comparison calculation and do not enter the weight (Premise 5; condition level)
Derivation 10 Same outcome from different paths is only an external classification and does not imply the same path or convergence (Law of the Black Box + Premise 1; constraint level)
Derivation 11 At the moments when a Wo is not locked in, an output cannot be calculated accurately beforehand, cannot be taken back afterwards, and not outputting is also one of the things that occur; so when an onlooker classes a single output as stable or risky, this is the onlooker's own distinction; whether a result is good or bad requires stating whose criterion, and the quality of the decision cannot be judged on it alone (Forced Wager + Wall of Provenance + Law of the Black Box + Law of Non-Restoration; constraint level)
Derivation 12 Want, can, and outcome do not imply one another (Law of the Black Box + Law of Non-Restoration + Derivation 1 + Derivation 3; constraint level)
Note: State and resource are the researcher's classification, and the threshold does not depend on this classification.
```
