## public 要能果三問 / Want-Can-Outcome Questions

- 定義：關於一個[個體](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20)的三個問題；這裡的[個體](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20)是生物人，屬上游所稱的[我](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)：能自述，也能對自己作[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)。對這樣的[個體](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20)，個體層的問題只有這三個：它要什麼，對應[權重](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06)與[輸出傾向](concepts.md#c02)，研究者單憑[記錄](concepts.md#c06)讀不出[權重](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06)的值，只能統計[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)的[記錄](concepts.md#c06)；它能不能做到，對應[門檻](concepts.md#c03)；做完會變成什麼樣，對應[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)外界之後外界的回應、時窗終點的[記錄](concepts.md#c06)，依[判準退場](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09)所記的存續，以及依另訂的事件判準所記的生殖（生殖不屬上游「[解](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c05)」的維度）。三問各自問不同的事：要什麼推不出能不能做到，能做到推不出會做，做了推不出結果是所要的。
- Definition: Three questions about an [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20); the [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) here is a biological human, a [Self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) in the upstream sense: one who can report on itself and can make a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) of itself. For such an [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20), the only questions at the individual level are these three: what it wants, which corresponds to [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) and [output tendency](concepts.md#c02) (the researcher cannot read off the value of the [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) from the [records](concepts.md#c06) alone and can only work statistically with [records](concepts.md#c06) of [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)); whether it can do it, which corresponds to the [threshold](concepts.md#c03); and what it becomes after doing it, which corresponds to the environment's response after an [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10), the [record](concepts.md#c06) at the end of the time window, and survival as recorded under a [criterion exit](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09) standard, and reproduction as recorded under a separately specified event criterion (reproduction is not a dimension of the upstream [solution](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c05)). The three questions ask about different things: what is wanted does not imply whether it can be done, being able does not imply doing it, and having done it does not imply that the result is what was wanted.
- 裁決：2026-10-27 依稽核意見，定義保留正文對研究對象的限定：個體是生物人，屬上游所稱的我（能自述，也能對自己作分別），避免下游把「只有三問」套到不符合這個限定的個體；限定內容直接寫出，不指涉本篇的前提編號。同日曾一併寫入「同一輪不能同時在兩地、體力與注意力總量有限」，後判定那是關於生物人的經驗前提，不用來界定對象，「只有三問」也不依賴它，故刪去，與正文〇一致。

## public 輸出傾向 / Output Tendency

- 定義：研究者對[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)的[記錄](concepts.md#c06)中反覆可見的形狀所作的歸納，描述一個[個體](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20)在指定[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)下傾向做出哪一類[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)。它是在指定的[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)、時窗、分類規則與[門檻](concepts.md#c03)條件下作的歸納。分類規則是研究者的[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)，所歸的類別不是行為本身自帶的界線。輸出傾向是[記錄](concepts.md#c06)的形狀，與[權重](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06)的關係只能是估計，不是[權重](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06)的讀數。
- Definition: A generalization by the researcher of the recurring shape in the [records](concepts.md#c06) of [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28), describing which kind of [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) an [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) tends to produce under a stated [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23). It is made under a stated [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23), time window, classification rule, and [threshold](concepts.md#c03) conditions. The classification rule is the researcher's [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18), and the resulting categories are not boundaries that the behavior carries by itself. An [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) tendency is a shape of the [records](concepts.md#c06); its relation to [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) can only be an estimate and is not a reading of the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06).
- 裁決：2026-10-27 判定本概念與上游「印象」不是同一個概念：輸出傾向是記錄的形狀。

<a id="c03"></a>
## public 門檻 / Threshold

- 定義：一條[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)[路徑](concepts.md#c07)成立所需的一組[前件](concepts.md#c08)。[路徑](concepts.md#c07)是研究者在指定[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)下劃出的一類[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)序列；[前件](concepts.md#c08)是[路徑](concepts.md#c07)成立所需的各項條件，可以是身體素質、[知識](concepts.md#c13)、時間、位置、外界條件、工具與[資源](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03)，記在哪一類由研究者講明（見[狀態變數](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c02)、[資源變數](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03)）。任一[前件](concepts.md#c08)不具備，該[路徑](concepts.md#c07)在該輪不可行；全部具備只表示可行，不表示會發生。
- Definition: The set of [antecedents](concepts.md#c08) required for an [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) [path](concepts.md#c07) to hold. A [path](concepts.md#c07) is a class of [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) sequences drawn by the researcher under a stated [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23); an [antecedent](concepts.md#c08) is any condition the [path](concepts.md#c07) requires, such as bodily qualities, [knowledge](concepts.md#c13), time, position, environmental conditions, tools and [resources](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03), recorded as a [state variable](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c02) or a [resource variable](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03), as the researcher states. If any [antecedent](concepts.md#c08) is absent, the [path](concepts.md#c07) is infeasible in that round; if all are present, it is only feasible, not thereby occurring.

## public 異途結果同類 / Same Outcome from Different Paths

- 定義：研究者依明示的結果變數、時窗與容許區間，把不同[個體](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20)各自不同的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)歷程所得的結果，歸入同一類。這是研究者的外部歸類；它不是[趨同](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c37)（[趨同](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c37)指[耦合](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c36)之下，指定維度上參與者[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)模式之間的距離縮短）。結果同類不推出[路徑](concepts.md#c07)相同，也不推出內部問題或驅動力相同；它也不提供發生頻率。
- Definition: A grouping by the researcher, under a stated outcome variable, time window and tolerance interval, of the outcomes that different [individuals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) reached through different [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) histories into the same class. It is an external classification by the researcher; it is not [convergence](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c37) ([convergence](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c37) is a shortening of the distance between participants' [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) patterns on a stated dimension under [coupling](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c36)). Sameness of outcome class does not imply sameness of [path](concepts.md#c07), nor sameness of any internal problem or driving force; nor does it supply any frequency of occurrence.
- 裁決：2026-10-28 依稽核意見（跨-11），定義補上「不提供發生頻率」，與正文規則七對齊：結果同類只是把結果歸入同一類，不是計數。

## public 機會成本 / Opportunity Cost

- 定義：研究者在自己指定的估值下，對同一輪中已走[路徑](concepts.md#c07)的終點與被排除[路徑](concepts.md#c07)的預期終點所作的比較。被排除[路徑](concepts.md#c07)限於與已走[路徑](concepts.md#c07)互相排斥、且[門檻](concepts.md#c03)具備者；它們沒有發生，其終點只能是依某個依據作的估計，沒有依據時終點未知。機會成本是研究者的比較計算，不是[個體](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20)的量，也不屬於[權重](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06)。
- Definition: A comparison by the researcher, under a valuation the researcher specifies, between the end point of the [path](concepts.md#c07) taken in a round and the expected end points of the excluded [paths](concepts.md#c07). The excluded [paths](concepts.md#c07) are limited to those that are mutually exclusive with the [path](concepts.md#c07) taken and whose [thresholds](concepts.md#c03) are met; they did not occur, so their end points can only be estimates made on some basis, and are unknown where there is none. Opportunity cost is a comparison computed by the researcher; it is not a quantity of the [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) and does not belong to the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06).

## public 記錄 / Record

- 定義：研究者在講明的[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)下，依自己的需要選取、對已發生之事所作的登記；記什麼由研究者的登記動作決定，不預先列舉內容。常見的有時窗內實際發生的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)、起點與終點的[狀態變數](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c02)、[資源變數](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03)，以及[判準退場](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09)是否發生；所記的內容可以包括發生了什麼、何時、何地、幾次、間隔多久。只登記已發生的事；沒登記的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)仍在[歷史](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13)裡，可能繼續影響[權重](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06)。自述登記為一筆[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)，與其他[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)同地位。[路徑](concepts.md#c07)的[門檻](concepts.md#c03)是研究者的判斷，可標「未知」，與[記錄](concepts.md#c06)並列附上，不算[記錄](concepts.md#c06)。
- Definition: A registration, made by the researcher under a stated [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23) and selected according to the researcher's own need, of what has happened; what is registered is fixed by the researcher's act of registering and is not listed in advance. Commonly it includes the [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) that actually occurred in the time window, the start-point and end-point [state](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c02) variables and [resource](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03) variables, and whether [criterion exit](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09) occurred; what is registered may include what happened, when, where, how many times, and at what intervals. Only events that have occurred are registered; unregistered [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) remain in the [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) and may continue to affect the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06). A self-report is registered as one [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28), with the same standing as any other [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28). The [threshold](concepts.md#c03) of a [path](concepts.md#c07) is the researcher's judgment, may be marked unknown, and is attached alongside the [records](concepts.md#c06) but is not itself a record.
- 裁決：2026-10-27 判定本概念與暗宇宙篇的「記錄」（信的組成單位）是不同的概念：本篇的記錄由研究者的登記動作定義（依切法選取已發生之事），不預先列舉內容；門檻是研究者的判斷，與記錄並列，不算記錄。

<a id="c07"></a>
## public 路徑 / Path

- 定義：研究者在指定[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)下劃出的一類[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)序列。怎麼劃是研究者的判定；路徑不是[個體](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20)自己帶著的邊界。一條路徑在某一輪是否可行，看它的[門檻](concepts.md#c03)。
- Definition: A class of [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) sequences drawn by the researcher under a stated [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23). How it is drawn is the researcher's determination; a path is not a boundary that the [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) carries. Whether a path is feasible in a given round is read from its [threshold](concepts.md#c03).

<a id="c08"></a>
## public 前件 / Antecedent

- 定義：一條[路徑](concepts.md#c07)成立所需的各項條件之一，是[門檻](concepts.md#c03)的組成項。可以是身體素質、[知識](concepts.md#c13)、時間、位置、外界條件、工具與[資源](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03)；記在[狀態變數](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c02)還是[資源變數](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03)，由研究者講明，不先分類。
- Definition: One of the conditions a [path](concepts.md#c07) requires in order to hold, and a component of the [threshold](concepts.md#c03). It may be bodily qualities, [knowledge](concepts.md#c13), time, position, environmental conditions, tools or [resources](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03); whether it is recorded as a [state](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c02) variable or a [resource](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03) variable is stated by the researcher and is not classified in advance.

## public 個體局部解 / Individual Local Solution

## public 可行集合 / Feasible Set

- 定義：某一輪中，[門檻](concepts.md#c03)的各項[前件](concepts.md#c08)都具備的[路徑](concepts.md#c07)所成的集合。它回答的是物理上能不能，不回答會不會發生；[前件](concepts.md#c08)隨[歷史](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13)可能變動，所以每一輪的可行集合可能擴大、縮小或不變。
- Definition: In a given round, the set of [paths](concepts.md#c07) whose [thresholds](concepts.md#c03) have all of their [antecedents](concepts.md#c08) met. It answers whether something is physically possible, not whether it will occur; because [antecedents](concepts.md#c08) may change with [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13), the feasible set of each round may grow, shrink or stay the same.

## public 知識 / Knowledge

- Definition: Among the [antecedents](concepts.md#c08) in the [threshold](concepts.md#c03) of a [path](concepts.md#c07), those that cannot be checked directly; it varies with the [path](concepts.md#c07) (having a cook's knowledge does not imply having medical knowledge). It can only be inferred from the [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) on that [path](concepts.md#c07), is recorded as "inferred from [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)" or "unknown", and is not assumed to be met. Bodily and external conditions involve physical changes that can be directly [recorded](concepts.md#c06) and checked directly, and are not included.

## public 現存樣本條件化 / Conditioning on Existing Samples

- 定義：研究者以「存續」（尚未發生[判準退場](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09)）為條件選樣的做法：樣本裡只有尚未[判準退場](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09)的[個體](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20)。[判準退場](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09)只取決於研究者所訂判準與實際事件，與[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)的好壞無必然關係，所以存續者的表現不足以直接用來評價好壞；已[判準退場](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09)者留下的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)，凡已[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)別人或留在[信號容器](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c26)中者，仍在[歷史](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13)裡，沒[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)任何人者研究者可能拿不到，所以推不出消失者不可研究。這是研究者的樣本邊界，不是外界有選擇目的。
- Definition: The researcher's practice of selecting a sample conditioned on survival (not yet having undergone [criterion exit](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09)): the sample contains only [individuals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) that have not yet undergone [criterion exit](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09). Whether [criterion exit](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09) occurs depends only on the criterion the researcher sets and on actual events, and has no necessary relation to whether the [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) are good or bad, so the performance of survivors is not enough to be used directly for evaluation; the [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) left by those who have undergone [criterion exit](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09), wherever they have already [arrived](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at others or remain in a [signal container](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c26), are still in the [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13), while those that [arrived](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at no one may be unobtainable by the researcher; so it does not follow that those who have disappeared cannot be studied. This is the researcher's sample boundary, not a purpose of selection on the part of the outside world.
- 裁決：2026-10-27 判定「現存樣本條件化」登記為本篇概念：它是第四章 4.4 的獨立小節，有明確的選樣方式與推論限制；原列「不登記」，依稽核意見改為登記。
