## public 要能果三問 / Want-Can-Outcome Questions

- 定義：關於一個[個體](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20)的三個問題：它要什麼，對應[權重](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06)與[輸出傾向](concepts.md#c02)，研究者讀不到[權重](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06)，只能統計[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)的[記錄](concepts.md#c06)；它能不能做到，對應[門檻](concepts.md#c03)；做完會變成什麼樣，對應[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)外界之後外界的回應、時窗終點的[記錄](concepts.md#c06)，依[判準退場](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09)所記的存續，以及依另訂的事件判準所記的生殖（生殖不屬上游「[解](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c05)」的維度）。三問各自問不同的事，彼此不互相推出。
- Definition: Three questions about an [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20): what it wants, which corresponds to [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) and [output tendency](concepts.md#c02) (the researcher cannot read [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) and can only work statistically with [records](concepts.md#c06) of [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)); whether it can do it, which corresponds to the [threshold](concepts.md#c03); and what it becomes after doing it, which corresponds to the environment's response after an [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10), the [record](concepts.md#c06) at the end of the time window, and survival as recorded under a [criterion exit](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09) standard, and reproduction as recorded under a separately specified event criterion (reproduction is not a dimension of the upstream [solution](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c05)). The three questions ask about different things and do not imply one another.

## public 輸出傾向 / Output Tendency

- 定義：研究者對[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)的[記錄](concepts.md#c06)中反覆可見的形狀所作的歸納，描述一個[個體](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20)在指定[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)下傾向做出哪一類[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)。使用時必須附上[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)、時窗、分類規則與[門檻](concepts.md#c03)條件。分類規則是研究者的[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)，所歸的類別不是行為本身自帶的界線。輸出傾向是[記錄](concepts.md#c06)的形狀，與[權重](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06)的關係只能是估計，不是[權重](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06)的讀數。
- Definition: A generalization by the researcher of the recurring shape in the [records](concepts.md#c06) of [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28), describing which kind of [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) an [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) tends to produce under a stated [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23). It must be stated together with the [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23), the time window, the classification rule, and the [threshold](concepts.md#c03) conditions. The classification rule is the researcher's [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18), and the resulting categories are not boundaries that the behavior carries by itself. An [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) tendency is a shape of the [records](concepts.md#c06); its relation to [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) can only be an estimate and is not a reading of the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06).
- 裁決：2026-10-27 判定本概念與上游「印象」不是同一個概念：輸出傾向是記錄的形狀。

<a id="c03"></a>
## public 門檻 / Threshold

- 定義：一條[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)[路徑](concepts.md#c07)成立所需的一組[前件](concepts.md#c08)。[路徑](concepts.md#c07)是研究者在指定[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)下劃出的一類[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)序列；[前件](concepts.md#c08)是[路徑](concepts.md#c07)成立所需的各項條件，可以是身體素質、知識、時間、位置、外界條件、工具與[資源](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03)，記在哪一類由研究者講明（見[狀態變數](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c02)、[資源變數](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03)）。任一[前件](concepts.md#c08)不具備，該[路徑](concepts.md#c07)在該輪不可行；全部具備只表示可行，不表示會發生。
- Definition: The set of [antecedents](concepts.md#c08) required for an [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) [path](concepts.md#c07) to hold. A [path](concepts.md#c07) is a class of [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) sequences drawn by the researcher under a stated [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23); an [antecedent](concepts.md#c08) is any condition the [path](concepts.md#c07) requires, such as bodily qualities, knowledge, time, position, environmental conditions, tools and [resources](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03), recorded as a [state](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c02) variable or a resource variable, as the researcher states. If any [antecedent](concepts.md#c08) is absent, the [path](concepts.md#c07) is infeasible in that round; if all are present, it is only feasible, not thereby occurring.

## public 異途結果同類 / Same Outcome from Different Paths

- 定義：研究者依明示的結果變數、時窗與容許區間，把不同[個體](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20)各自不同的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)歷程所得的結果，歸入同一類。這是研究者的外部歸類；它不是[趨同](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c37)（[趨同](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c37)指[耦合](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c36)之下，指定維度上參與者[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)模式之間的距離縮短）。結果同類不推出[路徑](concepts.md#c07)相同，也不推出內部問題或驅動力相同。
- Definition: A grouping by the researcher, under a stated outcome variable, time window and tolerance interval, of the outcomes that different [individuals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) reached through different [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) histories into the same class. It is an external classification by the researcher; it is not [convergence](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c37) ([convergence](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c37) is a shortening of the distance between participants' [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) patterns on a stated dimension under [coupling](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c36)). Sameness of outcome class does not imply sameness of [path](concepts.md#c07), nor sameness of any internal problem or driving force.

## public 機會成本 / Opportunity Cost

- 定義：研究者在自己指定的估值下，對同一輪中已走[路徑](concepts.md#c07)的終點與被排除[路徑](concepts.md#c07)的預期終點所作的比較。被排除[路徑](concepts.md#c07)限於與已走[路徑](concepts.md#c07)互相排斥、且[門檻](concepts.md#c03)具備者；它們沒有發生，其終點只能是估計，須寫明估計依據，沒有依據時記為未知。機會成本是研究者的比較計算，不是[個體](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20)的量，也不屬於[權重](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06)。
- Definition: A comparison by the researcher, under a valuation the researcher specifies, between the end point of the [path](concepts.md#c07) taken in a round and the expected end points of the excluded [paths](concepts.md#c07). The excluded [paths](concepts.md#c07) are limited to those that are mutually exclusive with the [path](concepts.md#c07) taken and whose [thresholds](concepts.md#c03) are met; they did not occur, so their end points can only be estimates, with the basis for estimation stated and recorded as unknown where there is none. Opportunity cost is a comparison computed by the researcher; it is not a quantity of the [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) and does not belong to the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06).

## public 記錄 / Record

- 定義：研究者在講明的[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)下，對時窗內實際發生的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)，以及起點與終點的[狀態變數](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c02)、[資源變數](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03)所作的登記：發生了什麼、何時、何地、幾次、間隔多久。只登記已發生的事；是研究者的選取，沒登記的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)仍在[歷史](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13)裡繼續影響[權重](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06)。自述登記為一筆[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)，與其他[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)同地位。
- Definition: A registration by the researcher, under a stated [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23), of the [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) that actually occurred in the time window, together with the start-point and end-point [state](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c02) variables and [resource](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03) variables: what happened, when, where, how many times, and at what intervals. Only events that have occurred are registered; the registration is the researcher's selection, and unregistered [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) remain in the [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) and continue to affect the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06). A self-report is registered as one [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28), with the same standing as any other [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28).
- 裁決：2026-10-27 判定本概念與暗宇宙篇的「記錄」（信的組成單位）是不同的概念：本篇的記錄是研究者對輸出與起點終點變數所作的登記。

## public 路徑 / Path

- 定義：研究者在指定[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)下劃出的一類[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)序列。怎麼劃由研究者講明；路徑不是[個體](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20)自己帶著的邊界。一條路徑在某一輪是否可行，看它的[門檻](concepts.md#c03)。
- Definition: A class of [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) sequences drawn by the researcher under a stated [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23). How it is drawn is stated by the researcher; a path is not a boundary that the [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) carries. Whether a path is feasible in a given round is read from its [threshold](concepts.md#c03).

<a id="c08"></a>
## public 前件 / Antecedent

- 定義：一條[路徑](concepts.md#c07)成立所需的各項條件之一，是[門檻](concepts.md#c03)的組成項。可以是身體素質、知識、時間、位置、外界條件、工具與[資源](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03)；記在[狀態變數](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c02)還是[資源變數](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03)，由研究者講明，不先分類。
- Definition: One of the conditions a [path](concepts.md#c07) requires in order to hold, and a component of the [threshold](concepts.md#c03). It may be bodily qualities, knowledge, time, position, environmental conditions, tools or [resources](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03); whether it is recorded as a [state](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c02) variable or a [resource](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03) variable is stated by the researcher and is not classified in advance.

## public 個體局部解 / Individual Local Solution

- 定義：指定[個體](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20)在所屬[推演輪](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c07)的下一刻[狀態](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c02)、[資源](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03)，以及[判準退場](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09)的相關[記錄](concepts.md#c06)；若該[個體](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20)已被列為指定[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)的[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)、這些欄位已記入該輪的[解](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c05)，則是上游[解](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c05)的單[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)部分。
- Definition: For a specified [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20), the next-moment [state](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c02) and [resources](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c03) in the [derivation round](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c07) it belongs to, together with the relevant [records](concepts.md#c06) of the [criterion exit](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09); if the [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) is listed as a [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) of the stated [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23) and these fields are entered in that round's [solution](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c05), it is the single-[node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) part of the upstream [solution](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c05).

## private 四輪記錄表 / Four-Round Record Table

- 定義：一份合成資料表，列出甲、乙、丙、己四人各自在第一輪的處境、[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)對外界的改變，以及第二輪起[門檻](concepts.md#c03)的變化。人物與數值皆為合成設定，非實測；它是校準用的示例，不作推導來源，也不構成經驗支持。
- Definition: A synthetic data table that lists, for four persons (A, B, C and F), each person's situation in the first round, the changes that the [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) makes to the outside world, and the change in the [threshold](concepts.md#c03) from the second round on. The persons and figures are synthetic settings, not measurements; the table is an example for calibration, not a source of derivation and not empirical support.

<a id="c11"></a>
## public 可行集合 / Feasible Set

- 定義：某一輪中，[門檻](concepts.md#c03)的各項[前件](concepts.md#c08)都具備的[路徑](concepts.md#c07)所成的集合。它回答的是物理上能不能，不回答會不會發生；[前件](concepts.md#c08)隨[歷史](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13)可能變動，所以每一輪的可行集合可能擴大、縮小或不變。
- Definition: In a given round, the set of [paths](concepts.md#c07) whose [thresholds](concepts.md#c03) have all of their [antecedents](concepts.md#c08) met. It answers whether something is physically possible, not whether it will occur; because [antecedents](concepts.md#c08) may change with [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13), the feasible set of each round may grow, shrink or stay the same.

## private 五條路徑門檻表 / Five-Path Threshold Table

- 定義：一份合成資料表，列出受雇工作、擺攤販售、自行種植販售、出售既有物品、搶劫五條[路徑](concepts.md#c07)各自的[門檻](concepts.md#c03)（[前件](concepts.md#c08)清單）與證據地位。[路徑](concepts.md#c07)與[前件](concepts.md#c08)皆為合成設定，非實測；它是校準用的示例，不作推導來源，也不構成經驗支持。
- Definition: A synthetic data table that lists, for five paths (employment, setting up a stall, growing and selling, selling existing goods, and robbery), each path's [threshold](concepts.md#c03) (the list of [antecedents](concepts.md#c08)) and the standing of the evidence. The [paths](concepts.md#c07) and antecedents are synthetic settings, not measurements; the table is an example for calibration, not a source of derivation and not empirical support.
