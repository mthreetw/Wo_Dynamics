# Tools and Examples Related to the Four Laws

## 0. Position

This paper sits between *Wo Dynamics* and the application papers. Its aim is not to propose anything new, but to state what is already known with a single stance and a single set of tools, so that different fields are unified under one framework.

The strength of this paper comes from prohibition. The more constraints, the smaller the feasible region of a prediction, and the higher its precision. Every tool comes with prohibitions, each marked with its form:

- [Never Occurs](concepts.md#c01): a single occurrence refutes it.
- [Not Guaranteed](concepts.md#c02): the framework does not assert that it necessarily happens. Whether other constraints make it hold in a particular case, the framework does not assert either; to claim that it necessarily happens, one must state the constraints relied on. What it limits is how much confidence a prediction can have.

Prohibitions derived from the framework hold for the past and for the future alike, because they are derived from the four laws and the setting of this paper, and both hold at every [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09); there is also only one [generating function](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c15), which does not change over time. Statistics of the past can only give [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) within the feasible region; they cannot give prohibitions.

The framework starts from a question about the mind: "why can I not understand you?" But its conclusions, apart from [impression](concepts.md#c45), do not depend on the mind.

Names of other disciplines in parentheses, such as "(cybernetics: feedback [loop](concepts.md#c31))", are only signposts for finding related literature. They carry no definitions and may not be used as grounds for inference: the definitions, presuppositions and conclusions of those disciplines are not brought into the tools of this paper. In case of doubt, the definitions and formal passages of this paper prevail.

### The place of the four laws in this paper

The four laws of the upstream paper, the [Law of Flux](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c24), the [Law of the Black Box](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c25), the [Law of Attrition](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c26) and the [Law of Non-Restoration](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c28), are the axioms of this paper and are derived from none of its definitions. They are the main source of the prohibitions in this paper; the remaining prohibitions are derived from the setting of this paper. Each law has a landing point in this paper; every application must be checked against the four laws at least once.

## 1. Setting

This paper takes over seven concepts from the upstream paper: [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18), [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19), and the [Law of Flux](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c24), the [Law of the Black Box](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c25), the [Law of Attrition](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c26) and the [Law of Non-Restoration](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c28). Terms used in the definitions of these concepts, such as [universe](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c02), [presentation](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04), [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06), [collapse](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c08), [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09), [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) and [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10), are also taken directly from the upstream definitions and are not redefined here. This section additionally defines the following concept.

### Definitions

- [Incompleteness of Knowing Others](concepts.md#c19): no [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) can guarantee that it has finished [distinguishing](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) another [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16). A [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) can [distinguish](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) the other only through the [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) that has [arrived](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at it; the other's information [arriving](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) is one event and its [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) is another, and between them the other's [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) may still grow, while, when the other is not [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12), the outcome of its next [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) cannot be derived beforehand ([Law of the Black Box](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c25)). Moreover, nothing guarantees that the parts of the other's [own segment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c17) that [arrive](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) only at the other also [arrive](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at the self; and once the self's [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18), as a [writing](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c29), becomes part of the other's [own segment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c17), it may change the other's [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) for its next [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09).

### Derived from the definitions

- [Granularity](concepts.md#c20): by the definition of [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06), if the outcome projection of a coarser [presentation](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04) can be obtained from the outcome projection of a finer [presentation](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04) by a mapping, then the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) distribution of the coarser [presentation](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04) equals the pushforward, by that mapping, of the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) distribution of the finer [presentation](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04). Thus this paper asserts consistency only between descriptions with compatible outcome projections; it does not assert that arbitrary [cuts](concepts.md#c23) are automatically compatible.
- [History](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) loses nothing: by the definition of [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13), after a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) ceases to be a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), its [own segment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c17) remains complete in [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13).
- The other may change as a result: by the definition of [own segment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c17), a [writing](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c29) left by a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18), if it belongs to the [own segment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c17) of another [presentation](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04), would, if replaced with something else, change the distribution of that presentation's [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) for its next [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09).

### Premises

- [Clock Time](concepts.md#c21): a [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) is only the order of [collapses](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c08), which no [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) can measure. The time a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) measures with a clock follows physics and is not formalized in this paper.
- The shape of [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) can repeat: by the definition of [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13), [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) is new at every [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09); but this paper assumes that different [histories](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) can correspond to [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) of similar shape. What is ever new is [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13); what has nothing new is [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06).
- There is only one [generating function](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c15): this paper assumes that there is only one [generating function](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c15), and that it does not change with the [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09).

The notation in the formal passage below is written from the definitions of the upstream concepts cited and does not change their meaning; where it differs from the upstream concept tables, the upstream prevails.

```text
Formal: this paper's notation
n, m: indices preserving the total order of moments only; this paper does not assume positive-integer indexing or use +1/−1
Branch set B; a weight distribution on B: every branch weight ≥ 0, summing to 1
The initial state of the universe is denoted position 0
Past(n): all content already in history before the collapse at n
W(n) = WriteRule(Past(n)); WriteRule is a function
H⁺(n) = (Past(n), W(n))                        -- history after writing, before the collapse at n
H≤(n) = (H⁺(n), C(n))                          -- history through the collapse at n
Uniqueness of history: for n < m, H≤(n) is a prefix of Past(m)
w(n) = G(H⁺(n))                                -- G: generating function
π_x : B → O_x ∪ {⊥}                            -- outcome projection of presentation x; ⊥: not falling on x
w(n, x) = (π_x)_* w(n)                         -- pushforward: branch weights with the same value are added
Arr(x, n) = {0}
  ∪ {positions of writings falling on x no later than n}
  ∪ {positions of collapse outcomes no later than n whose collapses involve x}
Inv(x, n) ⟺ w(n, x)(⊥) < 1
T(x) = {n : Inv(x, n)}                         -- set of moments of x
Next_x(n) = min{m ∈ T(x) : n < m}, when it exists
Lock(x, n) ⟺ Inv(x, n) ∧ w(n, x) has exactly one value of non-zero weight, and that value ≠ ⊥
Rep(p, v): replace position p in the history already generated by v; later writings are recomputed by WriteRule,
  later weights by G, and later outcomes are selected anew according to those weights
Dist(q | c): the distribution of q given c; actual: the actual value at that position
Own(x, n) = {p : ∃ v, Dist(w(Next_x(n), x) | Rep(p, v)) ≠ Dist(w(Next_x(n), x) | Rep(p, actual))}
Wo(x, n): x satisfies the definition of wo at n
Timing: the participation condition of Wo(x, n) is judged by w(Next_x(n)), the weights of x's next moment;
  the not-locked-in condition looks at Next_x(n) and later moments of x
D(x, n): the content of the distinction made by x at n; by the upstream definition, that distinction leaves a writing in history
```

The following is the formal statement of this paper's [granularity](concepts.md#c20), written in the notation above.

```text
Formal:
Granularity: if π_x = f ∘ π_y, then w(n, x) = f_* w(n, y)
```

## 2. The Basic Unit

The basic unit of this paper is the [loop](concepts.md#c31), not the [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20).

- **The [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) cannot serve as the unit.** By definition, an [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) is a [presentation](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04) marked off by some [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) through [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18); by the definition of [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) has no fixed core, and a [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) is a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), so there is no [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) with a fixed core in the [universe](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c02). When inference takes the [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) as the unit and treats the [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) as something unchanged across [moments](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09), it amounts to putting a fixed core back in at the bottom.
- **Every [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) is a [loop](concepts.md#c31).** By the definition of [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10), the outcome of a [collapse](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c08) that [involves](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c11) a [presentation](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04) [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at it; by the definition of [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), whatever [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at it takes part in its [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) for its next [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09). So as long as a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) has had a [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09), its own outcomes come back to take part in itself. This is derived from the definitions and need not be posited separately.
- **Not every [loop](concepts.md#c31) is a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16).** A [loop](concepts.md#c31) is a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) only if, under some [cut](concepts.md#c23), it satisfies the definition of [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16): all the [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) that [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at it takes part in its [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) for its next [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09), and from this [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) on there is still at least one [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) at which it is not [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12). A [loop](concepts.md#c31) is a shape; [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) are the ones among them that qualify.
- **Scale is not fixed.** By [granularity](concepts.md#c20) (Section 1), when a stretch of the [universe](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c02) is cut as one [loop](concepts.md#c31) or as many nested [loops](concepts.md#c31), the corresponding [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) agree if the outcome projections at those levels satisfy the compatibility condition stated by [Granularity](concepts.md#c20). A neuron and a civilization can each be a [loop](concepts.md#c31).

To describe a [loop](concepts.md#c31), one must cut out parts and say what [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at what. This is a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) made by the analyst and cannot be avoided. This paper allows [individuals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) to be used in description, but does not allow inference to rely on [individuals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20): an [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) is only a provisional name, with no fixed core. Apart from [impression](concepts.md#c45), the conclusions of every tool must survive [recutting](concepts.md#c22); this requirement applies only to [recuttings](concepts.md#c22) that satisfy the outcome-projection compatibility condition stated by [Granularity](concepts.md#c20).

More precisely: under [recutting](concepts.md#c22) that satisfies the [Granularity](concepts.md#c20) compatibility condition, [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) and inferences do not change; tools that describe relations between [loops](concepts.md#c31) ([coupling](concepts.md#c36), [taiji](concepts.md#c39), [impression](concepts.md#c45)) turn into internal structure when parts are merged, and no longer appear as relations. An extreme [cut](concepts.md#c23) is to cut the whole [universe](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c02) as a single [loop](concepts.md#c31). This [cut](concepts.md#c23) is legitimate in itself, but it belongs to the [recuttings](concepts.md#c22) for which this paper requires invariant [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) and inferences only when it and the original [cut](concepts.md#c23) satisfy the outcome-projection compatibility condition stated by [Granularity](concepts.md#c20). In that case there is no second [loop](concepts.md#c31) outside the [universe](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c02), so [coupling](concepts.md#c36), [taiji](concepts.md#c39) and [impression](concepts.md#c45) are left only as internal structure.

The following two things remain [distinctions](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18):

- [Cut](concepts.md#c23): which stretch of the [universe](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c02) is taken as one [loop](concepts.md#c31).
- [Identity](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c21): whether the [Identity](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c21) relation holds between [loops](concepts.md#c31) at different [moments](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09).

By the definition of [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), once the [cut](concepts.md#c23) is stated, whether something is a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) is not decided by [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18). So a [loop](concepts.md#c31) in this paper always means "a [loop](concepts.md#c31) under a stated [cut](concepts.md#c23)", and persistence across [moments](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) is [Not Guaranteed](concepts.md#c02).

The only tool that can be stated only by marking off "that is him" is [impression](concepts.md#c45).

## 3. Notation

- [Boundary](concepts.md#c24): every example must first fix a geographical [boundary](concepts.md#c24) and a temporal [boundary](concepts.md#c24). This is a [cut](concepts.md#c23) and must be stated.
- [Node](concepts.md#c25): a [presentation](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04) under a [cut](concepts.md#c23); it is a provisional name. There are three kinds of [node](concepts.md#c25):
  - [Wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16): its own [output](concepts.md#c28) comes back to itself, and it is subject to [cognitive obscuration](concepts.md#c35); when it is not [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12), only its [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) can be discussed.
  - [Signal Container](concepts.md#c26): a [node](concepts.md#c25) that is not a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) and, from this [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) on, is [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12) at every step. It usually persists and [outputs](concepts.md#c28) to [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) at many [moments](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09), but this is not a condition. Its content may be written by [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), as with books or statutes, or may come from other [presentations](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04), as with observations brought by a telescope. [Cognitive obscuration](concepts.md#c35) has no content for it; nor can it be the object of an [impression](concepts.md#c45): reading a book is an [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10), not an [impression](concepts.md#c45).
  - [Random Source](concepts.md#c27): not a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), but at this [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) or later there is still at least one [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) at which it is not [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12). By the definition of [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), not all the [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) that [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at it takes part in its [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06): either not all of it takes part now, or not all of it takes part at the [moments](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) at which it is not [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12). Examples are a thrown die and a radioactively decaying atom. When it is not [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12), only its [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) can be discussed; nor can it be the object of an [impression](concepts.md#c45).
- [Output](concepts.md#c28): the outcomes of a [presentation](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04)'s [collapses](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c08), together with the content of its [distinctions](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18).
- [Edge](concepts.md#c29): directed; it means that the [output](concepts.md#c28) of one [node](concepts.md#c25) takes part in the [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) of another [node](concepts.md#c25). An [edge](concepts.md#c29) carries no magnitude of its own: how much an [edge](concepts.md#c29) matters depends on the receiver's [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13), not on the [edge](concepts.md#c29). In the examples, [edges](concepts.md#c29) are drawn only for direct [arrivals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10).
- [Timing](concepts.md#c30): [edges](concepts.md#c29) may be marked with their order and with how long it takes to go round once. [Nodes](concepts.md#c25) may appear or disappear at some time within the [boundary](concepts.md#c24).

```text
Formal:
Out(x) = {position of the outcome of collapse k : k ∈ T(x)} ∪ {position of D(x, k)}
Edge(x, y, n) ⟺ ∃ p ∈ Out(x) with p ∈ Own(y, n)
```

Whether an [edge](concepts.md#c29) is direct or indirect depends on the [cut](concepts.md#c23).

## 4. Tools

### 1. Loop (cybernetics: feedback loop)

**Definition**: a [loop](concepts.md#c31) is a structure in which the [output](concepts.md#c28) of a [presentation](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04), through itself or through other [presentations](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04), takes part again in its own [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06).

- [Positive Feedback](concepts.md#c32): pushes the [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) toward the direction that produced it.
- [Negative Feedback](concepts.md#c33): pushes the [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) away from the direction that produced it.
- [Loop Speed](concepts.md#c34): how long it takes to go round once. [Loop speed](concepts.md#c34) is measured in [clock time](concepts.md#c21), which differs from [moments](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) (see the premises in Section 1).

The direction must contain the [loop](concepts.md#c31)'s own [output](concepts.md#c28) in question, which anchors it; it is not chosen freely by the analyst. How coarsely the direction is drawn belongs to the [cut](concepts.md#c23) and must be stated.

**A point to keep clear**: resistance met by a [loop](concepts.md#c31), if caused by its own [output](concepts.md#c28) coming back round, is [negative feedback](concepts.md#c33), as with habituation, saturation or resource depletion; if it is the [output](concepts.md#c28) of another [loop](concepts.md#c31) pressing in through [coupling](concepts.md#c36), it is not [negative feedback](concepts.md#c33). For this relation between two [positive feedback](concepts.md#c32) [loops](concepts.md#c31) pointing in opposite directions, see [taiji](concepts.md#c39). [Negative feedback](concepts.md#c33) really exists under physical conditions, and cannot be replaced by the [coupling](concepts.md#c36) of two [positive feedbacks](concepts.md#c32).

**Grounds**: the definitions of [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) and [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16).

**Prohibitions**:

- [Not Guaranteed](concepts.md#c02): persistence of a [loop](concepts.md#c31) is [Not Guaranteed](concepts.md#c02): the definition of [loop](concepts.md#c31) contains no condition of persistence; and as long as it is not [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12), every round must also pass through [collapse](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c08).

**Examples**:

- Neurons: repeated firing strengthens synapses ([positive feedback](concepts.md#c32)); responses decline under repeated [stimuli](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c30) ([negative feedback](concepts.md#c33)).
- People: beliefs reinforce themselves ([positive feedback](concepts.md#c32)); what becomes habitual is no longer noticed ([negative feedback](concepts.md#c33)).
- Civilizations: a school's writings train the next generation of scholars ([positive feedback](concepts.md#c32)); resource depletion halts expansion ([negative feedback](concepts.md#c33)).

```text
Formal:
Loop(x, n) ⟺ ∃ p ∈ Out(x), p in H⁺(n), with p ∈ Own(x, n)
Sign: let A ⊆ O_x be a direction, p an outcome of x falling in A, m after p.
  Actual = Dist(Σ_A w(m, x) | Rep(p, actual))
  Control = Dist(Σ_A w(m, x) | Rep(p, a value outside A))
  If for every threshold, P(Actual > threshold) ≥ P(Control > threshold), strictly somewhere,
    positive feedback;
  if the reverse, negative feedback;
  otherwise, neither.
```

### 2. Cognitive Obscuration

**Definition**: [cognitive obscuration](concepts.md#c35) is the fact that the [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) of the outcomes of a [presentation](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04) at its next [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) sum to one, so that pushing one direction up necessarily pushes the total of the other directions down. All learning has a cost; knowing and obscuration are one and the same.

**Grounds**: the definition of [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06): the [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) of the [branches](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c05) of a [collapse](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c08) sum to one, and the [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) of a [presentation](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04) are divided out of them, so they also sum to one.

**Note**: [cognitive obscuration](concepts.md#c35) guarantees only that one rises as the others fall; it does not decide which direction an [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) pushes up, or by how much (see Section 5, Rule 1).

**Prohibitions**:

- [Never occurs](concepts.md#c01): one side is pushed up while the total of the others does not fall.

**Examples**:

- Cells: when the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) of one mode of response of an immune cell to an antigen rises, the total [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) of its other modes of response necessarily falls.
- People: after learning to read, on seeing a word, the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) of "reading it" rises and the [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) of the other directions necessarily fall (the Stroop effect: the meaning of a word interferes with naming its color). This step is [cognitive obscuration](concepts.md#c35). "Being unable to go back to illiteracy" comes from the [Law of Non-Restoration](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c28): the occurrence again of the self that could not yet read [Never Occurs](concepts.md#c01). "Finding it hard to understand the illiterate" is a matter of [impression](concepts.md#c45): that experience is still in [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) (Section 1: [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) loses nothing), but the [impression](concepts.md#c45) of it is formed with the present [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06), and explaining why acting that way made sense back then is [Not Guaranteed](concepts.md#c02). When the object is another illiterate person, it is an [impression](concepts.md#c45); when it is one's own past self, it is a [self-impression](concepts.md#c46); the reasoning is the same. Neither of the latter two is derived from [cognitive obscuration](concepts.md#c35); of them, "completely unable" is an extreme concentration and belongs to empirical description.
- Civilizations: when the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) of one institutional arrangement rises, the total [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) of the other arrangements necessarily falls.

[Cognitive obscuration](concepts.md#c35) concerns only the distribution of the [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) of one [presentation](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04) at its next [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09). Trade-offs between different environments (such as the fitness cost of drug resistance), the size of an effect, and the range of generalization are not derived from [cognitive obscuration](concepts.md#c35); they are empirical assumptions.

```text
Formal:
Let A ⊆ O_x be a direction.
Σ_A w(n, x) + Σ_{O_x∖A} w(n, x) = 1
```

### 3. Coupling (cybernetics: coupling)

**Definition**: [coupling](concepts.md#c36) is the situation in which the [own segments](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c17) of two or more [presentations](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04) overlap in a stated [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10). What takes part in [coupling](concepts.md#c36) may be [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), or [loops](concepts.md#c31) made of many [presentations](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04) (see [taiji](concepts.md#c39)). The overlap takes one of two forms: the same thing [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at all of them, as with a shared scripture or the [initial state of the universe](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c14); or their [outputs](concepts.md#c28) [arrive](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at one another, as with two civilizations at war.

[Coupling](concepts.md#c36) is always relative to a stated [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10). By the definition of the [initial state of the universe](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c14), the [initial state of the universe](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c14) [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at every [presentation](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04), so any two [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) overlap at this point; to say that two [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) are [coupled](concepts.md#c36) without stating which [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) is meant says nothing.

**Directions of development**: under [coupling](concepts.md#c36), the patterns of [outputs](concepts.md#c28) along any one dimension have only three directions of development:

- [Convergence](concepts.md#c37) (resonance): the distance between patterns shrinks.
- [Divergence](concepts.md#c38) (differentiation; Bateson: schismogenesis): the distance between patterns grows.
- [Stasis](concepts.md#c52): the distance between patterns stays the same.

**High dimensionality**: patterns are high-dimensional. The same stretch of [coupling](concepts.md#c36) can show [convergence](concepts.md#c37) along one dimension and [divergence](concepts.md#c38) along another at the same time. So to speak of [convergence](concepts.md#c37), [divergence](concepts.md#c38) or [stasis](concepts.md#c52), one must state which dimension is meant. Which dimension to choose is the analyst's [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18).

**Grounds**: the definition of [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10); the definition of [own segment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c17) (the [own segments](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c17) of different [presentations](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04) may overlap); [cognitive obscuration](concepts.md#c35); the [Law of the Black Box](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c25).

**Prohibitions**:

- [Not Guaranteed](concepts.md#c02): the occurrence under [coupling](concepts.md#c36) of [convergence](concepts.md#c37), [divergence](concepts.md#c38), or [stasis](concepts.md#c52) along any dimension is [Not Guaranteed](concepts.md#c02): the definition of [coupling](concepts.md#c36) says only that [own segments](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c17) overlap in an [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10), and contains no direction of development; moreover, when the participating [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) are not [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12), the outcomes of their next [moments](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) cannot be derived ([Law of the Black Box](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c25)). Both "exchange brings understanding" and "contact inevitably leads to conflict" are prohibited.
- [Not Guaranteed](concepts.md#c02): the same results following [convergence](concepts.md#c37) are [Not Guaranteed](concepts.md#c02): the same [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) is only one item in each one's [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13), and the results are determined by each one's whole [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) (the definition of the [generating function](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c15)); moreover, when each [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) is not [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12), its next [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) cannot be derived ([Law of the Black Box](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c25)).

**Examples**:

- Neurons: two populations of neurons oscillate in synchrony ([convergence](concepts.md#c37)); lateral inhibition drives the responses of neighboring neurons ever further apart ([divergence](concepts.md#c38)).
- People: a married couple grows more and more alike ([convergence](concepts.md#c37)); brothers go their separate ways after dividing the family estate ([divergence](concepts.md#c38)).
- Civilizations: in more than a thousand years of contact, Christianity and Islam showed [divergence](concepts.md#c38) in faith and identity, and [convergence](concepts.md#c37) in philosophy, mathematics and trade.

Formal:

MutCoup(a, b, n) ⟺ ∃ p_a ∈ Out(a) ∩ Arr(b, n) with p_a ∈ Own(b, n),
  and ∃ p_b ∈ Out(b) ∩ Arr(a, n) with p_b ∈ Own(a, n)

#### Corollary: Taiji

**Definition**: [taiji](concepts.md#c39) is a structure in which two [positive feedback](concepts.md#c32) [loops](concepts.md#c31) pointing in opposite directions are [coupled](concepts.md#c36) with each other and show [divergence](concepts.md#c38) within that [coupling](concepts.md#c36), forming a dynamic balance. Each side's [output](concepts.md#c28), [arriving](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at the other, strengthens the other's own direction, so whichever side grows is feeding its opponent.

[Taiji](concepts.md#c39) takes place between one [loop](concepts.md#c31) and another [loop](concepts.md#c31); it is not the rise and fall of the [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) of a single [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16). It is a structure of [loops](concepts.md#c31), and does not require every participant to be a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16).

"Opposite directions" can be judged only after choosing a dimension and matching the [outputs](concepts.md#c28) of the two [loops](concepts.md#c31) to each other; this is a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18). So to say that two [loops](concepts.md#c31) form a [taiji](concepts.md#c39), one must state along which dimension.

**Nesting**: [taiji](concepts.md#c39) can be nested. One side of a [taiji](concepts.md#c39) can contain another [taiji](concepts.md#c39) within it; which level one looks at is decided by the [cut](concepts.md#c23). For example, royal power and ecclesiastical power within medieval Europe form one [taiji](concepts.md#c39), while the whole of Christendom and Islam form another [taiji](concepts.md#c39), the former nested within the latter.

**Ways of ending**: there are at least two:

- [Withdrawal of One Side](concepts.md#c40): most of the [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) on one side [exit](concepts.md#c43) (see [renewal](concepts.md#c42)), or that side no longer reinforces itself.
- [Merging](concepts.md#c41): the [coupling](concepts.md#c36) turns from [divergence](concepts.md#c38) to [convergence](concepts.md#c37), and the two [loops](concepts.md#c31) become one. Under [recutting](concepts.md#c22), this can also be seen as the two [loops](concepts.md#c31) being cut as one.

**Prohibitions**:

- [Not Guaranteed](concepts.md#c02): the ending of a [taiji](concepts.md#c39) is [Not Guaranteed](concepts.md#c02); when it does end, which side disappears or whether the two sides [merge](concepts.md#c41) is also [Not Guaranteed](concepts.md#c02): the definition of [taiji](concepts.md#c39) contains no condition for ending; moreover, as long as there are [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) among them that are not [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12), the outcomes of both sides' next [moments](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) cannot be derived ([Law of the Black Box](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c25)).
- "One side disappears" must be judged under some [cut](concepts.md#c23); it is a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18).

**Examples**:

- Cells: tumors and the immune system. The theory of immunoediting distinguishes three phases, elimination, equilibrium and escape; the equilibrium phase can last for years.
- Artificial systems: generative adversarial networks. The generator and the discriminator point in opposite directions, and each one's [output](concepts.md#c28) is the other's training material; when training fails, one side overwhelms the other.
- Civilizations: royal power and ecclesiastical power in medieval Europe. The Investiture Controversy, the bull *Unam Sanctam* of 1302 and the Anagni incident of the following year: each side's claims fueled the other's. The two sides showed [divergence](concepts.md#c38) over who held authority, yet depended on each other for legitimacy and protection, so the balance could last for centuries. In England, the Act of Supremacy of 1534 made the king the head of the church, an ending by [merging](concepts.md#c41).

```text
Formal:
Loop(a, n) ∧ Loop(b, n) ∧ MutCoup(a, b, n)
```

The formal passage gives only the structural premises of [taiji](concepts.md#c39); opposite directions, [divergence](concepts.md#c38), dynamic balance and the ways of ending all require choosing a dimension and comparing [outputs](concepts.md#c28); this is a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18), and is not formalized.

### 4. Renewal (Ryder: demographic metabolism; Planck's principle)

**Definition**: [renewal](concepts.md#c42) is the going and coming of [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16): some [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) cease to be [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), and some [presentations](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04) begin to be [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16).

- [Exit](concepts.md#c43): a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) ceases to be a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16): it is a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) at one [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) and is not a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) at its next [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09). By the definition of [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13), the [own segments](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c17) of [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) that have [exited](concepts.md#c43) remain in [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) (Section 1: [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) loses nothing); the [outputs](concepts.md#c28) they left behind, such as texts, institutions and buildings, can still [arrive](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at later [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) through [signal containers](concepts.md#c26).
- [Entry](concepts.md#c44): a [presentation](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04) begins to be a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16): it is not a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) at one [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) and is a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) at its next [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09), or it is already a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) at its first [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09). The [own segments](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c17) of those that have [entered](concepts.md#c44) overlap with those of the old [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), for example in the parts that come from parents, teachers and texts.

**Grounds**: the definition of [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) (participation may fade away; if from some [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) on it is [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12) at every step, it is no longer a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) either), the [Law of Flux](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c24), the [Law of Non-Restoration](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c28), and the definition of [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13).

By the [Law of Flux](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c24), at each [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) of a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), the [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) that [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at it differs from that at its previous [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09). For the frozen-insect example, assume that freezing and thawing each correspond to a [collapse](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c08) that [involves](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c11) the insect, and that the insect is a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) at both of those [moments](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09). If no other [collapse](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c08) [involves](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c11) it between them, then there is no new [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) for it in between, and thawing is its next [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) after freezing. By the [Law of Flux](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c24), the [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) that [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at it at thawing differs from the [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) that [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at it at freezing. Therefore freezing and then thawing alone does not guarantee the same response as before freezing; the same response is [Not Guaranteed](concepts.md#c02), but this does not mean that a different response is necessary. In this case freezing is not an [exit](concepts.md#c43) either: the insect is a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) at both freezing and thawing, and thawing is its next [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) after freezing, so it does not change from a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) into a non-[wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16). Freezing cannot fix [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) at its pre-freezing content, nor can thawing be understood as returning to the original [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09); whether an observable state or response happens to be the same is not guaranteed by the framework. By the [Law of Non-Restoration](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c28), the [own segment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c17) of what has [exited](concepts.md#c43) happening again [Never Occurs](concepts.md#c01). Only the two together are the full grounds of [renewal](concepts.md#c42): the former says [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) keeps being appended, while the latter says a [Restoration](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c27) cannot be made. The [own segment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c17) of one who [enters](concepts.md#c44) may overlap that of an old [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16); different [individuals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) may share the same prior history before they separate, after which new [arrivals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) and [writings](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c29) can make their paths of dependence diverge, and a later merging can let a new [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) jointly inherit the [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) left by previously different paths. Overlap, divergence and merging do not by themselves determine [Identity](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c21); whether they stand in [Identity](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c21) is a [Distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18).

**Prohibitions**:

- [Never occurs](concepts.md#c01): the [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) in which a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) lies stops changing after some [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09). "The tradition froze" and "the institution stood still" are statements of this kind; one can only say that the [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) are highly concentrated. What is ever new is [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13); what has nothing new is [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) (the premises in Section 1).
- [Not Guaranteed](concepts.md#c02): the old [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) making again the choices they made while not [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12) is [Not Guaranteed](concepts.md#c02). So the [convergence](concepts.md#c37) within a [coupling](concepts.md#c36), once the participating [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) have gone through [renewal](concepts.md#c42), continuing as it was is also [Not Guaranteed](concepts.md#c02): the definition of [convergence](concepts.md#c37) contains no condition for continuing, and the choices that sustained it being made again is likewise [Not Guaranteed](concepts.md#c02).
- This paper does not assert that any [loop](concepts.md#c31), nation or culture must disappear. Disappearance and extinction are labels, and belong to [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18). What the framework can derive is that the [universe](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c02) has only one [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13), and it keeps being appended at every [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09); therefore, when different global [moments](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) are compared, the content of [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) differs. This does not mean that each [loop](concepts.md#c31), nation, or culture has a separate [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13), nor does it decide whether it has become something different; whether to say so, and whether to call that extinction, is a matter of [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18).

**Note**: "as it was" must be judged under some [cut](concepts.md#c23). At a coarse enough scale, a tradition may look unchanged; an application must state the scale of judgment.

**Examples**:

- Cells: cell turnover in tissues.
- People: old habits fading and new habits forming over a lifetime.
- Civilizations: generational succession. In the late Qing dynasty, scholars who had spent their lives immersed in the imperial examinations, faced with an [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) as high in [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49) as science, tended to explain it away rather than be overturned by it (the empirical assumption of Explicit Rule 1: the more concentrated the receiver's [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13), the more likely a shock is explained away). The Qing court undertook [renewal](concepts.md#c42): from 1872 it sent young students to study in the United States, so that a group of new [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) without this [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) would receive science; in 1905 it abolished the imperial examinations, cutting off the old [loop](concepts.md#c31)'s source of new [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16).

**[Cognitive Obscuration](concepts.md#c35) and [Renewal](concepts.md#c42)**. [Cognitive obscuration](concepts.md#c35) is not an obstacle that needs to be "undone"; it is the structural tradeoff that follows when the total [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) is one. When the [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) are redistributed, the configuration of knowing and obscuring changes with them, while the new configuration remains subject to the same structure. [Renewal](concepts.md#c42) only describes the going and coming of [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16); this paper does not designate [renewal](concepts.md#c42) as a special mechanism for handling [cognitive obscuration](concepts.md#c35), nor does it compare the effectiveness of [renewal](concepts.md#c42) with other ways of change.

```text
Formal:
prev(x, n) = max{k ∈ T(x) : k < n}, when it exists
Exit(x, n) ⟺ n ∈ T(x) ∧ prev(x, n) exists ∧ Wo(x, prev(x, n)) ∧ ¬Wo(x, n)
Entry(x, n) ⟺ n ∈ T(x) ∧ Wo(x, n) ∧ (prev(x, n) undefined ∨ ¬Wo(x, prev(x, n)))
```

"Continuing as it was" requires taking those who have [entered](concepts.md#c44) and those who have [exited](concepts.md#c43) to stand in [identity](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c21); this is a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18), and is not formalized.

### 5. Impression (theory of mind; good regulator theorem)

**Definition**: an [impression](concepts.md#c45) is a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) made by a [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) of a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), that is, its estimate of that [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16)'s [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06). The object may be another [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), or the [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) itself, in which case it is called a [self-impression](concepts.md#c46). Every statistical estimate about a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), including statistics of historical records, is an [impression](concepts.md#c45).

**Grounds**: the definitions of [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) and [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19), the definition of [writing](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c29) (a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) leaves a [writing](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c29) in [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13)), the [Law of the Black Box](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c25), the [Law of Attrition](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c26), and the [Incompleteness of Knowing Others](concepts.md#c19).

**[Self-impression](concepts.md#c46)**: by the [Law of Attrition](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c26), the material of a [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)'s [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) of itself does not include that [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) itself, so it always lags one step behind; it cannot finish [distinguishing](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) even its own [own segment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c17). So a [self-impression](concepts.md#c46), like an [impression](concepts.md#c45) of others, is an estimate, can only be corrected through [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10), and is incomplete at every [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09). The [Incompleteness of Knowing Others](concepts.md#c19) and the [Law of Attrition](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c26) are the same structure: the former directed at others, the latter at oneself.

**Material** comes from three sources:

1. the other's past [outputs](concepts.md#c28);
2. the part of the [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)'s own [own segment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c17) that overlaps with the other's (putting oneself in another's place);
3. the [couplings](concepts.md#c36) the other is in, such as roles, institutions and beliefs.

**Properties**:

- An [impression](concepts.md#c45) can only be corrected through [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10): the material for correction must come from [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) that has already [arrived](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10). It may be a newly [arriving](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) [output](concepts.md#c28); or, when such material exists, one may go back to [distinguish](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) that has long since [arrived](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) but has not yet been [distinguished](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18). The existence of material of the latter kind in every case is [Not Guaranteed](concepts.md#c02); this paper also does not assert that an [impression](concepts.md#c45) must change after such a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18).
- By the definition of [collapse](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c08), a single [output](concepts.md#c28) is only one outcome settled according to the [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06), not the complete [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) distribution. This paper adds no assumption that a complete distribution can be inverted from a single outcome, so it does not use a single [output](concepts.md#c28) to determine the complete shape of the [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06). How much several [outputs](concepts.md#c28) taken together can show depends on whether the shapes of the [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) at those [moments](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) are similar (the premises in Section 1; whether this holds is an empirical assumption).
- Merely accumulating on the same judgment, with neither a new [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) nor a new [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) of [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) that has already [arrived](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10), is not a correction; improving calibration is [Not Guaranteed](concepts.md#c02).
- An [impression](concepts.md#c45), as a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18), leaves a [writing](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c29) in [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13); once this [writing](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c29) [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at the other, it may itself change the other (see "Derived from the definitions" in Section 1); when the other's changed [output](concepts.md#c28) [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) back at this [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19), the [impression](concepts.md#c45) forms a [loop](concepts.md#c31).
- This paper takes anything a [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) says as one of its [outputs](concepts.md#c28); when what is said is about some object, its content is a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18), determined by the [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) up to that point: when it speaks of itself, this is a [self-impression](concepts.md#c46); when it speaks of another [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), it is an [impression](concepts.md#c45). So what is said cannot be taken as a complete description of what it is about: of oneself, the [self-impression](concepts.md#c46) always lags one step behind ([Law of Attrition](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c26)); of others, the completeness of the [impression](concepts.md#c45) is [Not Guaranteed](concepts.md#c02) ([Incompleteness of Knowing Others](concepts.md#c19)).
- The accuracy of an [impression](concepts.md#c45) should be measured by calibration, that is, by whether the [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) bet on are right, not by hit rate.

**Prohibitions**:

- [Never occurs](concepts.md#c01): for a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) that is not [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12), no [impression](concepts.md#c45) can uniquely derive its next [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) ([Law of the Black Box](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c25)). The same holds when the object is oneself: before the choice, even it does not know.
- [Never occurs](concepts.md#c01): a [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) completely [distinguishes](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) itself at some [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) ([Law of Attrition](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c26)).
- [Not guaranteed](concepts.md#c02): no [impression](concepts.md#c45) of another can be guaranteed to be complete ([Incompleteness of Knowing Others](concepts.md#c19)).

**Note**: this is the only tool in this paper that requires [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18), so it does not survive [recutting](concepts.md#c22) across scales: cells cannot serve as an example. Whether an AI in conversation has [impressions](concepts.md#c45) depends on whether it is a [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19); whether it is a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) is currently undecided (see Section 5, Rule 5).

**Examples**: a person's understanding of a friend; one nation's judgment of another; the church regarding a school as heretical; a person looking back on why they made a certain decision.

Material ⊆ Arr(x, n)

## 5. Explicit Rules

1. **This paper does not decide the [impact](concepts.md#c47) of an [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10).** How much [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) an [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) can move is called its [impact](concepts.md#c47), and it is a matter internal to the [generating function](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c15). An application must state the assumptions it bets on and mark them as empirical assumptions. At least two must be stated:
   - [Frequency](concepts.md#c48): how often it [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10).
   - [Discrepancy](concepts.md#c49) (information theory: surprisal): how far this [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) lies from what the receiver has received before. [Discrepancy](concepts.md#c49) is relative to the receiver's [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13): the same [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10), falling on different [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), can have entirely different [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49).

   The examples in this paper adopt the following empirical assumptions: repeated [arrivals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) of low [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49) have diminishing effects; a rare [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) of high [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49) can, in one go, outweigh a long accumulation; the more concentrated the receiver's [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13), the more likely a shock of high [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49) is explained away rather than overturning it.

   [Impacts](concepts.md#c47) do not add up: a hundred small earthquakes are not one large earthquake. This agrees with the definition of the [generating function](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c15): [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) are a function of the whole [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13), and the effect of an [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) depends on the [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) before it.

2. **Statistics belong to [impression](concepts.md#c45).** Historical records can be used to estimate the [impact](concepts.md#c47) of [arrivals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10). For such estimates to be meaningful, the shape of [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) must actually repeat (the premises in Section 1); whether this holds in a given field is an empirical assumption. The records themselves are filtered [outputs](concepts.md#c28); before doing statistics, one must first ask who left them.

3. **What is derived to be impossible must be kept apart from what has not occurred.** The former holds across time; the latter is only a [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06), and may be overturned by an [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) of high [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49).

4. **Formalization stops at structure.** What this paper formalizes are relations: [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10), participation, one rising as another falls, overlap. What it does not formalize is content: [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49), the dimensions of [convergence](concepts.md#c37) and [divergence](concepts.md#c38), [impression](concepts.md#c45), the shape of the [generating function](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c15). This paper does not decide the latter; they can only be described qualitatively in the application papers and marked as empirical assumptions.

5. **[Application assumption](concepts.md#c50).** The examples in this paper all adopt the following [application assumption](concepts.md#c50): at the levels of biology and the social sciences, if the next [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) of a [presentation](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04) cannot be uniquely derived from the [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) available at that level, it is assumed not to be [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12). It is further assumed that, for every [presentation](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04) listed as a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) in the examples, the next [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) cannot be uniquely derived from the [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) available at that level. This is not a theorem; whether humans and AI are not [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12) is currently undecided for both. If a [presentation](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04) is [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12) for some period, as with coerced behavior, the rule "only [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) can be discussed" does not apply to that period.

6. **Whatever can be expressed statistically is not expressed teleologically.** A purpose belongs to a [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)'s [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) of itself; only a [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) has one. For a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) that is not a [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19), write only statistical changes, such as "drug-resistant strains have a higher survival rate in environments with the drug", not "the bacteria developed resistance in order to survive". For a [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19), purposes may be written, but they still affect only [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06): one may be hungry and still not eat. A purpose stated by a [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) is also something it says (see the properties of [impression](concepts.md#c45) in Section 4): as a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) whose object is itself, it is a [self-impression](concepts.md#c46), and as something said, it is one [output](concepts.md#c28). The writing rule of this paper is: from that self-report alone, do not write "what he says he did it for" as a complete explanation of his behavior. This rule does not assert that a stated purpose can never constitute a complete explanation in any particular case; whether it is complete in a particular case is not decided by this paper.

7. **[Empirical prohibition](concepts.md#c51).** A necessary condition distilled from cases is called an [empirical prohibition](concepts.md#c51): without a certain condition, a certain outcome [never occurs](concepts.md#c01). It is not derived from the framework but obtained by calibration across cases, so it must be marked as such, and may be revised by new cases. It is stronger than mere "has not occurred", because it has been tested across cases; but it is still not a derived impossibility (see Rule 3). State only necessary conditions, never sufficient ones: even when all the conditions are met, one can only say that the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) of that outcome has become heavier, not that it must happen.

| [Impression](concepts.md#c45) | Uniquely deriving the next [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) of a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) that is not [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12), including one's own ([Law of the Black Box](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c25)) | [Never Occurs](concepts.md#c01) |
| [Impression](concepts.md#c45) | A [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) knows itself completely ([Law of Attrition](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c26)) | [Never Occurs](concepts.md#c01) |
| [Impression](concepts.md#c45) | Completely understanding another [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) ([Incompleteness of Knowing Others](concepts.md#c19)) | [Not Guaranteed](concepts.md#c02) |

The prohibitions above hold for the past and for the future alike.

### 2. Prohibitions on statements

These say how the analyst may not infer or write. Their form is always "methodological prohibition".

| Source | Prohibited statement |
|---|---|
| Basic unit | Explaining an outcome by the essence of a person or group (an [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) has no fixed core) |
| Basic unit | A conclusion changes with the [cut](concepts.md#c23), yet the [cut](concepts.md#c23) is not stated |
| [Renewal](concepts.md#c42) | Asserting that a nation or culture must become extinct (extinction is a label; it is a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)) |
| [Impression](concepts.md#c45) | Writing a prediction about a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) that is not [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12) as an outcome rather than as [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) |
| [Impression](concepts.md#c45) | Taking what a [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) says as a complete description of what it is about |
| Explicit rules | From a purpose stated by a [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) alone, writing it as a complete explanation of the behavior |
| [Coupling](concepts.md#c36), [taiji](concepts.md#c39) | Speaking of [convergence](concepts.md#c37), [divergence](concepts.md#c38) or opposite directions without stating the dimension |
| Explicit rules | Treating "has not occurred" as "impossible" |
| Explicit rules | Treating the [impacts](concepts.md#c47) of [arrivals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) as additive |
| Explicit rules | Writing an [empirical prohibition](concepts.md#c51) as a sufficient condition, or as a derived impossibility |

These are distilled from the examples; see "Conditions and reuse" in each example. They are revised with new cases, so they are not listed here.

## 7. Format of Examples

Examples are qualitative analyses and need no data. They are written in the following seven steps, each of which must produce something.

1. **[Boundary](concepts.md#c24)**: state the geographical [boundary](concepts.md#c24) and the temporal [boundary](concepts.md#c24).
2. **Initial state**: list the [nodes](concepts.md#c25), marking whether each [node](concepts.md#c25) is a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), a [signal container](concepts.md#c26) or a [random source](concepts.md#c27); describe the [loops](concepts.md#c31) and [couplings](concepts.md#c36) they are in, and along which dimensions they show [convergence](concepts.md#c37) or [divergence](concepts.md#c38).
3. **New [nodes](concepts.md#c25) appear**: state when the new [nodes](concepts.md#c25) appear, what they bring that was not there before, and what conflict they cause.
4. **Changes in [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06)**: state how the new and old [nodes](concepts.md#c25) each move; which [loops](concepts.md#c31) are fast and which are slow; whether there is a [taiji](concepts.md#c39); and the assumptions about [impact](concepts.md#c47) bet on ([frequency](concepts.md#c48), [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49)).
5. **Outcome**: state the outcome, and the scale at which it is judged.
6. **Inferences, historical facts, empirical assumptions and prohibitions**: mark item by item which are derived from the framework, which are historical facts (used only for calibration), which are empirical assumptions, and which statements are prohibited in this example.
7. **Conditions and reuse**: distill [empirical prohibitions](concepts.md#c51) from the example, that is, necessary conditions for some outcome, and test them against other cases. State only necessary conditions, never sufficient ones. Agreement strengthens the [empirical prohibition](concepts.md#c51); deviation means either the conditions were matched wrongly or the prohibition must be revised (see Explicit Rule 7).

## 8. Example: Heliocentrism Replacing Geocentrism

### 1. Boundary

- Geography: the European learned world, chiefly Italy, Central Europe and England.
- Time: from 1543 to around 1700.

### 2. Initial state (before 1543)

**[Nodes](concepts.md#c25)**:

- [Wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16): the older generation of astronomers, the universities, the church.
- [Signal container](concepts.md#c26): the Ptolemaic system (chiefly the writings of Aristotle and Ptolemy).

**State**: the Ptolemaic system [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at all three [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), forming a [coupling](concepts.md#c36), and the three show [convergence](concepts.md#c37) in astronomical ideas. The universities teach it to students, the students become scholars and return to teach in the universities, forming a [positive feedback](concepts.md#c32) [loop](concepts.md#c31). This [loop](concepts.md#c31) takes a generation to go round once. There is only one theory in the picture, and no conflict.

### 3. New nodes appear

- **1543, *De revolutionibus*** ([signal container](concepts.md#c26)) appears and [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at a few scholars, forming a small [positive feedback](concepts.md#c32) [loop](concepts.md#c31). At this point it is only theory against theory: both systems can roughly explain the observations of the time, and there is no conflict over facts.
- **The supernova of 1572 and the great comet of 1577**: one-off [arrivals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) of high [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49), which shake "the heavens do not change".
- **1588, the Tychonic system** ([signal container](concepts.md#c26)) appears: the earth is still, the sun goes round the earth, and the other planets go round the sun. It keeps "the earth is still" while accommodating many new observations.
- **From 1609, the telescope** ([signal container](concepts.md#c26)) appears. It [outputs](concepts.md#c28) facts not previously in the picture, such as the moons of Jupiter and the phases of Venus. The phases of Venus are incompatible with the Ptolemaic system, but compatible with both the Tychonic system and heliocentrism. For the first time the picture contains a conflict between facts and the traditional theory. The telescope does not [output](concepts.md#c28) once and stop; it keeps [outputting](concepts.md#c28): more and more telescopes, more and more observations.
- **1609 and 1619, Kepler's laws of planetary motion; 1627, the *Rudolphine Tables*** ([signal container](concepts.md#c26)): tables computed from elliptical orbits, with predictive accuracy clearly higher than before, tested at every observation thereafter; for example, the transit of Mercury of 1631 predicted by them was observed as expected.

### 4. Changes in weight

- **The Ptolemaic [loop](concepts.md#c31) is broken**: the phases of Venus are an [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) of high [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49). After the 1610s, the pure Ptolemaic system quickly lost ground among astronomers. But many [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) originally in the Ptolemaic [loop](concepts.md#c31) did not turn to heliocentrism; they moved into the Tychonic system and kept "the earth is still". How far each moved depended on each one's [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13), a question of [impact](concepts.md#c47) (empirical assumption).
- **[Taiji](concepts.md#c39)**: the real standoff of the following decades was between the Tychonic [loop](concepts.md#c31) and the heliocentric [loop](concepts.md#c31) (the side of Kepler and Galileo). The two point in opposite directions along the dimension "does the earth move?", and are [coupled](concepts.md#c36) with each other in [divergence](concepts.md#c38). The church's prohibition of 1616 and the trial of 1633 were [outputs](concepts.md#c28) of the geostatic side [arriving](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at the heliocentric [loop](concepts.md#c31); they were not [negative feedback](concepts.md#c33) of the heliocentric [loop](concepts.md#c31).
- **[Loop speed](concepts.md#c34)**: the heliocentric side's [loop](concepts.md#c31) (observation, tables, prediction, observation again) took only a few years to go round once; the traditional [loop](concepts.md#c31) of master and disciple took a generation, as long as the cycle of [renewal](concepts.md#c42), so it could not reinforce itself repeatedly within the same generation.
- **[Cognitive obscuration](concepts.md#c35)**: this example considers only two directions, heliocentric and geostatic; at the time there was no other developed theory. For any newly entering scholar, when the heliocentric [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) rises, the geostatic [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) (including the Tychonic system) necessarily falls.
- **[Renewal](concepts.md#c42)**: newly entering scholars received the [outputs](concepts.md#c28) of both sides at once, along with the continual high-[discrepancy](concepts.md#c49) [outputs](concepts.md#c28) of telescopes and tables, and the new [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) mostly joined the heliocentric [loop](concepts.md#c31).
- **Assumption about [impact](concepts.md#c47)**: this example bets that [frequency](concepts.md#c48) and [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49) both favored the heliocentric side (empirical assumption).

### 5. Outcome

In 1687 the *Principia* appeared, providing the heliocentric side with dynamics, and became the core [signal container](concepts.md#c26) of the new [loop](concepts.md#c31). By around 1700, at the scale of Europe's leading astronomers, the [taiji](concepts.md#c39) ended with the [withdrawal of one side](concepts.md#c40), the geostatic side.

### 6. Inferences, historical facts, empirical assumptions and prohibitions

**Inferences**:

- The [convergence](concepts.md#c37) within the Ptolemaic [loop](concepts.md#c31) and within the Tychonic [loop](concepts.md#c31) continuing as it was is [Not Guaranteed](concepts.md#c02) ([renewal](concepts.md#c42): [Law of Flux](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c24), [Law of Non-Restoration](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c28)).
- When the same observation [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at different scholars, their [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) moving in the same direction is [Not Guaranteed](concepts.md#c02) ([coupling](concepts.md#c36)).
- For a given scholar, when the heliocentric [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) rises, the geostatic [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) necessarily falls ([cognitive obscuration](concepts.md#c35); this example considers only two directions).

**Historical facts (for calibration only)**:

- After the 1610s, the pure Ptolemaic system quickly lost ground, and the main form of the geostatic side became the Tychonic system.
- The [taiji](concepts.md#c39) ended around 1700 with the [withdrawal of one side](concepts.md#c40), the geostatic side.

**Empirical assumptions**:

- The heliocentric side's [loop](concepts.md#c31) was faster than the traditional [loop](concepts.md#c31), and each of its [outputs](concepts.md#c28) was of high [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49), so it prevailed. That a faster [loop](concepts.md#c31) [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) more often at each [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) within the same [clock time](concepts.md#c21) is also an empirical assumption; even if it arrives more often, prevailing is [Not Guaranteed](concepts.md#c02).

**Statements prohibited in this example**:

- "The Ptolemaic system held out until 1700." It was broken after the 1610s; what held out to the end on the geostatic side was the Tychonic system.
- "Heliocentrism replacing geocentrism was inevitable."
- "The old school rejected heliocentrism because it was conservative." This explains an outcome by the essence of a group.
- "After the trial, the new school gave up heliocentrism." One can only say: the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) of the new school publicly supporting heliocentrism became lighter.

### 7. Conditions and reuse

**[Empirical prohibitions](concepts.md#c51) distilled from this example** (necessary conditions only; even when all are met, one can only say that the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) of the [taiji](concepts.md#c39) ending with the [withdrawal of one side](concepts.md#c40), the old side, has become heavier):

1. **[Signal container](concepts.md#c26)**: if the content of the new side does not enter a [signal container](concepts.md#c26), “the new side survives until new [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) join” [Never Occurs](concepts.md#c01).
2. **Sustained [arrivals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) of high [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49)**: if the new side lacks sustained [arrivals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) of high [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49), “the [taiji](concepts.md#c39) ends with the old side’s [withdrawal of one side](concepts.md#c40)” [Never Occurs](concepts.md#c01).
3. **The old side lacks matching [arrivals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)**: if the old side can keep receiving matching [arrivals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) of high [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49), “the [taiji](concepts.md#c39) ends with the old side’s [withdrawal of one side](concepts.md#c40)” [Never Occurs](concepts.md#c01).
4. **[Renewal](concepts.md#c42)** (downgraded; see the revision below): if the [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) of the old side do not [exit](concepts.md#c43) and no new [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) [enter](concepts.md#c44), “the [taiji](concepts.md#c39) ends with the old side’s [withdrawal of one side](concepts.md#c40)” [Never Occurs](concepts.md#c01).

**Reuse tests**:

- **Quantum mechanics (about 1900–1930)**: papers entered a [signal container](concepts.md#c26); [arrivals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) of high [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49) such as black-body radiation, the photoelectric effect and atomic spectra kept appearing; classical physics received no matching new [arrivals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10). Conditions 1 to 3 were all met, and the [taiji](concepts.md#c39) ended in about thirty years. What differed from heliocentrism was the way of ending: classical mechanics did not withdraw, but was absorbed as an approximation of quantum mechanics under particular conditions, an ending by [merging](concepts.md#c41). This does not refute the [empirical prohibitions](concepts.md#c51) above: they only set necessary conditions for "ending with the [withdrawal of one side](concepts.md#c40), the old side", and do not exclude [merging](concepts.md#c41).
- **Plate tectonics (1912–1970s)**: Wegener's hypothesis of continental drift entered a [signal container](concepts.md#c26), so condition 1 was met; but in his lifetime, sustained [arrivals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) of high [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49) were lacking, so condition 2 was not met, and the [taiji](concepts.md#c39) did not end, in agreement with condition 2. In the 1950s and 1960s, evidence from paleomagnetism and seafloor spreading appeared in succession; once condition 2 was met, the [taiji](concepts.md#c39) ended within about ten years.

**Revision**: the [taiji](concepts.md#c39) of plate tectonics ended within about ten years, shorter than a generation, and many established geologists changed their positions in their lifetimes. This weakens condition 4: [renewal](concepts.md#c42) affects how fast a [taiji](concepts.md#c39) ends, but it is not a necessary condition. Condition 4 is therefore downgraded from an [empirical prohibition](concepts.md#c51) to an empirical assumption about [impact](concepts.md#c47).

## 9. Example: Drug Resistance in Staphylococcus aureus

### 1. Boundary

- Geography: the hospital systems of Britain and the United States.
- Time: from the 1940s to the 2000s.

### 2. Initial state (early 1940s)

**[Nodes](concepts.md#c25)**:

- [Wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16): the population of *Staphylococcus aureus*, the community of physicians, the drug research institutions.
- [Signal container](concepts.md#c26): penicillin.

**State**: penicillin comes into large-scale use and [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at the staphylococcal population. At this point the great majority of strains are sensitive to it, and almost every [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) of penicillin pushes the population in the direction of "disappearing". Judging by therapeutic effect, physicians form an [impression](concepts.md#c45) of how the staphylococcal population responds under penicillin: "penicillin is effective against this population", and so use penicillin more often. The object of this [impression](concepts.md#c45) is the staphylococcal population as a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16); penicillin remains a [signal container](concepts.md#c26), not the object of the [impression](concepts.md#c45). This is a [positive feedback](concepts.md#c32) [loop](concepts.md#c31).

### 3. New nodes appear

- **Late 1940s**: strains able to break down penicillin spread in hospitals. They became new [nodes](concepts.md#c25) in the population, bringing something that was not there before: when penicillin [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at them, it no longer causes death.
- **1959**: the research institutions introduce methicillin ([signal container](concepts.md#c26)) to deal with these strains.
- **1961**: strains resistant to methicillin as well appear in Britain, later known as methicillin-resistant *Staphylococcus aureus*.
- **Thereafter**: vancomycin is treated as the last line of defense; in 2002, *Staphylococcus aureus* resistant to vancomycin as well appears in the United States.

### 4. Changes in weight

- **[Taiji](concepts.md#c39)**: the research [loop](concepts.md#c31) (a new drug is launched, used, selects resistant bacteria, and research begins again) and the resistance [loop](concepts.md#c31) (the drug [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10), sensitive bacteria die, resistant bacteria spread) point in opposite directions and are [coupled](concepts.md#c36) with each other, in [divergence](concepts.md#c38) along the dimension "is the drug effective?". Every new drug selects new resistant strains, and every resistant strain forces out a new drug; each side's [outputs](concepts.md#c28) fuel the other.
- **[Arrivals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at the research side** (empirical assumption): within the [boundary](concepts.md#c24) of this example, the bacterial population keeps producing new genetic or phenotypic variation, giving rise to new resistance phenomena; these [arrive](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at the research side as surveillance data, experimental results and research questions. On this basis the research side forms new knowledge, techniques or drug regimens, and launches new drugs. Feedback on the clinical efficacy of the new drugs [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at the research institutions and the community of physicians: relative to their recent [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) of "old drugs failing", this is an [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) of high [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49), and it is enough to restore clinically the direction "the drug is effective", matching the spread of resistant strains. The new drugs in turn become new conditions faced by the bacterial population. Resistance phenomena [arriving](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at the research side show the structure of the [taiji](concepts.md#c39); what sustains the research side's own direction is the feedback on the efficacy of new drugs.
- **[Loop speed](concepts.md#c34)**: a bacterial generation takes only tens of minutes, while a new drug takes more than ten years from research to market. In contrast to heliocentrism, this time the fast side is the bacteria.
- **[Coupling](concepts.md#c36)**: resistance genes can be transferred between different strains, even different species, through mobile genetic elements. The same [output](concepts.md#c28) [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at many [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), bringing them into [convergence](concepts.md#c37) along the dimension of resistance.
- **[Cognitive obscuration](concepts.md#c35)**: as regards the composition of the population at its next [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09), this example considers only two kinds of strains, resistant and sensitive: when the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) held by resistant strains rises, the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) held by sensitive strains necessarily falls.
- **Fitness cost** (empirical): resistance usually has a cost. In environments without the drug, the survival rate of resistant strains is statistically often lower. This is an empirical fact of biology, not derived from [cognitive obscuration](concepts.md#c35).
- **[Renewal](concepts.md#c42)**: each generation of bacteria is a new [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16). They inherit only the genes left by the previous generation, so the composition of the population drifts with every use of the drug.
- **[Impression](concepts.md#c45)**: physicians judge how the staphylococcal population responds under a drug by monitoring statistics; the object of the [impression](concepts.md#c45) remains the staphylococcal population as a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), while the drug is a condition in the content of the judgment. Such estimates keeping up with the actual changes in the population is [Not Guaranteed](concepts.md#c02).
- **Assumption about [impact](concepts.md#c47)**: this example bets on [frequency](concepts.md#c48). The drug is used on a large scale and frequently, and every [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) is selecting (empirical assumption).

### 5. Outcome

By the 2000s, the [taiji](concepts.md#c39) had not ended. The research [loop](concepts.md#c31) and the resistance [loop](concepts.md#c31) were still pushing against each other, while the discovery of new classes of antibiotics had clearly slowed. This is a judgment at the scale of the hospital systems of Britain and the United States.

### 6. Inferences, historical facts, empirical assumptions and prohibitions

**Inferences**:

- Resistance disappearing after the drug is withdrawn or persisting forever is [Not Guaranteed](concepts.md#c02) ([renewal](concepts.md#c42): [Law of Flux](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c24), [Law of Non-Restoration](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c28)).
- When sensitive and resistant strains receive the same drug, the same result is [Not Guaranteed](concepts.md#c02) ([coupling](concepts.md#c36)).
- The completeness of physicians' [impression](concepts.md#c45) of the staphylococcal population's response under a drug is [Not Guaranteed](concepts.md#c02).

**Historical facts (for calibration only)**:

- Methicillin-resistant *Staphylococcus aureus* appeared in 1961; *Staphylococcus aureus* resistant to vancomycin appeared in 2002.
- The [taiji](concepts.md#c39) had still not ended by the 2000s.

**Empirical assumptions**:

- Without the drug, the survival rate of resistant strains is statistically lower, so sensitive strains have a chance to recover after the drug is withdrawn. But resistant strains may mutate again and narrow this gap, so this can only be bet on as [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06).
- The research side keeps launching new drugs, and the feedback on their clinical efficacy is, for the research institutions and the community of physicians, a matching [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) of high [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49) (see step 4). This is stated only for the [boundary](concepts.md#c24) of this example and is not extended beyond it.

**Statements prohibited in this example**:

- "As long as the drug is withdrawn, the sensitive bacteria will come back."
- "Once resistance appears, it will never disappear."
- "Humanity will ultimately defeat bacteria", or "bacteria will ultimately win this war". The ending of a [taiji](concepts.md#c39), and which side disappears, are both [Not Guaranteed](concepts.md#c02).
- "The bacteria developed resistance in order to survive." It should be written: resistant strains have a statistically higher survival rate in environments with the drug (Explicit Rule 6).

### 7. Conditions and reuse

Testing this example against the [empirical prohibitions](concepts.md#c51) distilled from the heliocentrism example, and considering the outcome "the research side's [withdrawal of one side](concepts.md#c40)":

- **Condition 1 ([signal container](concepts.md#c26))**: resistance genes can reside on mobile genetic elements and be transferred between strains; met.
- **Condition 2 (sustained [arrivals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) of high [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49))**: the drug is used on a large scale and frequently, continually selecting the population; met.
- **Condition 3 (the old side lacks matching [arrivals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10))**: the research side keeps launching new drugs, and the feedback on their clinical efficacy [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at the research institutions and the community of physicians; by the empirical assumption in step 4, this is a matching [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) of high [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49). So within the [boundary](concepts.md#c24) of this example, condition 3 is not met.

Within the [boundary](concepts.md#c24) of this example, since condition 3 is not met, “the [taiji](concepts.md#c39) ends with the research side’s [withdrawal of one side](concepts.md#c40)” [Never Occurs](concepts.md#c01), in agreement with the historical facts. The slowing discovery of new classes of antibiotics makes the matching [arrivals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) brought by effective new drugs ever rarer, bringing condition 3 gradually closer to being met; by Explicit Rule 7, one can only say that the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) of this outcome has become heavier, not that it must happen.

This example also shows that [empirical prohibitions](concepts.md#c51) are only necessary conditions: conditions 1 and 2 are both met, yet the [taiji](concepts.md#c39) has still not ended.

### Comparison with the heliocentrism example

Both examples are [taiji](concepts.md#c39) between a fast [loop](concepts.md#c31) and a slow [loop](concepts.md#c31). In heliocentrism the fast one was the [loop](concepts.md#c31) of facts, and the [taiji](concepts.md#c39) ended around 1700; in drug resistance the fast one is the bacteria, and the [taiji](concepts.md#c39) has not ended to this day. The framework guarantees neither outcome; it only marks them as historical facts.
