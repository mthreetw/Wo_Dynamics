# Tools and Examples Related to the Four Laws

## 0. Position

This paper sits between *Wo Dynamics* and the application papers. Its aim is not to propose anything new, but to state what is already known with a single stance and a single set of tools, so that different fields are unified under one framework.

The strength of this paper comes from prohibition. The more constraints, the smaller the feasible region of a prediction, and the higher its precision. Every tool comes with prohibitions, each marked with its form:

- [Never Occurs](concepts.md#c01): a single occurrence refutes it.
- [Not Guaranteed](concepts.md#c02): the framework cannot derive that it necessarily happens; whether other constraints make it hold in a particular case, the framework does not assert. What it limits is how much confidence a prediction can have.

Prohibitions derived from the framework hold for the past and for the future alike, because they are derived from the setting of this paper, and the setting holds at every [moment](concepts.md#c08); there is also only one [generating function](concepts.md#c12), which does not change over time. Statistics of the past can only give [weights](concepts.md#c06) within the feasible region; they cannot give prohibitions.

The framework starts from a question about the mind: "why can I not understand you?" But its conclusions, apart from [impression](concepts.md#c45), do not depend on the mind.

Names of other disciplines in parentheses, such as "(cybernetics: feedback [loop](concepts.md#c31))", are only signposts for finding related literature. They carry no definitions and may not be used as grounds for inference: the definitions, presuppositions and conclusions of those disciplines are not brought into the tools of this paper. In case of doubt, the definitions and formal passages of this paper prevail.

### The place of the four laws in this paper

The four laws of the upstream paper, the [Law of Flux](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c24), the [Law of the Black Box](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c25), the [Law of Attrition](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c26) and the [Law of Non-Restoration](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c28), are the main source of the prohibitions in this paper; the remaining prohibitions are derived from the setting of this paper. Each law has a landing point in this paper; every application must be checked against the four laws at least once.

## 1. Setting

This paper takes over seven concepts from the upstream paper: [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18), [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19), and the [Law of Flux](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c24), the [Law of the Black Box](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c25), the [Law of Attrition](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c26) and the [Law of Non-Restoration](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c28). Terms such as [presentation](concepts.md#c04) and [history](concepts.md#c10) used in the definitions of these concepts are understood in this paper as defined in this section. The other concepts defined in this section are used only in this paper.

### Definitions

- [Universe](concepts.md#c03): the one world in which there is [collapse](concepts.md#c07). Only two things in the [universe](concepts.md#c03) are not determined by the past: the [initial state of the universe](concepts.md#c11), and the outcome of each [collapse](concepts.md#c07); everything else, including every [writing](concepts.md#c09), is determined by the [history](concepts.md#c10) up to that point.
- [Presentation](concepts.md#c04): any part of the [universe](concepts.md#c03).
- [Branch](concepts.md#c05): each possible outcome of a [collapse](concepts.md#c07).
- [Weight](concepts.md#c06): how heavy each [branch](concepts.md#c05) is. The [weights](concepts.md#c06) of the [branches](concepts.md#c05) of a [collapse](concepts.md#c07) are all non-negative and sum to one. The [weight](concepts.md#c06) of a [presentation](concepts.md#c04) is the part of the [weight](concepts.md#c06) of the [universe](concepts.md#c03) that falls on it: once each [branch](concepts.md#c05) is read as an outcome for that [presentation](concepts.md#c04), the [weights](concepts.md#c06) of branches giving the same outcome are added together. A [weight](concepts.md#c06) belongs to [branches](concepts.md#c05); it is not an internal parameter of any [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16).
- [Collapse](concepts.md#c07): the event in which one of the [branches](concepts.md#c05) is settled according to the [weights](concepts.md#c06). [Branches](concepts.md#c05) not settled do not remain in the [universe](concepts.md#c03). Given the [history](concepts.md#c10) up to that point, the outcome of a [collapse](concepts.md#c07) is settled according to the [weights](concepts.md#c06), and nothing in the [history](concepts.md#c10) says anything more than the [weights](concepts.md#c06).
- [Moment](concepts.md#c08): a [collapse](concepts.md#c07). [Collapses](concepts.md#c07) happen one at a time and form a total order; without [collapse](concepts.md#c07) there is no [moment](concepts.md#c08). The [moments](concepts.md#c08) of a [presentation](concepts.md#c04) are the [collapses](concepts.md#c07) that [involve](concepts.md#c14) it; its next [moment](concepts.md#c08) is the next [collapse](concepts.md#c07) that [involves](concepts.md#c14) it; between two of them, there is no [moment](concepts.md#c08) for it.
- [Writing](concepts.md#c09): the item added to [history](concepts.md#c10) before each [collapse](concepts.md#c07). The content of a [writing](concepts.md#c09) is entirely determined by the [history](concepts.md#c10) up to that point. Every [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) of a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) leaves a [writing](concepts.md#c09) in [history](concepts.md#c10).
- [History](concepts.md#c10): begins with the [initial state of the universe](concepts.md#c11), followed by every [writing](concepts.md#c09) and the outcome of every [collapse](concepts.md#c07), arranged by [moment](concepts.md#c08). The [universe](concepts.md#c03) has only one [history](concepts.md#c10); [history](concepts.md#c10) is only ever appended to, and nothing already in it ever leaves.
- [Initial State of the Universe](concepts.md#c11): everything that is already there before the first [collapse](concepts.md#c07); it is the starting point of [history](concepts.md#c10). Its content is not analyzed.
- [Generating Function](concepts.md#c12): maps the [history](concepts.md#c10) up to that point, including the [writing](concepts.md#c09) that has just taken place, to the [weights](concepts.md#c06) of the [branches](concepts.md#c05) of the next [moment](concepts.md#c08). It takes nothing but [history](concepts.md#c10), there is only one of it, and its internals are not analyzed.
- [Arrival](concepts.md#c13): a [writing](concepts.md#c09) [arrives](concepts.md#c13) at a [presentation](concepts.md#c04) when it falls on it. When a [collapse](concepts.md#c07) [involves](concepts.md#c14) a [presentation](concepts.md#c04), its outcome [arrives](concepts.md#c13) at that [presentation](concepts.md#c04); if the outcome does not fall on it, the fact that "this time it did not happen to it" is also something that [arrives](concepts.md#c13) at it. The [initial state of the universe](concepts.md#c11) [arrives](concepts.md#c13) at every [presentation](concepts.md#c04).
- [Involvement](concepts.md#c14): a [collapse](concepts.md#c07) [involves](concepts.md#c14) a [presentation](concepts.md#c04) when its outcome falls on that [presentation](concepts.md#c04) with positive [weight](concepts.md#c06).
- [Lock-in](concepts.md#c15): the next [moment](concepts.md#c08) of a [presentation](concepts.md#c04) has only one [branch](concepts.md#c05) of non-zero [weight](concepts.md#c06). [Lock-in](concepts.md#c15) means being [involved](concepts.md#c14) yet having only one outcome; a period in which a [presentation](concepts.md#c04) has no [moment](concepts.md#c08) is not [lock-in](concepts.md#c15).
- [Own Segment](concepts.md#c16): the part of [history](concepts.md#c10) on which the [weights](concepts.md#c06) of a [presentation](concepts.md#c04)'s next [moment](concepts.md#c08) depend: an item of [history](concepts.md#c10) belongs to its [own segment](concepts.md#c16) if replacing that item with something else would, with the influence passing down the [history](concepts.md#c10), change the distribution of its [weights](concepts.md#c06) for the next [moment](concepts.md#c08). The [own segment](concepts.md#c16) is determined by dependence, not by any [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18); it is part of the one [history](concepts.md#c10), and the [own segments](concepts.md#c16) of different [presentations](concepts.md#c04) may overlap.
- [Individual](concepts.md#c17): a [presentation](concepts.md#c04) marked off by some [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) through [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18). The same stretch of the [universe](concepts.md#c03) may be [distinguished](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) as one [individual](concepts.md#c17) or as many [individuals](concepts.md#c17), and either holds.
- [Identity](concepts.md#c18): a kind of [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18): whether a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) at one [moment](concepts.md#c08) and a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) at another [moment](concepts.md#c08) are the same one is decided by the [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) that makes the [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18). Whether a [presentation](concepts.md#c04) is a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) is not a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18); whether the [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) at two [moments](concepts.md#c08) are the same one is a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18).
- [Incompleteness of Knowing Others](concepts.md#c19): no [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) can guarantee that it has finished [distinguishing](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) another [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16). A [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) can [distinguish](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) the other only through the [history](concepts.md#c10) that has [arrived](concepts.md#c13) at it; the other's information [arriving](concepts.md#c13) is one event and its [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) is another, and between them the other's [history](concepts.md#c10) may still grow, while, when the other is not [locked in](concepts.md#c15), the outcome of its next [moment](concepts.md#c08) cannot be derived beforehand ([Law of the Black Box](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c25)). Moreover, nothing guarantees that the parts of the other's [own segment](concepts.md#c16) that [arrive](concepts.md#c13) only at the other also [arrive](concepts.md#c13) at the self; and once the self's [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18), as a [writing](concepts.md#c09), becomes part of the other's [own segment](concepts.md#c16), it may change the other's [weights](concepts.md#c06) for its next [moment](concepts.md#c08).

### Derived from the definitions

- [Granularity](concepts.md#c20): by the definition of [weight](concepts.md#c06), whether the same stretch of the [universe](concepts.md#c03) is described as one [presentation](concepts.md#c04) or as many [presentations](concepts.md#c04), the [weights](concepts.md#c06) of all these [presentations](concepts.md#c04) are divided out of the one [weight](concepts.md#c06) of the [universe](concepts.md#c03), so the results necessarily agree.
- [History](concepts.md#c10) loses nothing: by the definition of [history](concepts.md#c10), after a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) ceases to be a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), its [own segment](concepts.md#c16) remains complete in [history](concepts.md#c10).
- The other may change as a result: by the definition of [own segment](concepts.md#c16), a [writing](concepts.md#c09) left by a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18), if it belongs to the [own segment](concepts.md#c16) of another [presentation](concepts.md#c04), would, if replaced with something else, change the distribution of that [presentation](concepts.md#c04)'s [weights](concepts.md#c06) for its next [moment](concepts.md#c08).

### Premises

- [Clock Time](concepts.md#c21): a [moment](concepts.md#c08) is only the order of [collapses](concepts.md#c07), which no [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) can measure. The time a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) measures with a clock follows physics and is not formalized in this paper.
- The shape of [weights](concepts.md#c06) can repeat: by the definition of [history](concepts.md#c10), [history](concepts.md#c10) is new at every [moment](concepts.md#c08); but this paper assumes that different [histories](concepts.md#c10) can correspond to [weights](concepts.md#c06) of similar shape. What is ever new is [history](concepts.md#c10); what has nothing new is [weight](concepts.md#c06).

```text
Formal: notation
n ∈ {1, 2, 3, …}: the order of moments; a total order, no two at once
Branch set B; a weight distribution on B: every branch weight ≥ 0, summing to 1
H(0) = (initial state of the universe); the initial state is denoted position 0
W(n+1) = WriteRule(H(n)); WriteRule is a function
H⁺(n) = (H(n), W(n+1))                         -- history after writing
H(n+1) = (H⁺(n), C(n+1))                       -- C(n+1): collapse outcome
Uniqueness of history: for n < m, H(n) is a prefix of H(m)
w(n) = G(H⁺(n−1))                              -- G: generating function
π_x : B → O_x ∪ {⊥}                            -- outcome projection of presentation x; ⊥: not falling on x
w(n, x) = (π_x)_* w(n)                         -- pushforward: branch weights with the same value are added
Arr(x, n) = {0}
  ∪ {positions of writings falling on x}
  ∪ {position of the outcome of collapse k : k ∈ T(x)},
  positions not exceeding the length of H⁺(n)
Inv(x, n) ⟺ w(n, x)(⊥) < 1
T(x) = {n : Inv(x, n)}                         -- set of moments of x
Lock(x, n) ⟺ Inv(x, n) ∧ w(n, x) has exactly one value of non-zero weight, and that value ≠ ⊥
Rep(p, v): replace position p of H⁺(n) by v; later writings recomputed by WriteRule,
  later outcomes settled anew by G
Dist(q | c): the distribution of q given c; actual: the actual value at that position
Own(x, n) = {p : ∃ v, Dist(w(n+1, x) | Rep(p, v)) ≠ Dist(w(n+1, x) | Rep(p, actual))}
Wo(x, n): x satisfies the definition of wo at n
D(x, n): the content of the distinction made by x at n; part of W(n+1)
Granularity: if π_x = f ∘ π_y, then w(n, x) = f_* w(n, y)
```

## 2. The Basic Unit

The basic unit of this paper is the [loop](concepts.md#c31), not the [individual](concepts.md#c17).

- **The [individual](concepts.md#c17) cannot serve as the unit.** By definition, an [individual](concepts.md#c17) is a [presentation](concepts.md#c04) marked off by some [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) through [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18); by the definition of [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) has no fixed core, and a [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) is a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), so there is no [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) with a fixed core in the [universe](concepts.md#c03). When inference takes the [individual](concepts.md#c17) as the unit and treats the [individual](concepts.md#c17) as something unchanged across [moments](concepts.md#c08), it amounts to putting a fixed core back in at the bottom.
- **Every [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) is a [loop](concepts.md#c31).** By the definition of [arrival](concepts.md#c13), the outcome of a [collapse](concepts.md#c07) that [involves](concepts.md#c14) a [presentation](concepts.md#c04) [arrives](concepts.md#c13) at it; by the definition of [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), whatever [arrives](concepts.md#c13) at it takes part in its [weights](concepts.md#c06) for its next [moment](concepts.md#c08). So as long as a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) has had a [moment](concepts.md#c08), its own outcomes come back to take part in itself. This is derived from the definitions and need not be posited separately.
- **Not every [loop](concepts.md#c31) is a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16).** A [loop](concepts.md#c31) is a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) only if, under some [cut](concepts.md#c23), it satisfies the definition of [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16): all the [history](concepts.md#c10) that [arrives](concepts.md#c13) at it takes part in its [weights](concepts.md#c06) for its next [moment](concepts.md#c08), and at least at some [moments](concepts.md#c08) it is not [locked in](concepts.md#c15). A [loop](concepts.md#c31) is a shape; [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) are the ones among them that qualify.
- **Scale is not fixed.** By [granularity](concepts.md#c20) (Section 1), whether a stretch of the [universe](concepts.md#c03) is cut as one [loop](concepts.md#c31) or as many nested [loops](concepts.md#c31), the [weights](concepts.md#c06) agree. A neuron and a civilization can each be a [loop](concepts.md#c31).

To describe a [loop](concepts.md#c31), one must cut out parts and say what [arrives](concepts.md#c13) at what. This is a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) made by the analyst and cannot be avoided. This paper allows [individuals](concepts.md#c17) to be used in description, but does not allow inference to rely on [individuals](concepts.md#c17): an [individual](concepts.md#c17) is only a provisional name, with no fixed core. Apart from [impression](concepts.md#c45), the conclusions of every tool must survive [recutting](concepts.md#c22): cutting one part into many, or merging many parts into one, leaves the conclusions unchanged.

More precisely: under [recutting](concepts.md#c22), [weights](concepts.md#c06) and inferences do not change; tools that describe relations between [loops](concepts.md#c31) ([coupling](concepts.md#c36), [taiji](concepts.md#c39), [impression](concepts.md#c45)) turn into internal structure when parts are merged, and no longer appear as relations. The extreme case is to cut the whole [universe](concepts.md#c03) as a single [loop](concepts.md#c31). This is a legitimate [cut](concepts.md#c23), but there is no second [loop](concepts.md#c31) outside the [universe](concepts.md#c03), so [coupling](concepts.md#c36), [taiji](concepts.md#c39) and [impression](concepts.md#c45) are left only as internal structure.

The following two things remain [distinctions](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18):

- [Cut](concepts.md#c23): which stretch of the [universe](concepts.md#c03) is taken as one [loop](concepts.md#c31).
- [Identity](concepts.md#c18): whether [loops](concepts.md#c31) at different [moments](concepts.md#c08) are the same one.

By the definition of [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), once the [cut](concepts.md#c23) is stated, whether something is a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) is not decided by [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18). So a [loop](concepts.md#c31) in this paper always means "a [loop](concepts.md#c31) under a stated [cut](concepts.md#c23)", and it is not guaranteed to persist across [moments](concepts.md#c08).

The only tool that can be stated only by marking off "that is him" is [impression](concepts.md#c45).

## 3. Notation

- [Boundary](concepts.md#c24): every example must first fix a geographical [boundary](concepts.md#c24) and a temporal [boundary](concepts.md#c24). This is a [cut](concepts.md#c23) and must be stated.
- [Node](concepts.md#c25): a [presentation](concepts.md#c04) under a [cut](concepts.md#c23); it is a provisional name. There are three kinds of [node](concepts.md#c25):
  - [Wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16): its own [output](concepts.md#c28) comes back to itself, and it is subject to [cognitive obscuration](concepts.md#c35); when it is not [locked in](concepts.md#c15), only its [weights](concepts.md#c06) can be discussed.
  - [Signal Container](concepts.md#c26): a [node](concepts.md#c25) that is not a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) and is [locked in](concepts.md#c15) at every step. It usually persists and [outputs](concepts.md#c28) to [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) at many [moments](concepts.md#c08), but this is not a condition. Its content may be written by [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), as with books or statutes, or may come from other [presentations](concepts.md#c04), as with observations brought by a telescope. [Cognitive obscuration](concepts.md#c35) has no content for it; nor can it be the object of an [impression](concepts.md#c45): reading a book is an [arrival](concepts.md#c13), not an [impression](concepts.md#c45).
  - [Random Source](concepts.md#c27): not a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), and not [locked in](concepts.md#c15) at every step: at least at some [moments](concepts.md#c08) it is not [locked in](concepts.md#c15), yet not all the [history](concepts.md#c10) that [arrives](concepts.md#c13) at it takes part in its [weights](concepts.md#c06); examples are a thrown die and a radioactively decaying atom. When it is not [locked in](concepts.md#c15), only its [weights](concepts.md#c06) can be discussed; nor can it be the object of an [impression](concepts.md#c45).
- [Output](concepts.md#c28): the outcomes of a [presentation](concepts.md#c04)'s [collapses](concepts.md#c07), together with the content of its [distinctions](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18).
- [Edge](concepts.md#c29): directed; it means that the [output](concepts.md#c28) of one [node](concepts.md#c25) takes part in the [weights](concepts.md#c06) of another [node](concepts.md#c25). An [edge](concepts.md#c29) carries no magnitude of its own: how much an [edge](concepts.md#c29) matters depends on the receiver's [history](concepts.md#c10), not on the [edge](concepts.md#c29). In the examples, [edges](concepts.md#c29) are drawn only for direct [arrivals](concepts.md#c13).
- [Timing](concepts.md#c30): [edges](concepts.md#c29) may be marked with their order and with how long it takes to go round once. [Nodes](concepts.md#c25) may appear or disappear at some time within the [boundary](concepts.md#c24).

```text
Formal:
Out(x) = {position of the outcome of collapse k : k ∈ T(x)} ∪ {position of D(x, k)}
Edge(x, y, n) ⟺ ∃ p ∈ Out(x) with p ∈ Own(y, n)
Whether an edge is direct or indirect depends on the cut
```

## 4. Tools

### 1. Loop (cybernetics: feedback loop)

**Definition**: a [loop](concepts.md#c31) is a structure in which the [output](concepts.md#c28) of a [presentation](concepts.md#c04), through itself or through other [presentations](concepts.md#c04), takes part again in its own [weights](concepts.md#c06).

- [Positive Feedback](concepts.md#c32): pushes the [weights](concepts.md#c06) toward the direction that produced it.
- [Negative Feedback](concepts.md#c33): pushes the [weights](concepts.md#c06) away from the direction that produced it.
- [Loop Speed](concepts.md#c34): how long it takes to go round once. [Loop speed](concepts.md#c34) is measured in [clock time](concepts.md#c21), which differs from [moments](concepts.md#c08) (see the premises in Section 1).

The direction must contain the [loop](concepts.md#c31)'s own [output](concepts.md#c28) in question, which anchors it; it is not chosen freely by the analyst. How coarsely the direction is drawn belongs to the [cut](concepts.md#c23) and must be stated.

**A point to keep clear**: resistance met by a [loop](concepts.md#c31), if caused by its own [output](concepts.md#c28) coming back round, is [negative feedback](concepts.md#c33), as with habituation, saturation or resource depletion; if it is the [output](concepts.md#c28) of another [loop](concepts.md#c31) pressing in through [coupling](concepts.md#c36), it is not [negative feedback](concepts.md#c33). For this relation between two [positive feedback](concepts.md#c32) [loops](concepts.md#c31) pointing in opposite directions, see [taiji](concepts.md#c39). [Negative feedback](concepts.md#c33) really exists under physical conditions, and cannot be replaced by the [coupling](concepts.md#c36) of two [positive feedbacks](concepts.md#c32).

**Grounds**: the definitions of [arrival](concepts.md#c13) and [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16).

**Prohibitions**:

- [Not guaranteed](concepts.md#c02): a [loop](concepts.md#c31) is not guaranteed to persist. As long as it is not [locked in](concepts.md#c15), every round must pass through [collapse](concepts.md#c07).

**Examples**:

- Neurons: repeated firing strengthens synapses ([positive feedback](concepts.md#c32)); responses decline under repeated stimulation ([negative feedback](concepts.md#c33)).
- People: beliefs reinforce themselves ([positive feedback](concepts.md#c32)); what becomes habitual is no longer noticed ([negative feedback](concepts.md#c33)).
- Civilizations: a school's writings train the next generation of scholars ([positive feedback](concepts.md#c32)); resource depletion halts expansion ([negative feedback](concepts.md#c33)).

```text
Formal:
Loop(x, n) ⟺ ∃ p ∈ Out(x), p in H⁺(n), with p ∈ Own(x, n)
Sign: let A ⊆ O_x be a direction, p an outcome of x falling in A, m after p.
  Actual = Dist(Σ_A w(m, x) | Rep(p, actual))
  Control = Dist(Σ_A w(m, x) | Rep(p, a value outside A))
  If for every threshold, P(Actual > threshold) ≥ P(Control > threshold), strictly somewhere,
    positive feedback;
  if the reverse, negative feedback;
  otherwise, neither.
Speed: measured in clock time; physics; not formalized
```

### 2. Cognitive Obscuration

**Definition**: [cognitive obscuration](concepts.md#c35) is the fact that the [weights](concepts.md#c06) of the outcomes of a [presentation](concepts.md#c04) at its next [moment](concepts.md#c08) sum to one, so that pushing one direction up necessarily pushes the total of the other directions down. All learning has a cost; knowing and obscuration are one and the same.

**Grounds**: the definition of [weight](concepts.md#c06): the [weights](concepts.md#c06) of the [branches](concepts.md#c05) of a [collapse](concepts.md#c07) sum to one, and the [weights](concepts.md#c06) of a [presentation](concepts.md#c04) are divided out of them, so they also sum to one.

**Note**: [cognitive obscuration](concepts.md#c35) guarantees only that one rises as the others fall; it does not decide which direction an [arrival](concepts.md#c13) pushes up, or by how much (see Section 5, Rule 1).

**Prohibitions**:

- [Never occurs](concepts.md#c01): one side is pushed up while the total of the others does not fall.

**Examples**:

- Cells: when the [weight](concepts.md#c06) of one mode of response of an immune cell to an antigen rises, the total [weight](concepts.md#c06) of its other modes of response necessarily falls.
- People: after learning to read, on seeing a word, the [weight](concepts.md#c06) of "reading it" rises and the [weights](concepts.md#c06) of the other directions necessarily fall (the Stroop effect: the meaning of a word interferes with naming its color). This step is [cognitive obscuration](concepts.md#c35). "Being unable to go back to illiteracy" comes from the [Law of Non-Restoration](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c28): the self that could not yet read will not happen again. "Finding it hard to understand the illiterate" is damage to the material of [impression](concepts.md#c45): that experience is still in [history](concepts.md#c10) (Section 1: [history](concepts.md#c10) loses nothing), but the present [weights](concepts.md#c06) can no longer reproduce it, so putting oneself in another's place does not work. Neither of the latter two is derived from [cognitive obscuration](concepts.md#c35); of them, "completely unable" is an extreme concentration and belongs to empirical description.
- Civilizations: when the [weight](concepts.md#c06) of one institutional arrangement rises, the total [weight](concepts.md#c06) of the other arrangements necessarily falls.

[Cognitive obscuration](concepts.md#c35) concerns only the distribution of the [weights](concepts.md#c06) of one [presentation](concepts.md#c04) at its next [moment](concepts.md#c08). Trade-offs between different environments (such as the fitness cost of drug resistance), the size of an effect, and the range of generalization are not derived from [cognitive obscuration](concepts.md#c35); they are empirical assumptions.

```text
Formal:
Let A ⊆ O_x be a direction.
Σ_A w(n, x) + Σ_{O_x∖A} w(n, x) = 1
```

### 3. Coupling (cybernetics: coupling)

**Definition**: [coupling](concepts.md#c36) is the situation in which the [own segments](concepts.md#c16) of two or more [presentations](concepts.md#c04) overlap in a stated [arrival](concepts.md#c13). What takes part in [coupling](concepts.md#c36) may be [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), or [loops](concepts.md#c31) made of many [presentations](concepts.md#c04) (see [taiji](concepts.md#c39)). The overlap takes one of two forms: the same [output](concepts.md#c28) [arrives](concepts.md#c13) at all of them, as with a shared scripture; or their [outputs](concepts.md#c28) [arrive](concepts.md#c13) at one another, as with two civilizations at war.

[Coupling](concepts.md#c36) is always relative to a stated [arrival](concepts.md#c13). By the definition of the [initial state of the universe](concepts.md#c11), the [initial state of the universe](concepts.md#c11) [arrives](concepts.md#c13) at every [presentation](concepts.md#c04), so any two [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) overlap at this point; to say that two [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) are [coupled](concepts.md#c36) without stating which [arrival](concepts.md#c13) is meant says nothing.

**Directions of development**: under [coupling](concepts.md#c36), the patterns of [outputs](concepts.md#c28) along any one dimension have only three directions of development:

- [Convergence](concepts.md#c37) (resonance): the distance between patterns shrinks.
- [Divergence](concepts.md#c38) (differentiation; Bateson: schismogenesis): the distance between patterns grows.
- [Stasis](concepts.md#c52): the distance between patterns stays the same.

**High dimensionality**: patterns are high-dimensional. The same stretch of [coupling](concepts.md#c36) can show [convergence](concepts.md#c37) along one dimension and [divergence](concepts.md#c38) along another at the same time. So to speak of [convergence](concepts.md#c37), [divergence](concepts.md#c38) or [stasis](concepts.md#c52), one must state which dimension is meant. Which dimension to choose is the analyst's [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18).

**Grounds**: the definition of [arrival](concepts.md#c13); the definition of [own segment](concepts.md#c16) (the [own segments](concepts.md#c16) of different [presentations](concepts.md#c04) may overlap); [cognitive obscuration](concepts.md#c35); the [Law of the Black Box](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c25).

**Prohibitions**:

- [Not guaranteed](concepts.md#c02): [coupling](concepts.md#c36) is not guaranteed to produce [convergence](concepts.md#c37) along any dimension, nor [divergence](concepts.md#c38) or [stasis](concepts.md#c52): the definition of [coupling](concepts.md#c36) says only that [own segments](concepts.md#c16) overlap in an [arrival](concepts.md#c13), and contains no direction of development; moreover, when the participating [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) are not [locked in](concepts.md#c15), the outcomes of their next [moments](concepts.md#c08) cannot be derived ([Law of the Black Box](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c25)). Both "exchange brings understanding" and "contact inevitably leads to conflict" are prohibited.
- [Not guaranteed](concepts.md#c02): [convergence](concepts.md#c37) does not guarantee the same results either: the same [arrival](concepts.md#c13) is only one item in each one's [history](concepts.md#c10), and the results are determined by each one's whole [history](concepts.md#c10) (the definition of the [generating function](concepts.md#c12)); moreover, when each [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) is not [locked in](concepts.md#c15), its next [moment](concepts.md#c08) cannot be derived ([Law of the Black Box](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c25)).

**Examples**:

- Neurons: two populations of neurons oscillate in synchrony ([convergence](concepts.md#c37)); lateral inhibition drives the responses of neighboring neurons ever further apart ([divergence](concepts.md#c38)).
- People: a married couple grows more and more alike ([convergence](concepts.md#c37)); brothers go their separate ways after dividing the family estate ([divergence](concepts.md#c38)).
- Civilizations: in more than a thousand years of contact, Christianity and Islam showed [divergence](concepts.md#c38) in faith and identity, and [convergence](concepts.md#c37) in philosophy, mathematics and trade.

```text
Formal:
Coup(a, b, p, n) ⟺ p ∈ Own(a, n) ∩ Own(b, n)
MutCoup(a, b, n) ⟺ ∃ p_a ∈ Out(a) with p_a ∈ Own(b, n),
  and ∃ p_b ∈ Out(b) with p_b ∈ Own(a, n)
Convergence, divergence: a dimension must be chosen and the outputs of a and b compared;
  this is a distinction; not formalized
```

#### Corollary: Taiji

**Definition**: [taiji](concepts.md#c39) is a structure in which two [positive feedback](concepts.md#c32) [loops](concepts.md#c31) pointing in opposite directions are [coupled](concepts.md#c36) with each other and show [divergence](concepts.md#c38) within that [coupling](concepts.md#c36), forming a dynamic balance. Each side's [output](concepts.md#c28), [arriving](concepts.md#c13) at the other, strengthens the other's own direction, so whichever side grows is feeding its opponent.

[Taiji](concepts.md#c39) takes place between [loops](concepts.md#c31); it is not the rise and fall of the [weights](concepts.md#c06) of a single [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16). It is a structure of [loops](concepts.md#c31), and does not require every participant to be a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16).

"Opposite directions" can be judged only after choosing a dimension and matching the [outputs](concepts.md#c28) of the two [loops](concepts.md#c31) to each other; this is a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18). So to say that two [loops](concepts.md#c31) form a [taiji](concepts.md#c39), one must state along which dimension.

**Nesting**: [taiji](concepts.md#c39) can be nested. One side of a [taiji](concepts.md#c39) can contain another [taiji](concepts.md#c39) within it; which level one looks at is decided by the [cut](concepts.md#c23). For example, royal power and ecclesiastical power within medieval Europe form one [taiji](concepts.md#c39), while the whole of Christendom and Islam form another [taiji](concepts.md#c39), the former nested within the latter.

**Ways of ending**: there are at least two:

- [Withdrawal of One Side](concepts.md#c40): most of the [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) on one side [exit](concepts.md#c43) (see [renewal](concepts.md#c42)), or that side no longer reinforces itself.
- [Merging](concepts.md#c41): the [coupling](concepts.md#c36) turns from [divergence](concepts.md#c38) to [convergence](concepts.md#c37), and the two [loops](concepts.md#c31) become one. Under [recutting](concepts.md#c22), this can also be seen as the two [loops](concepts.md#c31) being cut as one.

**Prohibitions**:

- [Not guaranteed](concepts.md#c02): a [taiji](concepts.md#c39) is not guaranteed to end; nor, when it ends, is it guaranteed which side disappears, or whether the two sides [merge](concepts.md#c41): the definition of [taiji](concepts.md#c39) contains no condition for ending; moreover, as long as there are [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) among them that are not [locked in](concepts.md#c15), the outcomes of both sides' next [moments](concepts.md#c08) cannot be derived ([Law of the Black Box](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c25)).
- "One side disappears" must be judged under some [cut](concepts.md#c23); it is a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18).

**Examples**:

- Cells: tumors and the immune system. The theory of immunoediting distinguishes three phases, elimination, equilibrium and escape; the equilibrium phase can last for years.
- Artificial systems: generative adversarial networks. The generator and the discriminator point in opposite directions, and each one's [output](concepts.md#c28) is the other's training material; when training fails, one side overwhelms the other.
- Civilizations: royal power and ecclesiastical power in medieval Europe. The Investiture Controversy, the bull *Unam Sanctam* of 1302 and the Anagni incident of the following year: each side's claims fueled the other's. The two sides showed [divergence](concepts.md#c38) over who held authority, yet depended on each other for legitimacy and protection, so the balance could last for centuries. In England, the Act of Supremacy of 1534 made the king the head of the church, an ending by [merging](concepts.md#c41).

```text
Formal:
Only the structural premises can be formalized: Loop(a, n), Loop(b, n), MutCoup(a, b, n)
Opposite directions, divergence, dynamic balance, ways of ending: all require choosing a
  dimension and comparing outputs; this is a distinction; not formalized
```

### 4. Renewal (Ryder: demographic metabolism; Planck's principle)

**Definition**: [renewal](concepts.md#c42) is the going and coming of [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16): some [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) cease to be [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), and some [presentations](concepts.md#c04) begin to be [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16). The [own segments](concepts.md#c16) of the new [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) overlap with those of the old ones, but can never be exactly the same.

- [Exit](concepts.md#c43): the participation of some [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) fades away, so that they are no longer [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16). By the definition of [history](concepts.md#c10), their [own segments](concepts.md#c16) remain in [history](concepts.md#c10) (Section 1: [history](concepts.md#c10) loses nothing); the [outputs](concepts.md#c28) they left behind, such as texts, institutions and buildings, can still [arrive](concepts.md#c13) at later [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) through [signal containers](concepts.md#c26).
- [Entry](concepts.md#c44): some [presentations](concepts.md#c04) begin to be [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16). Their [own segments](concepts.md#c16) overlap with those of the old [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), for example in the parts that come from parents, teachers and texts.

**Grounds**: the definition of [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) (participation may fade away), the [Law of Flux](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c24), the [Law of Non-Restoration](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c28), and the definition of [history](concepts.md#c10).

By the [Law of Flux](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c24), no [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) lies in the same [history](concepts.md#c10) as at its previous moment; freezing a [presentation](concepts.md#c04) does not stop the [history](concepts.md#c10) it lies in. So there is no static state that could be preserved "as it was". By the [Law of Non-Restoration](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c28), the [own segment](concepts.md#c16) of what has [exited](concepts.md#c43) will not happen again. Only the two together are the full grounds of [renewal](concepts.md#c42): the former says it will not stop, the latter says it cannot go back.

**Prohibitions**:

- [Never occurs](concepts.md#c01): the [history](concepts.md#c10) in which a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) lies stops changing after some [moment](concepts.md#c08). "The tradition froze" and "the institution stood still" are statements of this kind; one can only say that the [weights](concepts.md#c06) are highly concentrated. What is ever new is [history](concepts.md#c10); what has nothing new is [weight](concepts.md#c06) (the premises in Section 1).
- [Not guaranteed](concepts.md#c02): no [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) can guarantee that the choices the old [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) made while not [locked in](concepts.md#c15) will be made again. So the [convergence](concepts.md#c37) within a [coupling](concepts.md#c36), once the participating [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) have gone through [renewal](concepts.md#c42), is not guaranteed to continue as it was: the definition of [convergence](concepts.md#c37) contains no condition for continuing, and the choices that sustained it are not guaranteed to be made again.
- This paper does not assert that any [loop](concepts.md#c31), nation or culture must disappear. Disappearance and extinction are labels, and belong to [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18). What the framework can derive is that the [history](concepts.md#c10) it lies in is necessarily different; whether to say it has become something different, and whether to call that extinction, is a matter of [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18).

**Note**: "as it was" must be judged under some [cut](concepts.md#c23). At a coarse enough scale, a tradition may look unchanged; an application must state the scale of judgment.

**Examples**:

- Cells: cell turnover in tissues.
- People: old habits fading and new habits forming over a lifetime.
- Civilizations: generational succession. In the late Qing dynasty, scholars who had spent their lives immersed in the imperial examinations, faced with an [arrival](concepts.md#c13) as high in [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49) as science, tended to explain it away rather than be overturned by it (the empirical assumption of Explicit Rule 1: the more concentrated the receiver's [history](concepts.md#c10), the more likely a shock is explained away). The Qing court's way out was [renewal](concepts.md#c42): from 1872 it sent young students to study in the United States, so that a group of new [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) without this [history](concepts.md#c10) would receive science; in 1905 it abolished the imperial examinations, cutting off the old [loop](concepts.md#c31)'s source of new [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16).

**Corollary: the way out of [cognitive obscuration](concepts.md#c35)**. [Cognitive obscuration](concepts.md#c35) cannot be undone within a single [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16): the [weights](concepts.md#c06) can move again, but by the [Law of Non-Restoration](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c28), that [own segment](concepts.md#c16) of [history](concepts.md#c10) will not recur. When the [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) of a [loop](concepts.md#c31) are generally held in some direction by [cognitive obscuration](concepts.md#c35), the way out is usually [renewal](concepts.md#c42) rather than persuasion. This is an empirical description, not an inference.

```text
Formal:
Exit(x, n) ⟺ Wo(x, n−1) ∧ ¬Wo(x, n)
Entry(x, n) ⟺ ¬Wo(x, n−1) ∧ Wo(x, n)
"Continuing as it was" requires matching the entering to the exiting as identical;
  this is a distinction; not formalized
```

### 5. Impression (theory of mind; good regulator theorem)

**Definition**: an [impression](concepts.md#c45) is a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) made by a [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) of a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), that is, its estimate of that [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16)'s [weights](concepts.md#c06). The object may be another [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), or the [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) itself, in which case it is called a [self-impression](concepts.md#c46). Every statistical estimate about a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), including statistics of historical records, is an [impression](concepts.md#c45).

**Grounds**: the definitions of [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) and [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19), the definition of [writing](concepts.md#c09) (a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) leaves a [writing](concepts.md#c09) in [history](concepts.md#c10)), the [Law of the Black Box](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c25), the [Law of Attrition](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c26), and the [Incompleteness of Knowing Others](concepts.md#c19).

**[Self-impression](concepts.md#c46)**: by the [Law of Attrition](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c26), the material of a [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)'s [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) of itself does not include that [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) itself, so it always lags one step behind; it cannot finish [distinguishing](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) even its own [own segment](concepts.md#c16). So a [self-impression](concepts.md#c46), like an [impression](concepts.md#c45) of others, is an estimate, can only be corrected through [arrival](concepts.md#c13), and is incomplete at every [moment](concepts.md#c08). The [Incompleteness of Knowing Others](concepts.md#c19) and the [Law of Attrition](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c26) are the same structure: the former directed at others, the latter at oneself.

**Material** comes from three sources:

1. the other's past [outputs](concepts.md#c28);
2. the part of the [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)'s own [own segment](concepts.md#c16) that overlaps with the other's (putting oneself in another's place);
3. the [couplings](concepts.md#c36) the other is in, such as roles, institutions and beliefs.

**Properties**:

- An [impression](concepts.md#c45) can only be corrected through [arrival](concepts.md#c13): newly [arriving](concepts.md#c13) [outputs](concepts.md#c28), or [history](concepts.md#c10) that has long since [arrived](concepts.md#c13) but has not yet been [distinguished](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18). By the definition of [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), all the [history](concepts.md#c10) that [arrives](concepts.md#c13) at it takes part in its [weights](concepts.md#c06); by the [Law of Attrition](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c26), this [history](concepts.md#c10) is never all [distinguished](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18), so going back to [distinguish](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) [history](concepts.md#c10) that has already [arrived](concepts.md#c13) can also change an [impression](concepts.md#c45).
- By the definition of [collapse](concepts.md#c07), a single [output](concepts.md#c28) is only one outcome settled according to the [weights](concepts.md#c06), not the [weights](concepts.md#c06) themselves, so a single [output](concepts.md#c28) cannot show the shape of the [weights](concepts.md#c06). How much several [outputs](concepts.md#c28) taken together can show depends on whether the shapes of the [weights](concepts.md#c06) at those [moments](concepts.md#c08) are similar (the premises in Section 1; whether this holds is an empirical assumption).
- Merely accumulating on the same judgment, with neither a new [arrival](concepts.md#c13) nor a new [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) of [history](concepts.md#c10) that has already [arrived](concepts.md#c13), is not a correction, and is not guaranteed to improve calibration.
- An [impression](concepts.md#c45) is a [writing](concepts.md#c09); once it [arrives](concepts.md#c13) at the other, it may itself change the other (see "Derived from the definitions" in Section 1); when the other's changed [output](concepts.md#c28) [arrives](concepts.md#c13) back at this [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19), the [impression](concepts.md#c45) forms a [loop](concepts.md#c31).
- The accuracy of an [impression](concepts.md#c45) should be measured by calibration, that is, by whether the [weights](concepts.md#c06) bet on are right, not by hit rate.

**Prohibitions**:

- [Never occurs](concepts.md#c01): for a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) that is not [locked in](concepts.md#c15), no [impression](concepts.md#c45) can uniquely derive its next [moment](concepts.md#c08) ([Law of the Black Box](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c25)). The same holds when the object is oneself: before the choice, even it does not know.
- [Never occurs](concepts.md#c01): a [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) completely [distinguishes](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) itself at some [moment](concepts.md#c08) ([Law of Attrition](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c26)).
- [Not guaranteed](concepts.md#c02): no [impression](concepts.md#c45) of another can be guaranteed to be complete ([Incompleteness of Knowing Others](concepts.md#c19)).

**Note**: this is the only tool in this paper that requires [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18), so it does not survive [recutting](concepts.md#c22) across scales: cells cannot serve as an example. Whether an AI in conversation has [impressions](concepts.md#c45) depends on whether it is a [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19); whether it is a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) is currently undecided (see Section 5, Rule 5).

**Examples**: a person's understanding of a friend; one nation's judgment of another; the church regarding a school as heretical; a person looking back on why they made a certain decision.

```text
Formal:
Imp(x, y, n) is some D(x, n) whose object is y; y may equal x (self-impression)
Material ⊆ Arr(x, n); content not formalized
```

## 5. Explicit Rules

1. **This paper does not decide the [impact](concepts.md#c47) of an [arrival](concepts.md#c13).** How much [weight](concepts.md#c06) an [arrival](concepts.md#c13) can move is called its [impact](concepts.md#c47), and it is a matter internal to the [generating function](concepts.md#c12). An application must state the assumptions it bets on and mark them as empirical assumptions. At least two must be stated:
   - [Frequency](concepts.md#c48): how often it [arrives](concepts.md#c13).
   - [Discrepancy](concepts.md#c49) (information theory: surprisal): how far this [arrival](concepts.md#c13) lies from what the receiver has received before. [Discrepancy](concepts.md#c49) is relative to the receiver's [history](concepts.md#c10): the same [arrival](concepts.md#c13), falling on different [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), can have entirely different [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49).

   The examples in this paper adopt the following empirical assumptions: repeated [arrivals](concepts.md#c13) of low [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49) have diminishing effects; a rare [arrival](concepts.md#c13) of high [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49) can, in one go, outweigh a long accumulation; the more concentrated the receiver's [history](concepts.md#c10), the more likely a shock of high [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49) is explained away rather than overturning it.

   [Impacts](concepts.md#c47) do not add up: a hundred small earthquakes are not one large earthquake. This agrees with the definition of the [generating function](concepts.md#c12): [weights](concepts.md#c06) are a function of the whole [history](concepts.md#c10), and the effect of an [arrival](concepts.md#c13) depends on the [history](concepts.md#c10) before it.

2. **Statistics belong to [impression](concepts.md#c45).** Historical records can be used to estimate the [impact](concepts.md#c47) of [arrivals](concepts.md#c13). For such estimates to be meaningful, the shape of [weights](concepts.md#c06) must actually repeat (the premises in Section 1); whether this holds in a given field is an empirical assumption. The records themselves are filtered [outputs](concepts.md#c28); before doing statistics, one must first ask who left them.

3. **What is derived to be impossible must be kept apart from what has not occurred.** The former holds across time; the latter is only a [weight](concepts.md#c06), and may be overturned by an [arrival](concepts.md#c13) of high [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49).

4. **Formalization stops at structure.** What this paper formalizes are relations: [arrival](concepts.md#c13), participation, one rising as another falls, overlap. What it does not formalize is content: [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49), the dimensions of [convergence](concepts.md#c37) and [divergence](concepts.md#c38), [impression](concepts.md#c45), the shape of the [generating function](concepts.md#c12). This paper does not decide the latter; they can only be described qualitatively in the application papers and marked as empirical assumptions.

5. **[Application assumption](concepts.md#c50).** The examples in this paper all adopt the following [application assumption](concepts.md#c50): at the levels of biology and the social sciences, if the next moment of a [presentation](concepts.md#c04) cannot be uniquely derived from the [history](concepts.md#c10) available at that level, it is assumed not to be [locked in](concepts.md#c15). This is not a theorem; whether humans and AI are not [locked in](concepts.md#c15) is currently undecided for both. If a [presentation](concepts.md#c04) is [locked in](concepts.md#c15) for some period, as with coerced behavior, the rule "only [weights](concepts.md#c06) can be discussed" does not apply to that period.

6. **Whatever can be expressed statistically is not expressed teleologically.** A purpose belongs to a [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)'s [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) of itself; only a [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) has one. For a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) that is not a [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19), write only statistical changes, such as "drug-resistant strains have a higher survival rate in environments with the drug", not "the bacteria developed resistance in order to survive". For a [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19), purposes may be written, but they still affect only [weights](concepts.md#c06): one may be hungry and still not eat. Moreover, a purpose stated by a [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) is its [self-impression](concepts.md#c46), which by the [Law of Attrition](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c26) always lags one step behind; "what he says he did it for" can be taken only as one [output](concepts.md#c28), not as a complete explanation of his behavior.

7. **[Empirical prohibition](concepts.md#c51).** A necessary condition distilled from cases is called an [empirical prohibition](concepts.md#c51): without a certain condition, a certain outcome [never occurs](concepts.md#c01). It is not derived from the framework but obtained by calibration across cases, so it must be marked as such, and may be revised by new cases. It is stronger than mere "has not occurred", because it has been tested across cases; but it is still not a derived impossibility (see Rule 3). State only necessary conditions, never sufficient ones: even when all the conditions are met, one can only say that the [weight](concepts.md#c06) of that outcome has become heavier, not that it must happen.

## 7. Format of Examples

Examples are qualitative analyses and need no data. They are written in the following seven steps, each of which must produce something.

1. **[Boundary](concepts.md#c24)**: state the geographical [boundary](concepts.md#c24) and the temporal [boundary](concepts.md#c24).
2. **Initial state**: list the [nodes](concepts.md#c25), marking whether each [node](concepts.md#c25) is a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), a [signal container](concepts.md#c26) or a [random source](concepts.md#c27); describe the [loops](concepts.md#c31) and [couplings](concepts.md#c36) they are in, and along which dimensions they show [convergence](concepts.md#c37) or [divergence](concepts.md#c38).
3. **New [nodes](concepts.md#c25) appear**: state when the new [nodes](concepts.md#c25) appear, what they bring that was not there before, and what conflict they cause.
4. **Changes in [weight](concepts.md#c06)**: state how the new and old [nodes](concepts.md#c25) each move; which [loops](concepts.md#c31) are fast and which are slow; whether there is a [taiji](concepts.md#c39); and the assumptions about [impact](concepts.md#c47) bet on ([frequency](concepts.md#c48), [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49)).
5. **Outcome**: state the outcome, and the scale at which it is judged.
6. **Inferences, historical facts, empirical assumptions and prohibitions**: mark item by item which are derived from the framework, which are historical facts (used only for calibration), which are empirical assumptions, and which statements are prohibited in this example.
7. **Conditions and reuse**: distill [empirical prohibitions](concepts.md#c51) from the example, that is, necessary conditions for some outcome, and test them against other cases. State only necessary conditions, never sufficient ones. Agreement strengthens the [empirical prohibition](concepts.md#c51); deviation means either the conditions were matched wrongly or the prohibition must be revised (see Explicit Rule 7).

## 8. Example: Heliocentrism Replacing Geocentrism

### 1. Boundary

- Geography: the European learned world, chiefly Italy, Central Europe and England.
- Time: from 1543 to around 1700.

### 2. Initial state (before 1543)

**[Nodes](concepts.md#c25)**:

- [Wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16): the older generation of astronomers, the universities, the church.
- [Signal container](concepts.md#c26): the Ptolemaic system (chiefly the writings of Aristotle and Ptolemy).

**State**: the Ptolemaic system [arrives](concepts.md#c13) at all three [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), forming a [coupling](concepts.md#c36), and the three show [convergence](concepts.md#c37) in astronomical ideas. The universities teach it to students, the students become scholars and return to teach in the universities, forming a [positive feedback](concepts.md#c32) [loop](concepts.md#c31). This [loop](concepts.md#c31) takes a generation to go round once. There is only one theory in the picture, and no conflict.

### 3. New nodes appear

- **1543, *De revolutionibus*** ([signal container](concepts.md#c26)) appears and [arrives](concepts.md#c13) at a few scholars, forming a small [positive feedback](concepts.md#c32) [loop](concepts.md#c31). At this point it is only theory against theory: both systems can roughly explain the observations of the time, and there is no conflict over facts.
- **The supernova of 1572 and the great comet of 1577**: one-off [arrivals](concepts.md#c13) of high [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49), which shake "the heavens do not change".
- **1588, the Tychonic system** ([signal container](concepts.md#c26)) appears: the earth is still, the sun goes round the earth, and the other planets go round the sun. It keeps "the earth is still" while accommodating many new observations.
- **From 1609, the telescope** ([signal container](concepts.md#c26)) appears. It [outputs](concepts.md#c28) facts not previously in the picture, such as the moons of Jupiter and the phases of Venus. The phases of Venus are incompatible with the Ptolemaic system, but compatible with both the Tychonic system and heliocentrism. For the first time the picture contains a conflict between facts and the traditional theory. The telescope does not [output](concepts.md#c28) once and stop; it keeps [outputting](concepts.md#c28): more and more telescopes, more and more observations.
- **1609 and 1619, Kepler's laws of planetary motion; 1627, the *Rudolphine Tables*** ([signal container](concepts.md#c26)): tables computed from elliptical orbits, with predictive accuracy clearly higher than before, tested at every observation thereafter; for example, the transit of Mercury of 1631 predicted by them was observed as expected.

### 4. Changes in weight

- **The Ptolemaic [loop](concepts.md#c31) is broken**: the phases of Venus are an [arrival](concepts.md#c13) of high [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49). After the 1610s, the pure Ptolemaic system quickly lost ground among astronomers. But many [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) originally in the Ptolemaic [loop](concepts.md#c31) did not turn to heliocentrism; they moved into the Tychonic system and kept "the earth is still". How far each moved depended on each one's [history](concepts.md#c10), a question of [impact](concepts.md#c47) (empirical assumption).
- **[Taiji](concepts.md#c39)**: the real standoff of the following decades was between the Tychonic [loop](concepts.md#c31) and the heliocentric [loop](concepts.md#c31) (the side of Kepler and Galileo). The two point in opposite directions along the dimension "does the earth move?", and are [coupled](concepts.md#c36) with each other in [divergence](concepts.md#c38). The church's prohibition of 1616 and the trial of 1633 were [outputs](concepts.md#c28) of the geostatic side [arriving](concepts.md#c13) at the heliocentric [loop](concepts.md#c31); they were not [negative feedback](concepts.md#c33) of the heliocentric [loop](concepts.md#c31).
- **[Loop speed](concepts.md#c34)**: the heliocentric side's [loop](concepts.md#c31) (observation, tables, prediction, observation again) took only a few years to go round once; the traditional [loop](concepts.md#c31) of master and disciple took a generation, as long as the cycle of [renewal](concepts.md#c42), so it could not reinforce itself repeatedly within the same generation.
- **[Cognitive obscuration](concepts.md#c35)**: this example considers only two directions, heliocentric and geostatic; at the time there was no other developed theory. For any newly entering scholar, when the heliocentric [weight](concepts.md#c06) rises, the geostatic [weight](concepts.md#c06) (including the Tychonic system) necessarily falls.
- **[Renewal](concepts.md#c42)**: newly entering scholars received the [outputs](concepts.md#c28) of both sides at once, along with the continual high-[discrepancy](concepts.md#c49) [outputs](concepts.md#c28) of telescopes and tables, and the new [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) mostly joined the heliocentric [loop](concepts.md#c31).
- **Assumption about [impact](concepts.md#c47)**: this example bets that [frequency](concepts.md#c48) and [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49) both favored the heliocentric side (empirical assumption).

### 5. Outcome

In 1687 the *Principia* appeared, providing the heliocentric side with dynamics, and became the core [signal container](concepts.md#c26) of the new [loop](concepts.md#c31). By around 1700, at the scale of Europe's leading astronomers, the [taiji](concepts.md#c39) ended with the [withdrawal of one side](concepts.md#c40), the geostatic side.

### 6. Inferences, historical facts, empirical assumptions and prohibitions

**Inferences**:

- The [convergence](concepts.md#c37) within the Ptolemaic [loop](concepts.md#c31) and within the Tychonic [loop](concepts.md#c31) is not guaranteed to continue as it was ([renewal](concepts.md#c42): [Law of Flux](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c24), [Law of Non-Restoration](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c28)).
- When the same observation [arrives](concepts.md#c13) at different scholars, their [weights](concepts.md#c06) are not guaranteed to move in the same direction ([coupling](concepts.md#c36)).
- For a given scholar, when the heliocentric [weight](concepts.md#c06) rises, the geostatic [weight](concepts.md#c06) necessarily falls ([cognitive obscuration](concepts.md#c35); this example considers only two directions).

**Historical facts (for calibration only)**:

- After the 1610s, the pure Ptolemaic system quickly lost ground, and the main form of the geostatic side became the Tychonic system.
- The [taiji](concepts.md#c39) ended around 1700 with the [withdrawal of one side](concepts.md#c40), the geostatic side.

**Empirical assumptions**:

- The heliocentric side's [loop](concepts.md#c31) was faster than the traditional [loop](concepts.md#c31), and each of its [outputs](concepts.md#c28) was of high [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49), so it prevailed. That a faster [loop](concepts.md#c31) [arrives](concepts.md#c13) more often at each [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) within the same [clock time](concepts.md#c21) is also an empirical assumption; even if it [arrives](concepts.md#c13) more often, it is not guaranteed to prevail.

**Statements prohibited in this example**:

- "The Ptolemaic system held out until 1700." It was broken after the 1610s; what held out to the end on the geostatic side was the Tychonic system.
- "Heliocentrism replacing geocentrism was inevitable."
- "The old school rejected heliocentrism because it was conservative." This explains an outcome by the essence of a group.
- "After the trial, the new school gave up heliocentrism." One can only say: the [weight](concepts.md#c06) of the new school publicly supporting heliocentrism became lighter.

### 7. Conditions and reuse

**[Empirical prohibitions](concepts.md#c51) distilled from this example** (necessary conditions only; even when all are met, one can only say that the [weight](concepts.md#c06) of the [taiji](concepts.md#c39) ending with the [withdrawal of one side](concepts.md#c40), the old side, has become heavier):

1. **[Signal container](concepts.md#c26)**: if the content of the new side does not enter a [signal container](concepts.md#c26), it will not survive until new [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) join.
2. **Sustained [arrivals](concepts.md#c13) of high [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49)**: if the new side lacks sustained [arrivals](concepts.md#c13) of high [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49), the [taiji](concepts.md#c39) will not end with the [withdrawal of one side](concepts.md#c40), the old side.
3. **The old side lacks matching [arrivals](concepts.md#c13)**: if the old side can keep receiving matching [arrivals](concepts.md#c13) of high [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49), the [taiji](concepts.md#c39) will not end with the [withdrawal of one side](concepts.md#c40), the old side.
4. **[Renewal](concepts.md#c42)** (downgraded; see the revision below): if the [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) of the old side do not [exit](concepts.md#c43) and no new [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) [enter](concepts.md#c44), the [taiji](concepts.md#c39) will not end with the [withdrawal of one side](concepts.md#c40), the old side.

**Reuse tests**:

- **Quantum mechanics (about 1900–1930)**: papers entered a [signal container](concepts.md#c26); [arrivals](concepts.md#c13) of high [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49) such as black-body radiation, the photoelectric effect and atomic spectra kept appearing; classical physics received no matching new [arrivals](concepts.md#c13). Conditions 1 to 3 were all met, and the [taiji](concepts.md#c39) ended in about thirty years. What differed from heliocentrism was the way of ending: classical mechanics did not withdraw, but was absorbed as an approximation of quantum mechanics under particular conditions, an ending by [merging](concepts.md#c41). This does not refute the [empirical prohibitions](concepts.md#c51) above: they only set necessary conditions for "ending with the [withdrawal of one side](concepts.md#c40), the old side", and do not exclude [merging](concepts.md#c41).
- **Plate tectonics (1912–1970s)**: Wegener's hypothesis of continental drift entered a [signal container](concepts.md#c26), so condition 1 was met; but in his lifetime, sustained [arrivals](concepts.md#c13) of high [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49) were lacking, so condition 2 was not met, and the [taiji](concepts.md#c39) did not end, in agreement with condition 2. In the 1950s and 1960s, evidence from paleomagnetism and seafloor spreading appeared in succession; once condition 2 was met, the [taiji](concepts.md#c39) ended within about ten years.

**Revision**: the [taiji](concepts.md#c39) of plate tectonics ended within about ten years, shorter than a generation, and many established geologists changed their positions in their lifetimes. This weakens condition 4: [renewal](concepts.md#c42) affects how fast a [taiji](concepts.md#c39) ends, but it is not a necessary condition. Condition 4 is therefore downgraded from an [empirical prohibition](concepts.md#c51) to an empirical assumption about [impact](concepts.md#c47).

## 9. Example: Drug Resistance in Staphylococcus aureus

### 1. Boundary

- Geography: the hospital systems of Britain and the United States.
- Time: from the 1940s to the 2000s.

### 2. Initial state (early 1940s)

**[Nodes](concepts.md#c25)**:

- [Wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16): the population of *Staphylococcus aureus*, the community of physicians, the drug research institutions.
- [Signal container](concepts.md#c26): penicillin.

**State**: penicillin comes into large-scale use and [arrives](concepts.md#c13) at the staphylococcal population. At this point the great majority of strains are sensitive to it, and almost every [arrival](concepts.md#c13) of penicillin pushes the population in the direction of "disappearing". Physicians, judging by therapeutic effect, form the [impression](concepts.md#c45) that penicillin is "effective", and so use it more often. This is a [positive feedback](concepts.md#c32) [loop](concepts.md#c31).

### 3. New nodes appear

- **Late 1940s**: strains able to break down penicillin spread in hospitals. They became new [nodes](concepts.md#c25) in the population, bringing something that was not there before: when penicillin [arrives](concepts.md#c13) at them, it no longer causes death.
- **1959**: the research institutions introduce methicillin ([signal container](concepts.md#c26)) to deal with these strains.
- **1961**: strains resistant to methicillin as well appear in Britain, later known as methicillin-resistant *Staphylococcus aureus*.
- **Thereafter**: vancomycin is treated as the last line of defense; in 2002, *Staphylococcus aureus* resistant to vancomycin as well appears in the United States.

### 4. Changes in weight

- **[Taiji](concepts.md#c39)**: the research [loop](concepts.md#c31) (a new drug is launched, used, selects resistant bacteria, and research begins again) and the resistance [loop](concepts.md#c31) (the drug [arrives](concepts.md#c13), sensitive bacteria die, resistant bacteria spread) point in opposite directions and are [coupled](concepts.md#c36) with each other, in [divergence](concepts.md#c38) along the dimension "is the drug effective?". Every new drug selects new resistant strains, and every resistant strain forces out a new drug; each side's [outputs](concepts.md#c28) fuel the other.
- **[Loop speed](concepts.md#c34)**: a bacterial generation takes only tens of minutes, while a new drug takes more than ten years from research to market. In contrast to heliocentrism, this time the fast side is the bacteria.
- **[Coupling](concepts.md#c36)**: resistance genes can be transferred between different strains, even different species, through mobile genetic elements. The same [output](concepts.md#c28) [arrives](concepts.md#c13) at many [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), bringing them into [convergence](concepts.md#c37) along the dimension of resistance.
- **[Cognitive obscuration](concepts.md#c35)**: as regards the composition of the population at its next moment, when the [weight](concepts.md#c06) held by resistant strains rises, the [weight](concepts.md#c06) held by sensitive strains necessarily falls.
- **Fitness cost** (empirical): resistance usually has a cost. In environments without the drug, the survival rate of resistant strains is statistically often lower. This is an empirical fact of biology, not derived from [cognitive obscuration](concepts.md#c35).
- **[Renewal](concepts.md#c42)**: each generation of bacteria is a new [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16). They inherit only the genes left by the previous generation, so the composition of the population drifts with every use of the drug.
- **[Impression](concepts.md#c45)**: physicians judge whether a drug is still effective by surveillance statistics. Such estimates are not guaranteed to keep up with the actual changes in the population.
- **Assumption about [impact](concepts.md#c47)**: this example bets on [frequency](concepts.md#c48). The drug is used on a large scale and frequently, and every [arrival](concepts.md#c13) is selecting (empirical assumption).

### 5. Outcome

By the 2000s, the [taiji](concepts.md#c39) had not ended. The research [loop](concepts.md#c31) and the resistance [loop](concepts.md#c31) were still pushing against each other, while the discovery of new classes of antibiotics had clearly slowed. This is a judgment at the scale of the hospital systems of Britain and the United States.

### 6. Inferences, historical facts, empirical assumptions and prohibitions

**Inferences**:

- Resistance is not guaranteed to disappear after the drug is withdrawn, nor guaranteed to persist forever ([renewal](concepts.md#c42): [Law of Flux](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c24), [Law of Non-Restoration](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c28)).
- When sensitive and resistant strains receive the same drug, their results are not guaranteed to be the same ([coupling](concepts.md#c36)).
- Physicians' judgment of a drug's effectiveness is not guaranteed to be complete ([impression](concepts.md#c45)).

**Historical facts (for calibration only)**:

- Methicillin-resistant *Staphylococcus aureus* appeared in 1961; *Staphylococcus aureus* resistant to vancomycin appeared in 2002.
- The [taiji](concepts.md#c39) had still not ended by the 2000s.

**Empirical assumptions**:

- Without the drug, the survival rate of resistant strains is statistically lower, so sensitive strains have a chance to recover after the drug is withdrawn. But resistant strains may mutate again and narrow this gap, so this can only be bet on as [weight](concepts.md#c06).

**Statements prohibited in this example**:

- "As long as the drug is withdrawn, the sensitive bacteria will come back."
- "Once resistance appears, it will never disappear."
- "Humanity will ultimately defeat bacteria", or "bacteria will ultimately win this war". A [taiji](concepts.md#c39) is not guaranteed to end, nor is it guaranteed which side disappears.
- "The bacteria developed resistance in order to survive." It should be written: resistant strains have a statistically higher survival rate in environments with the drug (Explicit Rule 6).

### 7. Conditions and reuse

Testing this example against the [empirical prohibitions](concepts.md#c51) distilled from the heliocentrism example, and considering the outcome "the research side's [withdrawal of one side](concepts.md#c40)":

- **Condition 1 ([signal container](concepts.md#c26))**: resistance genes can reside on mobile genetic elements and be transferred between strains; met.
- **Condition 2 (sustained [arrivals](concepts.md#c13) of high [discrepancy](concepts.md#c49))**: the drug is used on a large scale and frequently, continually selecting the population; met.
- **Condition 3 (the old side lacks matching [arrivals](concepts.md#c13))**: the research side can still keep launching new drugs, that is, it still has matching new [arrivals](concepts.md#c13); not met.

Since condition 3 is not met, the [taiji](concepts.md#c39) will not end with the research side's [withdrawal of one side](concepts.md#c40), in agreement with the historical facts. The slowing discovery of new classes of antibiotics brings condition 3 gradually closer to being met; by Explicit Rule 7, one can only say that the [weight](concepts.md#c06) of this outcome has become heavier, not that it must happen.

This example also shows that [empirical prohibitions](concepts.md#c51) are only necessary conditions: conditions 1 and 2 are both met, yet the [taiji](concepts.md#c39) has still not ended.

### Comparison with the heliocentrism example

Both examples are [taiji](concepts.md#c39) between a fast [loop](concepts.md#c31) and a slow [loop](concepts.md#c31). In heliocentrism the fast one was the loop of facts, and the [taiji](concepts.md#c39) ended around 1700; in drug resistance the fast one is the bacteria, and the [taiji](concepts.md#c39) has not ended to this day. The framework guarantees neither outcome; it only marks them as historical facts.
