## public 幻覺構件 / Illusory Construct

- 定義：幻覺構件是這樣的構件：它的對象不由物理世界給出，卻被用來描述或組織[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)。例如[我們](concepts.md#c04)，「丈夫」這類身分，以及[二階能力](concepts.md#c02)。它有兩種出現方式：一、研究者用它描述複雜的一階[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)，把大量[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)歸成少數[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)；二、它以名稱、[宣誓](concepts.md#c06)、寫進[信號容器](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c26)的分類等形式出現在[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)中；這筆[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)若[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)他人，便參與他人的[權重](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06)。幻覺構件不是[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)：[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)是[渦](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16)的動作，在[歷史](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13)中留下[寫入](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c29)；幻覺構件是[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)的內容，或描述與[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)中所用的構件。稱某構件為幻覺構件，不主張它的對象存在，也不否認。
- Definition: An illusory construct is a construct whose object is not given by the physical world, yet which is used to describe or organize [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28). Examples are [we](concepts.md#c04), identities such as "husband", and [second-order capacity](concepts.md#c02). It appears in two ways: first, a researcher uses it to describe complex first-order outputs, sorting many outputs into a few [distinctions](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18); second, it appears in outputs as a name, an [avowal](concepts.md#c06), or a classification written into a [signal container](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c26); if such an [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at others, it takes part in their [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06). An illusory construct is not a distinction: a distinction is an act of a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) that leaves a [writing](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c29) in [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13); an illusory construct is the content of a distinction, or a construct used in descriptions and outputs. Calling a construct an illusory construct neither asserts nor denies that its object exists.

## public 二階能力 / Second-Order Capacity

- 定義：二階能力是同時具有[二階分別](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c01)與[看法歸屬](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c02)。它是兩者的合稱，不主張兩者是同一種能力；兩者的定義互不以對方為條件。具有二階能力，指能作這兩種[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)，不指每一次[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)都作了。二階能力是[幻覺構件](concepts.md#c01)：它可用來簡稱複雜的一階[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)。
- Definition: Second-order capacity is having both [second-order distinction](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c01) and [view attribution](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c02). It is a joint name for the two and does not assert that they are one capacity; neither definition takes the other as a condition. To have second-order capacity means being able to make these two kinds of [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18), not that it makes them every time. Second-order capacity is an [illusory construct](concepts.md#c01): it can serve as shorthand for complex first-order [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28).

## public 社會人 / Social Person

- 定義：社會人是具有[二階能力](concepts.md#c02)的生物人。社會人是[幻覺構件](concepts.md#c01)：稱某個生物人為社會人，是研究者以該生物人為對象的一次[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)，其材料可以包含[看法歸屬](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c02)，依[配置假定](../paper_ae79e938_76e1_4bf9_b2ab_995c03e16c9e/concepts.md#c01)作出，對講明的階段成立。社會人指單一的生物人；由具有[二階分別](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c01)與[看法歸屬](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c02)的[我](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)及其間的[耦合](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c36)構成的集合，是[二階網路](../paper_ae79e938_76e1_4bf9_b2ab_995c03e16c9e/concepts.md#c04)，不是社會人。
- Definition: A social person is a biological human with [second-order capacity](concepts.md#c02). A social person is an [illusory construct](concepts.md#c01): calling a biological human a social person is a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) the researcher makes with that biological human as object, whose material may include [view attribution](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c02), made under the [configuration assumption](../paper_ae79e938_76e1_4bf9_b2ab_995c03e16c9e/concepts.md#c01) and holding for the stated stage. A social person is a single biological human; the set formed by [selves](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) with [second-order distinction](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c01) and [view attribution](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c02) and the [couplings](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c36) among them is a [second-order network](../paper_ae79e938_76e1_4bf9_b2ab_995c03e16c9e/concepts.md#c04), not a social person.

## public 我們 / We

- 定義：我們是一個[社會人](concepts.md#c03)運用的[幻覺構件](concepts.md#c01)：在某個維度上，把自己和被它歸為[我](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)的其他[呈現](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04)，當成同一邊的一個單位，用來組織它的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)。劃出我們，是一次用到這個構件的[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)；我們的作用，只經由運用它的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)；一個從未在[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)中運用的我們，和沒有劃出，在[記錄](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06)中沒有差別。我們依對象定義：對象包含自己。它是單方面的，屬於運用它的[社會人](concepts.md#c03)，不要求被劃進去的[呈現](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04)也這樣劃。維度由劃的人劃出；同一對[呈現](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04)可以在一個維度上被劃為我們，在另一個維度上不是。歸為[我](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)可以是對一個類的[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)，不要求對類中每個成員都作過[看法歸屬](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c02)，要求的是有[看法歸屬](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c02)的能力，也不保證被歸為[我](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)的對象確實是一個[我](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)。劃出我們，不自動把其餘[呈現](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04)劃為[你們](concepts.md#c05)。
- Definition: We is an [illusory construct](concepts.md#c01) used by a [social person](concepts.md#c03): with which the social person, along some dimension, treats itself and other [presentations](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04) it counts as a [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) as one unit on the same side, to organize its [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28). Drawing we is a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) that uses this construct; we has effect only through output that uses it; a we never used in output does not differ, in the [record](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06), from one never drawn. We is defined by its object: the object includes the one who draws it. It is one-sided and belongs to the social person who uses it; it does not require those drawn in to draw it the same way. The dimension is drawn by the one who draws; the same pair of presentations can be drawn as we along one dimension and not along another. Counting others as selves can be a distinction about a class, without a [view attribution](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c02) for each member, though it requires the capacity for view attribution, and does not guarantee that what is counted as a self really is one. Drawing we does not automatically draw the remaining presentations as [plural you](concepts.md#c05).

## public 你們 / Plural You

- 定義：你們是一個[社會人](concepts.md#c03)運用的[幻覺構件](concepts.md#c01)：在某個維度上，把不含自己、被它歸為[我](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)的其他[呈現](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04)，當成一個單位，用來組織它的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)。劃出你們，是一次用到這個構件的[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)；你們的作用只經由運用它的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)。你們依對象定義：對象不含自己；與[我們](concepts.md#c04)的差別在對象，不在內容。它是單方面的，屬於運用它的[社會人](concepts.md#c03)，不要求被劃進去的[呈現](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04)也這樣劃，也不要求它們劃出任何[我們](concepts.md#c04)。維度由劃的人劃出；歸為[我](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)可以是對一個類的[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)，不要求對類中每個成員都作過[看法歸屬](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c02)，要求的是有[看法歸屬](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c02)的能力，也不保證對象確實是一個[我](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)。
- Definition: Plural you is an [illusory construct](concepts.md#c01) used by a [social person](concepts.md#c03): with which the social person, along some dimension, treats other [presentations](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04) it counts as [selves](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19), excluding itself, as one unit, to organize its [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28). Drawing plural you is a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) that uses this construct; plural you has effect only through output that uses it. Plural you is defined by its object: the object excludes the one who draws it; it differs from [we](concepts.md#c04) in object, not in content. It is one-sided and belongs to the social person who uses it; it does not require those drawn in to draw it the same way, nor that they draw any we. The dimension is drawn by the one who draws; counting others as selves can be a distinction about a class, without a [view attribution](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c02) for each member, though it requires the capacity for view attribution, and does not guarantee that the object really is a self.

## public 宣誓 / Avowal

- 定義：宣誓是一個[社會人](concepts.md#c03)的一筆[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)，以接收者對宣誓者自己的[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)為對象：在某個維度上，要接收者把宣誓者劃進某一類。宣誓只是要求，不是宣誓者自己[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)的讀數：宣誓者有沒有這樣劃、劃得多深，不在[記錄](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06)中；說謊也是宣誓。以對物的[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)為對象的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)不是宣誓；「這是我們的」若以對某物的[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)為對象，不因字面上有「我們」就成為宣誓。把一筆[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)歸為宣誓，是研究者的[看法歸屬](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c02)。
- Definition: An avowal is an [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of a [social person](concepts.md#c03) whose object is the receiver's [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) of the avower: along some dimension, it asks the receiver to place the avower in some class. An avowal is only a request, not a reading of the avower's own distinction: whether and how deeply the avower draws it is not in the [record](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06); a lie is also an avowal. An output whose object is a distinction about a thing is not an avowal; "this is ours", if its object is a distinction about some thing, does not become an avowal merely because its wording contains "our". Classifying an output as an avowal is a researcher's [view attribution](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c02).

## public 對物的歸屬分別 / Belonging Distinction of Things

- 定義：對物的歸屬分別是一個[社會人](concepts.md#c03)作出的一次[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)：在某個維度上，以已經[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)它的某物之[呈現](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04)為材料，把該物劃為「我的」「你的」「我們的」或「你們的」。這四種說法是這次[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)所用的[幻覺構件](concepts.md#c01)，作用只經由運用它們的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)，不是物本身具有的權利關係；它參照哪個[我們](concepts.md#c04)或[你們](concepts.md#c05)，屬於[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)的內容。被劃為「我們的」的物，不因此成為另一個[我](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)。對物的歸屬分別不推出誰能使用、處置或分配該物，也不要求先劃出[我們](concepts.md#c04)才作出；同一件物可以在不同維度上被同一[社會人](concepts.md#c03)作出不同[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)。
- Definition: A belonging distinction of things is a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) made by a [social person](concepts.md#c03): along some dimension, taking the [presentation](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04) of a thing that has [arrived](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at it as material, it classes the thing as "mine", "yours", "ours", or "yours (plural)". These four are [illusory constructs](concepts.md#c01) this distinction uses, having effect only through [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) that uses them, not rights the thing itself carries; which [we](concepts.md#c04) or [plural you](concepts.md#c05) it refers to belongs to the content of the distinction. A thing classed as "ours" does not thereby become another [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19). A belonging distinction of things does not imply who may use, dispose of, or allocate the thing, and does not require that a we be drawn first; the same social person can make different distinctions about the same thing along different dimensions.

## public 分別邊際 / Distinction Margin

- 定義：分別邊際是一個[社會人](concepts.md#c03)在某個維度上作出[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)，使對象在它的[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)中落在不同一邊。把某些[呈現](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04)劃為[我們](concepts.md#c04)、另一些劃為[你們](concepts.md#c05)，或把物劃為「我們的」「你們的」（[對物的歸屬分別](concepts.md#c07)），都是分別邊際的例子。分別邊際只屬於作出它的[社會人](concepts.md#c03)，不同[社會人](concepts.md#c03)可以對同一批人、同一件物劃出不同的邊際；它不要求兩邊都有名稱。說[經濟模式](../paper_d244789a_9261_4a0d_a98a_feb8db56b511/concepts.md#c09)出現分別邊際，指[社會人](concepts.md#c03)得以在該模式已[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)它的材料上另作[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)，不是[經濟模式](../paper_d244789a_9261_4a0d_a98a_feb8db56b511/concepts.md#c09)取得一個共有的內部[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)。分別邊際只合稱既有的[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)，它對他人與物的作用經由[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)與[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)。
- Definition: A distinction margin is a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) a [social person](concepts.md#c03) makes along some dimension, so that objects fall on different sides within its distinction. Drawing some [presentations](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04) as [we](concepts.md#c04) and others as [plural you](concepts.md#c05), or classing things as "ours" or "yours (plural)" ([belonging distinction of things](concepts.md#c07)), are examples. A distinction margin belongs only to the social person who makes it; different social persons can draw different margins over the same people and things; it does not require both sides to have names. To say an [economic mode](../paper_d244789a_9261_4a0d_a98a_feb8db56b511/concepts.md#c09) shows a distinction margin means a social person is able to make a further distinction on material from the mode that has arrived at it, not that the mode acquires a shared internal distinction. A distinction margin only names existing distinctions jointly; its effect on others and on things goes through [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) and [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10).

## public 內外差異 / Inside Outside Difference

- 定義：內外差異是研究者在[記錄](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06)中作出的一次[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)：同類的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)，在某組參與者之間，與在該組參與者和組外參與者之間，有所不同。哪些[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)算同類、哪些參與者算一組、在哪個維度上比較，由研究者在講明的[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)下劃出；換一種[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)，內外差異可以不同。研究者劃的組，與任何[社會人](concepts.md#c03)劃的[我們](concepts.md#c04)，範圍與維度不必一致。同類的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)以某些物為對象時與以其他物為對象時有所不同，也是內外差異。內外差異只描述[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)的不同，不是規則、權利或制度。
- Definition: An inside outside difference is a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) a researcher makes in the [record](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06): outputs of the same kind differ between members of some group and between those members and participants outside it. Which [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) count as the same kind, which participants form a group, and along which dimension they are compared are drawn by the researcher under a stated [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23); under another cut the difference can differ. The researcher's group need not match, in range or dimension, any [we](concepts.md#c04) drawn by any [social person](concepts.md#c03). Outputs of the same kind that differ when directed at some things rather than others also form an inside outside difference. It only describes differences in output; it is not a rule, a right, or an institution.

## public 宣稱界線 / Claimed Boundary

- 定義：宣稱界線是研究者在[記錄](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06)中作出的一次[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)：依一筆以分類為內容的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)，劃出它所提出的界線。這類[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)包括[宣誓](concepts.md#c06)、對人或對物的分類聲明，以及寫進[信號容器](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c26)的分類，例如法條、證書、規章。一筆[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)提出了哪條界線，由研究者依該[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)劃出，並在講明的[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)下判定；它不是[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)者[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)的讀數。宣稱界線與[內外差異](concepts.md#c09)都依[記錄](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06)中的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)劃出，不是被研究的[社會人](concepts.md#c03)的[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)。
- Definition: A claimed boundary is a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) a researcher makes in the [record](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06): from an [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) whose content is a classification, it draws the boundary that output proposes. Such outputs include [avowals](concepts.md#c06), classificatory statements about people or things, and classifications written into a [signal container](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c26), such as statutes, certificates, and regulations. Which boundary an output proposes is drawn by the researcher from that output and judged under a stated [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23); it is not a reading of the outputter's distinction. Claimed boundaries and [inside outside differences](concepts.md#c09) are both drawn from [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) in the [record](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06), and are not [distinctions](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) of the [social persons](concepts.md#c03) studied.

## public 界線的移動 / Boundary Shift

- 定義：界線的移動是研究者在[記錄](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06)中作出的一次[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)：比較兩個[檢查窗口](../paper_d244789a_9261_4a0d_a98a_feb8db56b511/concepts.md#c03)，把[內外差異](concepts.md#c09)或[宣稱界線](concepts.md#c10)在兩者之間的不同，記為界線的移動。移動在[記錄](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06)中的結構可以依[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)描述，例如兩邊有差別的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)互相強化；多人有差別的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)由相容轉為不相容，[內外差異](concepts.md#c09)變淡；某個參與者所受的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)，由一邊的樣子轉為另一邊的樣子；[宣稱界線](concepts.md#c10)與[內外差異](concepts.md#c09)之間的距離拉長或縮短。在講明的維度上，這些結構可以用[趨同](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c37)、[趨異](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c38)、[持平](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c52)、[太極](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c39)與[合流](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c41)描述。同一段[記錄](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06)換一種[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)，可以記為移動，也可以記為不動。
- Definition: A boundary shift is a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) a researcher makes in the [record](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06): comparing two [check windows](../paper_d244789a_9261_4a0d_a98a_feb8db56b511/concepts.md#c03), it records the difference in an [inside outside difference](concepts.md#c09) or a [claimed boundary](concepts.md#c10) between them as a boundary shift. The structure of a shift in the record can be described by [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28), for example: differential outputs on the two sides reinforce each other; differential outputs of many participants turn from compatible to incompatible, and the inside outside difference fades; the outputs a participant receives change from those of one side to those of the other; the distance between a claimed boundary and an inside outside difference lengthens or shortens. Along a stated dimension, these structures can be described as [convergence](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c37), [divergence](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c38), [stasis](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c52), [taiji](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c39), and [merging](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c41). Under another [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23), the same stretch of record can be recorded as a shift or as no shift.
