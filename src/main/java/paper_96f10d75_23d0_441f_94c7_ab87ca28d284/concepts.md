## public 生產 / Production

- 定義：[生產](concepts.md#c01)是研究者對[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)[歷史](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13)所作的一種[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)：在講明的[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)下，把某段[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)看作[生產](concepts.md#c01)。[生產](concepts.md#c01)不賦予[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)獨立本體。
- Definition: Production is a distinction a researcher draws over the output history: under a stated way of cutting, a stretch of output is viewed as production. It gives output no independent ontology.

## public 狀態變數 / State Variable

- 定義：研究者在講明的[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)下，為[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)指定的一種可記錄變數，用來描述該[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)下一刻的樣子，並供[退場判準](concepts.md#c04)表述。什麼算狀態，隨[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)邊界而定，是[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)下的歸類，不是事物本身的性質；常以「在該[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)的視角下不可轉移、不可儲存」作為辨認依據，但這只是依據，不是定義。《生產動力學》這篇論文的記錄只涵蓋物理改變；[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)（對自己或他人的看法）會進入[歷史](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13)，但不在《生產動力學》的記錄範圍內。
- Definition: A recordable variable that a researcher, under a stated way of cutting, assigns to a node to describe how it is at the next moment and in which the exit criterion is expressed. What counts as state depends on the node boundary; it is a classification under a cut, not a property of the thing itself. Non-transferability and non-storability from the node's viewpoint are common cues, not the definition. The records of the paper 'Production Dynamics' cover only physical changes; distinctions (views about oneself or others) enter history but fall outside what 'Production Dynamics' records.

## public 資源變數 / Resource Variable

- 定義：研究者在講明的[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)下，為[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)指定的一種可記錄變數，其值在每個[推演輪](concepts.md#c07)中可增可減，由實際事件決定。什麼算資源，隨[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)邊界而定，是[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)下的歸類，不是事物本身的性質；常以「在該[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)的視角下可轉移、可儲存」作為辨認依據，但這只是依據，不是定義。記錄[資源變數](concepts.md#c03)的增減，不預設不同[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)的增減對應同一份東西。《生產動力學》這篇論文的記錄只涵蓋物理改變；[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)（對自己或他人的看法）會進入[歷史](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13)，但不在《生產動力學》的記錄範圍內。
- Definition: A recordable variable that a researcher, under a stated way of cutting, assigns to a node, whose value may increase or decrease in each derivation round according to actual events. What counts as a resource depends on the node boundary; it is a classification under a cut, not a property of the thing itself. Transferability and storability from the node's viewpoint are common cues, not the definition. Recording increases and decreases presupposes no correspondence between different nodes' changes. The records of the paper 'Production Dynamics' cover only physical changes; distinctions (views about oneself or others) enter history but fall outside what 'Production Dynamics' records.

## public 退場判準 / Exit Criterion

- 定義：研究者在講明的[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)下，用[狀態變數](concepts.md#c02)表述的一條準則，用來判定[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)在某個[推演輪](concepts.md#c07)中是否[判準退場](concepts.md#c09)。判準是研究者的一次[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)，是定義；是否[判準退場](concepts.md#c09)是該[推演輪](concepts.md#c07)實際的狀態事件，是事實，兩者不互相替代。判準可以是上游所稱的「不再是[渦](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16)」，也可以是研究者明示的其他定義。[判準退場](concepts.md#c09)是《生產動力學》自己的概念，指依研究者所訂判準記為退出；它與上游的[退場](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c43)（不再是[渦](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16)）不同，兩者可以一致，也可以不一致。
- Definition: A criterion, expressed in state variables under a stated way of cutting, by which a researcher decides whether a node undergoes criterion exit in a given derivation round. The criterion is a distinction by the researcher and hence a definition; whether the node actually undergoes criterion exit is a fact about the state events of that derivation round. The two do not substitute for each other. The criterion may be the upstream 'no longer a vortex' or any other explicitly stated definition. Criterion exit is the own concept of 'Production Dynamics': being recorded as leaving under the researcher's stated criterion. It differs from the upstream exit (no longer a vortex); the two may coincide or not.

## public 解 / Solution

- 定義：研究者在講明的[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)下，記錄的各[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)下一刻的[狀態變數](concepts.md#c02)、[資源變數](concepts.md#c03)，以及依[退場判準](concepts.md#c04)所記的是否[判準退場](concepts.md#c09)，合起來是該[推演輪](concepts.md#c07)的[解](concepts.md#c05)。[判準退場](concepts.md#c09)只是[解](concepts.md#c05)的一個維度，與其他維度並列；沒有記錄的東西不在[解](concepts.md#c05)裡，但仍在[歷史](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13)中。《生產動力學》這篇論文的記錄只涵蓋物理改變；[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)（對自己或他人的看法）會進入[歷史](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13)，但不在《生產動力學》的記錄範圍內。
- Definition: The recorded next-moment state variables and resource variables of the nodes under a stated way of cutting, together with whether each node undergoes criterion exit according to the exit criterion, taken together form the solution of that derivation round. Criterion exit is only one dimension of the solution, alongside the others; what is not recorded is not in the solution but remains in history. The records of the paper 'Production Dynamics' cover only physical changes; distinctions (views about oneself or others) enter history but fall outside what 'Production Dynamics' records.

## public 推演輪 / Derivation Round

- 定義：[推演輪](concepts.md#c07)是研究者在講明的[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)下，把一個模型往前推進一次的單位：在場的各[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)各自走一次上游的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)、[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)、入[歷史](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13)的循環，再加上研究者所作的記錄，以及成員依[判準退場](concepts.md#c09)與[進場](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c44)所作的成員變動。單位是整個切出的範圍，不是單一[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)。記錄的內容與成員變動的規則由使用者指定，不假定各[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)同步，[推演輪](concepts.md#c07)只是排序。
- Definition: A derivation round is the unit by which a researcher, under a stated way of cutting, advances a model by one step: each node present runs once through the upstream loop of output, arrival and entry into history, after which the researcher makes records and the membership changes through criterion exit and entry. The unit is the whole cut-out range, not a single node. What is recorded and how membership changes are specified by the user; nodes are not assumed synchronous, and the round is only an ordering.

## public 判準退場 / Criterion Exit

- 定義：在講明的[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)下，依研究者所訂的[退場判準](concepts.md#c04)，某[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)在某個[推演輪](concepts.md#c07)被記為退出。[判準退場](concepts.md#c09)是《生產動力學》自己的概念，不等於上游的[退場](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c43)（不再是[渦](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16)）：判準可以取上游的「不再是[渦](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16)」，也可以取退休、離職、公司解散或生理界線等其他明示定義，所以兩者可以一致，也可以不一致。是否[判準退場](concepts.md#c09)是該[推演輪](concepts.md#c07)實際的狀態事件，是事實；判準本身是定義。
- Definition: Criterion exit is the recording of a node as leaving in a given derivation round according to the exit criterion the researcher has stated under a stated way of cutting. It is the own concept of 'Production Dynamics' and is not the upstream exit (no longer a vortex): the criterion may be 'no longer a vortex' or any other explicit definition such as retirement, resignation, dissolution or a physiological threshold, so the two may coincide or not. Whether criterion exit occurs is a fact about the state events of that derivation round; the criterion itself is a definition.
