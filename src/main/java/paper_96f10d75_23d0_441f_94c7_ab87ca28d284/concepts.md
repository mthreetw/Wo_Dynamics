## public 生產 / Production

- 定義：[生產](concepts.md#c01)是研究者對[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)[歷史](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13)所作的一種[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)：在講明的[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)下，把某段[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)看作[生產](concepts.md#c01)。[生產](concepts.md#c01)不賦予[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)獨立本體。
- Definition: [Production](concepts.md#c01) is a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) a researcher draws over the [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13): under a stated way of [cutting](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23), a stretch of [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) is viewed as [production](concepts.md#c01). It gives [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) no independent ontology.

## public 狀態變數 / State Variable

- 定義：研究者在講明的[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)下，為[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)指定的一種可記錄變數，用來描述該[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)下一刻的樣子，並供[退場判準](concepts.md#c04)表述。什麼算狀態，隨[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)邊界而定，是[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)下的歸類，不是事物本身的性質；常以「在該[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)的視角下不可轉移、不可儲存」作為辨認依據，但這只是依據，不是定義。
- Definition: A recordable variable that a researcher, under a stated way of [cutting](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23), assigns to a [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) to describe how it is at the next moment and in which the [exit criterion](concepts.md#c04) is expressed. What counts as state depends on the [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) boundary; it is a classification under a [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23), not a property of the thing itself. Non-transferability and non-storability from the [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)'s viewpoint are common cues, not the definition.

## public 資源變數 / Resource Variable

- 定義：研究者在講明的[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)下，為[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)指定的一種可記錄變數，其值在每個[推演輪](concepts.md#c07)中可增可減，由實際事件決定。什麼算資源，隨[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)邊界而定，是[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)下的歸類，不是事物本身的性質；常以「在該[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)的視角下可轉移、可儲存」作為辨認依據，但這只是依據，不是定義。記錄[資源變數](concepts.md#c03)的增減，不預設不同[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)的增減對應同一份東西。
- Definition: A recordable variable that a researcher, under a stated way of [cutting](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23), assigns to a [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25), whose value may increase or decrease in each [derivation round](concepts.md#c07) according to actual events. What counts as a resource depends on the [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) boundary; it is a classification under a [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23), not a property of the thing itself. Transferability and storability from the [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)'s viewpoint are common cues, not the definition. Recording increases and decreases of a [resource variable](concepts.md#c03) presupposes no correspondence between different [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)' changes.

## public 退場判準 / Exit Criterion

- 定義：研究者在講明的[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)下，用[狀態變數](concepts.md#c02)表述的一條準則，用來判定[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)在某個[推演輪](concepts.md#c07)中是否[判準退場](concepts.md#c09)。判準是研究者的一次[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)，是定義；是否[判準退場](concepts.md#c09)是該[推演輪](concepts.md#c07)實際的狀態事件，是事實，兩者不互相替代。判準可以是上游所稱的「不再是[渦](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16)」，也可以是研究者明示的其他定義。[判準退場](concepts.md#c09)是《生產動力學》自己的概念，指依研究者所訂判準記為退出；它與上游的[退場](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c43)（不再是[渦](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16)）不同，兩者可以一致，也可以不一致。
- Definition: A criterion, expressed in [state variables](concepts.md#c02) under a stated way of [cutting](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23), by which a researcher decides whether a [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) undergoes [criterion exit](concepts.md#c09) in a given [derivation round](concepts.md#c07). The criterion is a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) by the researcher and hence a definition; whether the [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) actually undergoes [criterion exit](concepts.md#c09) is a fact about the state events of that [derivation round](concepts.md#c07). The two do not substitute for each other. The criterion may be the upstream 'no longer a [vortex](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16)' or any other explicitly stated definition. [Criterion exit](concepts.md#c09) is the own concept of 'Production Dynamics': being recorded as leaving under the researcher's stated criterion. It differs from the upstream [exit](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c43) (no longer a [vortex](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16)); the two may coincide or not.

## public 解 / Solution

- 定義：研究者在講明的[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)下，記錄的各[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)下一刻的[狀態變數](concepts.md#c02)、[資源變數](concepts.md#c03)，以及依[退場判準](concepts.md#c04)所記的是否[判準退場](concepts.md#c09)，合起來是該[推演輪](concepts.md#c07)的[解](concepts.md#c05)。[判準退場](concepts.md#c09)只是[解](concepts.md#c05)的一個維度，與其他維度並列；沒有記錄的東西不在[解](concepts.md#c05)裡，但仍在[歷史](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13)中。
- Definition: The recorded next-moment [state variables](concepts.md#c02) and [resource variables](concepts.md#c03) of the [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) under a stated way of [cutting](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23), together with whether each [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) undergoes [criterion exit](concepts.md#c09) according to the [exit criterion](concepts.md#c04), taken together form the [solution](concepts.md#c05) of that [derivation round](concepts.md#c07). [Criterion exit](concepts.md#c09) is only one dimension of the [solution](concepts.md#c05), alongside the others; what is not recorded is not in the [solution](concepts.md#c05) but remains in [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13).

## public 推演輪 / Derivation Round

- 定義：[推演輪](concepts.md#c07)是研究者在講明的[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)下，把一個模型往前推進一次的單位：在場的各[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)各自走一次上游的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)、[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)、入[歷史](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13)的循環，再加上研究者所作的記錄，以及成員依[判準退場](concepts.md#c09)與[進場](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c44)所作的成員變動。單位是整個切出的範圍，不是單一[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)。記錄的內容與成員變動的規則由使用者指定，不假定各[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)同步，[推演輪](concepts.md#c07)只是排序。
- Definition: A [derivation round](concepts.md#c07) is the unit by which a researcher, under a stated way of [cutting](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23), advances a model by one step: each [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) present runs once through the upstream loop of [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28), [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) and entry into [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13), after which the researcher makes records and the membership changes through [criterion exit](concepts.md#c09) and [entry](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c44). The unit is the whole cut-out range, not a single [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25). What is recorded and how membership changes are specified by the user; [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) are not assumed synchronous, and the [round](concepts.md#c07) is only an ordering.

## public 判準退場 / Criterion Exit

- 定義：在講明的[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)下，依研究者所訂的[退場判準](concepts.md#c04)，某[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)在某個[推演輪](concepts.md#c07)被記為退出。[判準退場](concepts.md#c09)是《生產動力學》自己的概念，不等於上游的[退場](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c43)（不再是[渦](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16)）：判準可以取上游的「不再是[渦](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16)」，也可以取退休、離職、公司解散或生理界線等其他明示定義，所以兩者可以一致，也可以不一致。是否[判準退場](concepts.md#c09)是該[推演輪](concepts.md#c07)實際的狀態事件，是事實；判準本身是定義。
- Definition: [Criterion exit](concepts.md#c09) is the recording of a [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) as leaving in a given [derivation round](concepts.md#c07) according to the [exit criterion](concepts.md#c04) the researcher has stated under a stated way of [cutting](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23). It is the own concept of 'Production Dynamics' and is not the upstream [exit](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c43) (no longer a [vortex](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16)): the criterion may be 'no longer a [vortex](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16)' or any other explicit definition such as retirement, resignation, dissolution or a physiological threshold, so the two may coincide or not. Whether [criterion exit](concepts.md#c09) occurs is a fact about the state events of that [derivation round](concepts.md#c07); the criterion itself is a definition.
