## public 配置假定 / Configuration Assumption

- 定義：配置假定是對一組具體對象所作的假定：在講明的階段，其中某些對象只具有[一階分別](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c24)，其餘對象有[二階分別](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c01)，也有[看法歸屬](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c02)。配置假定不是定理。從外部確認某個對象某一刻有沒有[二階分別](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c01)，[不被保證](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c02)；只有找到機制，證明該對象的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)只能由[鎖死的回合](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c25)落出，或只能由[隨機的回合](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c26)落出，「對象具有二階分別」這一半的假定對它才不成立。
- Definition: A configuration assumption is an assumption made about a set of concrete objects: at a stated stage, some of the objects have only [First-Order Distinction](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c24), and the others have [Second-Order Distinction](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c01) and also [View Attribution](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c02). A configuration assumption is not a theorem. Confirming from outside that an object has [second-order distinction](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c01) at some moment is [Not Guaranteed](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c02); the half of the assumption that the object has second-order distinction fails for that object only if a mechanism is found showing that its [Outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) can come only from [locked-in rounds](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c25) or only from [random rounds](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c26).
- 裁決：2026-10-13 作者裁決：概念表登記預設為 public。

## public 外生背景 / Exogenous Background

- 定義：外生背景是在講明的時窗與[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)下，視為從模型外部進入、不隨模型中的[迴路](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c31)更新的背景。它是範圍上的設定，不主張背景不變；進入模型的是背景，不是各個[我](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)從中取得的內容；每個[我](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)只拿得到[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)它自己的那一部分，各個[我](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)取得的內容可以不同。
- Definition: Exogenous background is a background that, under a stated time window and a stated [Cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23), is treated as entering the model from outside and as not being updated by the [Loops](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c31) in the model. It is a scope setting and does not claim that the background is unchanging; what enters the model is the background, not what each [Self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) draws from it; each [Self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) gets only the part that [Arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at it, and what each [Self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) obtains may differ.

## public 驅動力不可確認 / Unconfirmability of Drives

- 定義：驅動力不可確認是這個結論：對不被[鎖死](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12)的對象，它的驅動力，也就是動機、需求與目的，從外部不能被完整觀測，不能被對象自己的自述確認，旁人事後對動機的判斷也只是[看法歸屬](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c02)或[印象](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c45)，不能算證實；動機本身不憑自身[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)旁人。適用範圍：一切不被[鎖死](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12)的對象，不分年齡，也不分有無[二階分別](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c01)；從外部無法確認對象是否被[鎖死](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12)，只能假定。
- Definition: The Unconfirmability of Drives is the conclusion that the drive of an object that is not in [Lock-in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12), meaning its motive, need, or purpose, cannot be completely observed from outside, cannot be confirmed by the object's own account, and a judgment about the motive made by others afterwards is only a [View Attribution](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c02) or an [Impression](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c45) and does not count as verification; the motive itself does not [Arrive](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at others by itself. Scope: every object that is not in [Lock-in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12), of any age, whether or not it has [Second-Order Distinction](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c01); whether an object is in [Lock-in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12) cannot be confirmed from outside and can only be assumed.
- 裁決：2026-10-13 作者裁決：概念表登記預設為 public。

## public 二階網路 / Second-Order Network

- 定義：二階網路是由具有[二階分別](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c01)與[看法歸屬](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c02)的[我](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)，以及它們之間的[耦合](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c36)所構成的集合：一個[我](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)對另一個[我](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)作[看法歸屬](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c02)，而不只把它當成一筆[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)來反應；每個參與者在參與的那一刻，既有[二階分別](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c01)，也有[看法歸屬](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c02)，兩者缺一不可。參與者至少兩個，但不必同時在場：寫下內容的一方，在寫下的那一刻是參與者，之後[退場](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c43)不影響這一點。網路在[歷史](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13)中形成的規律，包括規範與制度，是它的模式，不是另一類實體。
- Definition: A second-order network is the set formed by [Selves](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) that have [Second-Order Distinction](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c01) and [View Attribution](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c02), together with the [Couplings](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c36) among them: one [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) makes a [view attribution](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c02) on another [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)'s [Output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28), rather than only reacting to it as an [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28); every participant, at the moment of taking part, has both [second-order distinction](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c01) and [view attribution](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c02), and neither alone suffices. There are at least two participants, who need not be present at the same time: a party who writes content is a participant at the moment of writing, and a later [Exit](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c43) does not change that. The regularities that form in the network over [History](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13), including norms and institutions, are its patterns and not a separate kind of entity.
- 裁決：2026-10-13 作者裁決：概念表登記預設為 public。二階網路視為新概念，不主張與《三重幻覺與被迫的賭注》中 private 的社會等同，也不寫兩者的關係。參與者更替前後是否仍是同一個網路，本篇不給判準。

## public 專屬歷史 / Dedicated History

- 定義：專屬歷史是兩個[我](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)之間，在一個講明的維度上，彼此互相[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)所構成的那一段[歷史](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13)，只屬於這兩者。
- Definition: A dedicated history is the part of [History](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13), between two [Selves](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) along a stated dimension, that is formed by the [Outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) that [arrive](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at each other; it belongs only to these two.

## public 默契 / Tacit Coordination

- 定義：默契是在指定的情境類型與維度中，兩個[我](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)經歷反覆的[專屬歷史](concepts.md#c05)，在可比較的情境下，呈現不必每次重新逐項指導、仍能相容接續的行為模式。判準是[歷史](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13)與行為模式，不是預言：它不要求任何一方事先算準對方的下一步，不要求友善，不要求彼此透明，也不要求一方對另一方的[印象](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c45)穩定。互動次數多，不等於已有默契。
- Definition: Tacit coordination is the behavioral pattern shown when, in a specified type of situation and along a specified dimension, two [Selves](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) have gone through a repeated [Dedicated History](concepts.md#c05), and in comparable situations can continue compatibly without being instructed item by item each time. The criterion is [History](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) and behavioral pattern, not prediction: it requires neither that either side calculate the other's next step beforehand, nor friendliness, nor mutual transparency, nor stability of one side's [Impression](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c45) of the other. A large number of interactions does not by itself amount to tacit coordination.
- 裁決：2026-10-13 作者裁決：概念表登記預設為 public。默契是一個概念；反覆的專屬歷史、可相容接續的行為模式、以及不要求預言友善透明與印象穩定，都是同一個判準的部分，不拆開。

## public 指定協調 / Specified Coordination

- 定義：指定協調是這樣一種判準：對一項已界定的任務，依明列的驗收條件，檢查某段實際發生的行動序列；序列符合驗收條件，稱這段序列在該任務上呈現協調。任務是研究者指定的判準，不是參與者驅動力的代名詞；成功也不等於關係改善或彼此理解。
- Definition: Specified coordination is a criterion: for a defined task, the actual sequence of actions that took place is checked against explicitly stated acceptance conditions; a sequence that meets them is said to show coordination on that task. The task is a criterion specified by the researcher and is not a stand-in for the participants' drives; success does not imply improved relations or mutual understanding.

## public 失配 / Mismatch

- 定義：失配是在研究者指定的維度與相容判準下，兩個[我](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)相鄰的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)序列不相容。判準看的是序列相容與否，不是任何一方想要而沒得到，也不是期待被違反。
- Definition: A mismatch is, along a researcher-specified dimension and under a specified compatibility criterion, an incompatibility between the adjacent sequences of [Outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of two [Selves](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19). The criterion concerns whether the sequence is compatible, not whether either party wanted something and did not get it, nor whether an expectation was violated.

## public 修復 / Repair

- 定義：修復是[失配](concepts.md#c08)之後，在同一維度與同一相容判準下，後續[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)序列重新相容。修復只是序列的記錄，不表示歸屬得到了檢驗，也不表示任何一方得到了想要的，也不表示關係改善。
- Definition: A repair is, after a [Mismatch](concepts.md#c08), a renewed compatibility of the subsequent sequence of [Outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) along the same dimension and under the same compatibility criterion. A repair is only a record of the sequence; it does not mean that the attribution was tested, that either party got what it wanted, or that relations improved.

## public 衝突 / Conflict

- 定義：衝突是在研究者指定的維度與時窗內，兩個[我](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)之間形成的[太極](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c39)：雙方的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)都針對對方在該維度上的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)，方向相反，而且每一方的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)對方，都強化對方自己的方向。至少一方的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)已[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)對方（含經由轉述），才可能形成。各做各的相反方向、沒有針對對方的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)，不是衝突。衝突是對序列的描述，不是對任何一方內在狀態或驅動力的描述。
- Definition: A conflict is, along a researcher-specified dimension and within a specified time window, a [Taiji](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c39) formed between two [Selves](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19): each side's [Output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) is aimed at the other's [Output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) along that dimension, in opposite directions, and each side's [Output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) [arriving](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at the other strengthens the other's own direction. It can form only after the [Output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of at least one has [arrived](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at the other (including through relay). Opposite directions pursued independently, without aiming at the other's [Output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28), are not a conflict. A conflict is a description of a sequence, not of any party's inner state or drive.

- 裁決：2026-10-15 作者裁決：衝突重新定義為太極型的結構（方向相反、互相針對、互相強化），不再用「序列持續不相容」或「距離持續趨異」；失配（一方沒接上）與趨異（只是距離變長）都不等於衝突。
