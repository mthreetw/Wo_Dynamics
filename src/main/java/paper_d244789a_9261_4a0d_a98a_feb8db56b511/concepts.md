## public 存續判準 / Persistence Criterion

- 定義：研究者為某個要追蹤的結構（例如[人群圖](concepts.md#c05)、[經濟模式](concepts.md#c09)）講明的判準，用來判定它在各個[檢查窗口](concepts.md#c03)中是否再次出現，包括[同一性條件](concepts.md#c02)與[檢查窗口](concepts.md#c03)兩部分。判準是研究者的定義；是否再次出現是該[檢查窗口](concepts.md#c03)實際發生的事實，兩者不互相替代。
- Definition: A criterion that a researcher states for a structure to be tracked (for example a [Population Graph](concepts.md#c05) or an [Economic Mode](concepts.md#c09)), used to decide whether it appears again in each [check window](concepts.md#c03). It has two parts: an [Identity Condition](concepts.md#c02) and a [Check Window](concepts.md#c03). The criterion is the researcher's definition; whether the structure actually reappears in a [check window](concepts.md#c03) is a fact, and neither replaces the other.

<a id="c02"></a>
## public 同一性條件 / Identity Condition

- 定義：[存續判準](concepts.md#c01)的一部分：研究者講明，在什麼等價條件下，不同[檢查窗口](concepts.md#c03)中的結構算作同一個。
- Definition: Part of a [Persistence Criterion](concepts.md#c01): the researcher states under what equivalence the structures in different [check windows](concepts.md#c03) count as the same one.

<a id="c03"></a>
## public 檢查窗口 / Check Window

- 定義：[存續判準](concepts.md#c01)的一部分：研究者講明，在哪些窗口中檢查某個結構是否再次出現。不同的結構各依自己的檢查窗口判定，不假定共同的輪次。
- Definition: Part of a [Persistence Criterion](concepts.md#c01): the researcher states in which windows a structure is checked for reappearing. Different structures are judged by their own check windows; no common round is assumed.

<a id="c04"></a>
## public 外界 / Exterior

- 定義：對某個[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)而言，自己以外的一切[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)來源，包含其他[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)與[自然環境](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c16)。外界是相對的，換一個[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)，外界就不同；[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)的大小由[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)決定，可以是一個人，也可以是一間公司；在一張[輸出圖](concepts.md#c06)中，[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)以外的一切都是這張圖的外界。
- Definition: For a given [Node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25), every source of [Arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) other than itself, including other [Node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) and the [Natural Environment](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c16). The exterior is relative: a different [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) has a different exterior. The size of a [Node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) is set by the [Cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23); it can be a person or a company. In an [Output Graph](concepts.md#c06), everything outside the [Cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23) is the exterior of that graph.

<a id="c05"></a>
## public 人群圖 / Population Graph

- 定義：在講明的[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)下，生物人[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)，以及它們之間的[邊](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c29)（一個[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)，參與另一個[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)的[權重](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06)），加上各[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)自己的[迴路](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c31)（自己的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)，經由自己，又參與自己的[權重](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06)）所成的靜態圖。人群圖的[存續判準](concepts.md#c01)由研究者講明，方式同[經濟模式](concepts.md#c09)。
- Definition: A static graph made, under a stated [Cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23), of human [Node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25), the [Edge](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c29) between them (the [Output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of one [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) takes part in the [Weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) of another [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)), and each [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)'s own [Loop](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c31) (its own [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28), through itself, takes part in its own [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06)). Its [Persistence Criterion](concepts.md#c01) is stated by the researcher in the same way as for an [Economic Mode](concepts.md#c09).

## public 輸出圖 / Output Graph

- 定義：在[人群圖](concepts.md#c05)的基礎上，在講明的[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)、維度與時窗內，把[記錄](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06)附在點與[邊](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c29)上所得的圖：點是[人群圖](concepts.md#c05)的[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)，加上[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)內的[信號容器](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c26)與[隨機源](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c27)；[邊](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c29)是[人群圖](concepts.md#c05)的[邊](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c29)，加上這些[信號容器](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c26)、[隨機源](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c27)與其他[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)之間的[邊](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c29)；各[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)[記錄](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06)附在點上，各次[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)的[記錄](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06)附在[邊](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c29)上，或附在[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)自己的[迴路](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c31)上，來自[外界](concepts.md#c04)的[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)附在接收的點上；另外附上研究者對某[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)某[路徑](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07)的[前件](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08)是否具備所作的判定，可以標「未知」。這項判定與[記錄](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06)並列附上，不算[記錄](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06)。[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)內的[信號容器](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c26)與[隨機源](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c27)也是[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)，與它們的[邊](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c29)一併記入。
- Definition: A graph obtained by attaching [Record](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06) to points and [edges](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c29) on top of a [Population Graph](concepts.md#c05), within a stated [Cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23), dimension and time window. The points are the [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) of the [population graph](concepts.md#c05), plus the [Signal Container](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c26) and [Random Source](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c27) within the [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23); the [edges](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c29) are the [edges](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c29) of the [population graph](concepts.md#c05), plus the [edges](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c29) between these [signal containers](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c26) or [random sources](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c27) and other [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25). A [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)'s [Output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) [records](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06) are attached to its point; [records](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06) of each [Arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) are attached to [edges](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c29) or to the [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)'s own [Loop](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c31); [arrivals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) from the [Exterior](concepts.md#c04) are attached to the receiving point. The researcher's judgments of whether an [Antecedent](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) of a [Path](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) of a [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) is satisfied (which may be marked unknown) are attached alongside and are not [records](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06). [Signal Container](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c26) and [Random Source](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c27) within the [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23) are also [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) and are recorded with their [edges](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c29).

## public 輸出圖集 / Output Graph Set

- 定義：同一研究中，各時窗、各維度、各尺度的[輸出圖](concepts.md#c06)所構成的集合。它是[歷史](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13)的子集：沒有登記的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)，以及「沒有發生在它身上」這類[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)，都不在圖集上，但仍在[歷史](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13)中，仍參與它們所[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)的[渦](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16)的[權重](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06)。
- Definition: The collection of [Output Graph](concepts.md#c06) for all time windows, dimensions and scales within one study. It is a subset of the [History](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13): [Output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) that were not registered, and [Arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) of the kind 'it did not happen to it', are not in the set but remain in the [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) and still take part in the weights ([Weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06)) of the [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) they [reach](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10).

## public 格局傾向 / Pattern Tendency

- 定義：研究者對[輸出圖集](concepts.md#c07)中反覆可見的結構所作的歸納，描述在指定[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)下，傾向出現哪一類[輸出圖](concepts.md#c06)結構。它是在指定的[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)、時窗、分類規則與比較條件下作的歸納，不屬於任何[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)，也不是任何[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)[權重](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06)的讀數；限定到單一[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)、單一[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)類型，且該[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)是一個[個體](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20)時，就回到該[個體](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20)的[輸出傾向](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c02)。
- Definition: A researcher's generalization of structures that repeatedly appear in an [Output Graph Set](concepts.md#c07), describing which kind of [output-graph](concepts.md#c06) structure tends to appear under a stated [Cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23). It is made under a stated [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23), time window, classification rule and comparison condition. It belongs to no [Node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) and is not a reading of any [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)'s [Weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06); restricted to a single [Node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) and a single type of [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28), where that [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) is an [Individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20), it reduces to that [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20)'s [Output Tendency](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c02).

## public 經濟模式 / Economic Mode

- 定義：依[存續判準](concepts.md#c01)，在[輸出圖集](concepts.md#c07)的多個[檢查窗口](concepts.md#c03)中反覆出現的某一個以[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)相連的結構。同一性看位置，不看是誰；可以只涉及單一[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)，也可以跨[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)；不限於市場，不分[生產](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c01)與消費、營利與否、有無貨幣。經濟模式帶有[前件](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08)判定與逐[檢查窗口](concepts.md#c03)的[存續判準](concepts.md#c01)，這是它與[共振](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c09)的區別。經濟模式有同一性，可以[判準中斷](concepts.md#c10)；[格局傾向](concepts.md#c08)是跨圖的歸納，沒有同一性，也沒有[中斷](concepts.md#c10)。經濟模式本身不是[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)，是研究者對[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)的[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)所作的歸類。
- Definition: A single structure, connected by [Arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10), that repeatedly appears in several [check windows](concepts.md#c03) of an [Output Graph Set](concepts.md#c07), according to a [Persistence Criterion](concepts.md#c01). Identity depends on position, not on who takes part; it may involve a single [Node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) or several [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25); it is not limited to markets, and it does not depend on [production](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c01) versus consumption, profit, or money. An economic mode carries [antecedent](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) judgments and a [persistence criterion](concepts.md#c01) applied [check window](concepts.md#c03) by [check window](concepts.md#c03), which distinguishes it from [Resonance](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c09). An economic mode has identity and can undergo a [Criterion Break](concepts.md#c10), whereas a [Pattern Tendency](concepts.md#c08) is a generalization across graphs, with neither identity nor [break](concepts.md#c10). An economic mode is not itself a [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25); it is the researcher's grouping of [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) [Output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28).

## public 判準中斷 / Criterion Break

- 定義：依[存續判準](concepts.md#c01)，某個被追蹤的結構（例如[經濟模式](concepts.md#c09)、[人群圖](concepts.md#c05)）在某[檢查窗口](concepts.md#c03)內未再出現，即記為判準中斷。什麼算中斷，由判準決定；判準是研究者的定義；是否中斷是該[檢查窗口](concepts.md#c03)實際發生的事實。判準中斷不等於[崩潰](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c15)、[退場](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c43)或[判準退場](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09)，彼此不互相蘊含。
- Definition: Recorded when, according to a [Persistence Criterion](concepts.md#c01), a structure being tracked (for example an [Economic Mode](concepts.md#c09) or a [Population Graph](concepts.md#c05)) does not appear again in a given [Check Window](concepts.md#c03). What counts as a break is decided by the criterion. The criterion is the researcher's definition; whether the break occurs is a fact about that [check window](concepts.md#c03). A criterion break is not the same as [Collapse](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c15), [Exit](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c43) or [Criterion Exit](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09), and none implies another.

## public 界面節點 / Interface Node

- 定義：[經濟模式](concepts.md#c09)中有[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)[外界](concepts.md#c04)的[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)。類型依上游[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)的分類，可以是[渦](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16)、[信號容器](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c26)或[隨機源](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c27)；同一[經濟模式](concepts.md#c09)可以有多種界面節點，[外界](concepts.md#c04)經由不同界面接觸到的是性質不同的[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)來源。
- Definition: A [Node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) of an [Economic Mode](concepts.md#c09) whose [Output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) [reaches](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) the [Exterior](concepts.md#c04). Its type follows the classification of [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25): it can be a [Wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), a [Signal Container](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c26) or a [Random Source](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c27). One [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) can have several kinds of interface nodes, and the [exterior](concepts.md#c04) meets [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) sources of different character through different interfaces.

## public 生殖模式 / Reproductive Mode

- 定義：產出[子代](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c15)的[經濟模式](concepts.md#c09)。這是依產出所作的稱呼，是研究者的[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)，沿用[經濟模式](concepts.md#c09)的定義，沒有另外的成立條件。
- Definition: An [Economic Mode](concepts.md#c09) whose product is [Offspring](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c15). It is a name given by what is produced, a researcher's [Distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18); it uses the definition of [Economic Mode](concepts.md#c09) as it stands and adds no further condition.
