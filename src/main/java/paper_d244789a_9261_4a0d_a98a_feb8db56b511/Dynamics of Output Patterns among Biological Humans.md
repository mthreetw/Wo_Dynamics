# Dynamics of Output Patterns among Biological Humans

---

## 0. Positioning and Method

### 0.1 Positioning

*Dynamics of Output Tendency in the Biological Human Individual* (hereafter the Individual paper) addresses the [output tendency](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c02) of a single biological human. This paper is its counterpart at the level of multiple biological humans. It addresses the structure of [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) among the [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of multiple biological humans, that is, the **output pattern**: how recurring structures within it are identified, how they persist and [break](concepts.md#c10), and how these structures become one another's [exteriors](concepts.md#c04).

<note>

*This paper is the "multi-person version" of the Individual paper. The Individual paper looks at what output one person tends to produce; this paper looks at what recurring structures remain once many people's outputs reach one another. Such structures are called "output patterns."*

</note>

### 0.2 Method

This paper uses the deductive method. Every conclusion states which definitions, premises or earlier conclusions it is derived from. Cases in the text serve only for calibration and carry no part of the argument.

<note>

*The method is deduction: every conclusion notes which definition, premise or earlier conclusion it comes from. Real-world examples are only for comparison, not used as evidence.*

</note>

### 0.3 Scope of Records

This paper follows the scope of [records](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06) used in *Production Dynamics*: only the physical changes of an [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) are registered, not the content of a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18). A [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) remains in the [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13), but it is not in this paper's [records](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06).

<note>

*A record only registers what physically happened in an output; it does not register how a person distinguishes or thinks inwardly. Distinctions are still in the history; this paper just does not write them into the record.*

</note>

### 0.4 Observational Premise

This paper takes [conditioning on existing samples](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c14) as its observational premise: every observed [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) is one that has passed every past [persistence criterion](concepts.md#c01). The study must also include [modes](concepts.md#c09) that have already undergone a [criterion break](concepts.md#c10) but still leave [records](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06). Wherever the text later touches on "survival" or "looking designed," it refers back to this item.

<note>

*Every economic mode we see is one that survived all past checks; modes that did not survive must also be counted in the study. Whenever later text says "what survived looks designed," it goes back to this item.*

</note>

### 0.5 Scope of Claims

This paper derives only two kinds of things: relations that necessarily hold by structure, and which things cannot be derived from which others. This paper does not predict which structures will become [economic modes](concepts.md#c09), how long an [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) will last, or in which direction their influence will go.

<note>

*This paper derives only two things: which relations necessarily hold, and what cannot be derived from what. It does not predict which structures will become economic modes, how long they will last, or which way their influence will go.*

</note>

All conclusions in this paper hold only for finite observers within the [universe](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c02): the researcher and every [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) are within the [universe](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c02), and no viewpoint stands outside the [universe](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c02). Wherever "cannot be derived" is said, it means that the researcher and every [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) cannot derive it beforehand.

<note>

*All conclusions are stated from inside the universe: the researcher and the studied objects are all inside it, and nobody looks from outside. So "cannot be derived" means that we (including the people studied) cannot derive it beforehand.*

</note>

### 0.6 Conventions

Each conclusion is followed, in order, by:

<note>

*The following explains what is attached after each conclusion.*

</note>

- **Source**: the parenthetical states which definitions, premises or conclusions it is derived from, supplying only the step newly added by this paper.
- **Explanation**: the part newly derived in this paper, broken into derivation steps.
- **Calibration**: the corresponding real-world phenomenon, for comparison only, carrying no part of the argument.

<note>

*After each conclusion, in order: the source (where it is derived from), the explanation (the derivation broken into steps), and the calibration (a real-world example for comparison). Only the first two carry the argument; the calibration is not evidence.*

</note>

Wherever this paper says "an [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) becomes an [exterior](concepts.md#c04)," "an [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) participates in [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06)," or "an [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) enters [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13)," these are researcher-level abbreviations: what actually [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10), enters [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) and participates in [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) is the [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of the [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) in the [mode](concepts.md#c09). An [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) itself is the researcher's classification of these [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28); it is not a [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25).

<note>

*From now on, saying things like "an economic mode becomes an exterior" is only shorthand. What actually reaches others and enters history is the outputs of the individual nodes in the mode; an economic mode is the researcher grouping these outputs into one class, and is not itself a node.*

</note>

## 2. Premises

**Premise 1**: If at least one [antecedent](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) in the [threshold](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c03) of some [path](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) can be satisfied only by the [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of other [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) [arriving](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10), then the feasibility of that [path](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) depends on the [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of other [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25).

<note>

*Premise 1: If some condition a path needs can only come from other people's outputs, then whether that path can be taken depends on others.*

</note>

Explanation: Premise 1 is a conditional and a scope setting; it is not an existence claim. If a research object has no [path](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) whose [antecedent](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) is satisfied in this way, this paper simply does not apply to it, and Premise 1 is not thereby refuted.

<note>

*Premise 1 is only "if... then...". For objects with no such path, this paper just does not apply; the premise is not wrong.*

</note>

Calibration: For biological humans, reproduction and rearing have no single-person [path](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07), so the [antecedent](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) of Premise 1 holds at least for these two kinds of [paths](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07). Reproduction needs a counterpart; an infant, when the criterion takes the physiological boundary, cannot persist without others' [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) (the criterion is set by the researcher). In a gold rush there is likewise no single-person [path](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07); see 11.1.

<note>

*Examples: having a child takes another person; an infant, when the criterion takes the physiological boundary, cannot persist without others' outputs (the criterion is set by the researcher); gold prospecting also depends on food and housing supplied by others. All of these are cases where "the condition can only come from others."*

</note>

**Premise 2**: The [persistence criterion](concepts.md#c01) of an [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) is stated by the researcher and includes:
1. **[Identity condition](concepts.md#c02)**: under what equivalence the structures in different [check windows](concepts.md#c03) count as the same one;
2. **[Check window](concepts.md#c03)**: in which windows the structure is checked for whether it appears again.

<note>

*Premise 2: Whether an economic mode counts as "continuing" must be settled by the researcher stating two things beforehand: what counts as the same structure, and in which time windows to check whether it reappears.*

</note>

Explanation: Reality has no unified rounds, only the order of [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) and [arrivals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10). A [derivation round](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c07) is the researcher's segmentation of a sequence of [arrivals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10), not a round in reality. Different [economic modes](concepts.md#c09) are each judged by their own [check windows](concepts.md#c03), and no common round is assumed; relations across [modes](concepts.md#c09) are [recorded](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06) according to the order of [arrivals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10).

<note>

*Reality has no unified rounds. Different modes each use their own check windows, with no assumption that everyone is synchronized; relations between modes are recorded according to the order of arrivals.*

</note>

Calibration: A wrongly chosen [window](concepts.md#c03) leads to a wrong judgment, for example checking a farming [mode](concepts.md#c09) in winter.

<note>

*Example: A wrongly chosen window leads to a wrong judgment. For instance, checking a farming mode in winter.*

</note>

**Premise 3**: The [antecedent](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) of a [path](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) can be satisfied by the [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) of some [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28); this [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) may come from another [path](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) of the same [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25), or from other [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25).

<note>

*Premise 3: A condition a path needs can be satisfied by some output being delivered; this output may be made by the person's own other path, or by someone else.*

</note>

Explanation: Premise 3 is a possibility claim and a scope setting: it says only that this supply relation can hold, not that a given [path](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) is necessarily so. What Premise 1 discusses is the case within this supply where "the [antecedent](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) can be supplied only by the [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of other [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)."

<note>

*Premise 3 says only that this supply "can hold," not that every path is like this. What Premise 1 discusses is the case within this supply where "the condition can only be supplied by others."*

</note>

Calibration: The bones left by a hunt are the material [antecedent](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) of the later [path](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) of making a bone spear; the bones may be left behind by oneself in the previous round, or by companions.

<note>

*Example: A hunt leaves bones, and only then is there material to make a bone spear. The bones may have been left by oneself last time, or by companions.*

</note>

**Premise 4**: The [entry](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c44) of every biological human [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) comes from some reproductive event: the [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) is [offspring](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c15) produced by that event.

<note>

*Premise 4: Every biological human node's appearance comes from some reproductive event; it is the offspring that event produced.*

</note>

Explanation: Premise 4 is a scope setting that concerns only biological human [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) and does not touch other types of [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25).

<note>

*Premise 4 covers only biological human nodes, not other kinds of nodes.*

</note>

Calibration: People who immigrate from elsewhere were also produced by reproductive events elsewhere.

<note>

*Example: People who move in from elsewhere were also produced by reproductive events elsewhere.*

</note>

**Premise 5**: The [paths](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) of a [reproductive mode](concepts.md#c12) have [antecedents](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) that can be supplied only by the [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of the counterpart.

<note>

*Premise 5: The reproduction path has some conditions that exist only through the counterpart's output.*

</note>

Explanation: Premise 5 is a scope setting and a biological fact; it concerns only the [reproductive mode](concepts.md#c12) and does not touch other [modes](concepts.md#c09).

<note>

*Premise 5 covers only the reproductive mode; it is a biological fact and says nothing about other modes.*

</note>

Calibration: Reproduction requires at least two people and cannot be accomplished by one person alone.

<note>

*Example: Having a child takes at least two people.*

</note>

**Premise 6**: A [signal container](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c26) or [random source](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c27) participating in an [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) counts as lying within the range of the [reachable subgraph](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c07) of the [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) it serves.

<note>

*Premise 6: A signal container or random source participating in a mode counts as within the range of the reachable subgraph of the wo it serves.*

</note>

Explanation: Premise 6 is a scope setting. The definition of [reachable subgraph](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c07) admits only the [presentations](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04) that send to the center; to cover containers used only by a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), this paper counts the participating [signal containers](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c26) and [random sources](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c27) in as well.

<note>

*Premise 6 is a scope setting. The definition of reachable subgraph admits only objects that send to the center; to cover containers used only by a wo, this paper counts them in as well.*

</note>

Calibration: An automated teller machine is not itself a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), but it serves the bank and its customers, so it counts as within the range of their [reachable subgraphs](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c07).

<note>

*Example: An automated teller machine is not itself a wo, but it serves the bank and its customers, so it counts as within the range of their reachable subgraphs.*

</note>

**Premise 7**: Whether an [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at a given [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) depends not only on that [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) but also on other parts of that [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)'s [exterior](concepts.md#c04), such as the route, the weather and transport.

<note>

*Premise 7: Whether an output gets through depends not only on the output itself but also on conditions along the way.*

</note>

Explanation: Premise 7 is an empirical premise. The definition of [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) says only that a [writing](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c29) counts as [arriving](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at some [presentation](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04) when it lands on it as a [stimulus](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c30); it does not say what decides whether it lands. This paper lists this as a premise.

<note>

*The definition of arrival does not say what decides whether something gets through; this paper makes that a premise.*

</note>

## 3. Definitions

### 3.1 Exterior

**[Exterior](concepts.md#c04)**: For a given [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25), every source of [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) other than itself, including other [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) and the [natural environment](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c16).

<note>

*Exterior: For a given node, all sources other than itself that can deliver something to it, including other nodes and the natural environment.*

</note>

- The [exterior](concepts.md#c04) is relative to a [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25); change the [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) and the [exterior](concepts.md#c04) differs. The size of a [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) is set by the [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23); it can be a person or a company: when a company is taken as a [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25), its employees are inside it and its customers are its [exterior](concepts.md#c04).
- In an [output graph](concepts.md#c06), everything outside the [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23) is the [exterior](concepts.md#c04) of that graph. The [natural environment](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c16) is not drawn separately on the graph; when a closer look is needed, the researcher enlarges the [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23).

<note>

*Exterior is relative: view from a different node and the exterior differs. In a graph, everything outside the graph counts as the graph's exterior; the natural environment is not drawn separately, and the range is enlarged when a closer look is needed.*

</note>

### 3.2 Population Graph, Output Graph and Output Graph Set

**[Population graph](concepts.md#c05)**: Under a stated [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23), the static graph formed by biological human [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) and the [edges](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c29) between them (the [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of one [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) participating in the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) of another [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)), plus each [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)'s own [loop](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c31) (its own [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28), through itself, participating again in its own [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06)). The [persistence criterion](concepts.md#c01) of a [population graph](concepts.md#c05) is stated by the researcher, in the same way as for an [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) (Premise 2).

<note>

*Population graph: Look only at the biological human nodes, and at the edges between nodes (whose output affects whose weight), then add each person's own loop of output returning to themselves, and draw a static graph. It too requires the researcher to state how to count "continuing," in the same way as for an economic mode.*

</note>

**[Output graph](concepts.md#c06)**: On the basis of a [population graph](concepts.md#c05), within a stated [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23), dimension and time window, the graph obtained by attaching [records](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06) to the points and [edges](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c29): the points are the [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) of the [population graph](concepts.md#c05), plus the [signal containers](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c26) and [random sources](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c27) within the [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23); the [edges](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c29) are the [edges](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c29) of the [population graph](concepts.md#c05), plus the [edges](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c29) between these [signal containers](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c26) or [random sources](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c27) and other [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25); the [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) [records](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06) of each [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) are attached to its point, the [records](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06) of each [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) are attached to the [edge](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c29), or to the [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)'s own [loop](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c31), and an [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) from the [exterior](concepts.md#c04) is attached to the receiving point; in addition, the researcher's judgment on whether the [antecedent](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) of some [path](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) of some [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) is satisfied is attached, and it may be marked "unknown." According to the Individual paper, this judgment is attached alongside the [records](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06) and does not count as a [record](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06). [Signal containers](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c26) and [random sources](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c27) within the [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23) are also [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25), and they are entered together with their [edges](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c29).

<note>

*Output graph: Add time onto the population graph: who output what and when (recorded on the point), who it reached and when (recorded on the edge, or on the person's own loop), with arrivals from the exterior recorded on the person who receives them; plus the researcher's judgment of "whether this person's conditions for this path are satisfied." Signal containers such as a calendar and random sources such as dice also count as nodes and are recorded as well.*

</note>

**[Output graph set](concepts.md#c07)**: The set formed by the [output graphs](concepts.md#c06) of the various time windows, dimensions and scales within the same study.

<note>

*Output graph set: Within the same study, the output graphs of the various times, dimensions and scales put together.*

</note>

Explanation:

<note>

*What follows are supplementary notes on the definitions above.*

</note>

- An [edge](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c29) does not distinguish [moments](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09), and many [arrivals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) may lie on one [edge](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c29); when the [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of a biological human [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) in the [population graph](concepts.md#c05) that is a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at itself, on the [population graph](concepts.md#c05) it is that [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)'s own [loop](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c31).
- The [output graph set](concepts.md#c07) is a subset of the [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13): [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) that are not registered, and [arrivals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) of the kind "did not happen to it," are not on the graph, but are still in the [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) and still participate in the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) of the [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) they [arrive](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at.

<note>

*The edge itself does not distinguish time, and one edge can carry many arrivals; the output of a biological human in the population graph that is a wo, returning to itself, is, on the population graph, this person's own loop. Also, the graph set is only a part of history: unregistered outputs and arrivals of the kind "this time it did not land on it" are not on the graph but are still in history and still affect the weight of the wos they arrive at.*

</note>

### 3.3 Pattern Tendency

**[Pattern tendency](concepts.md#c08)**: The researcher's generalization over structures that repeatedly appear in the [output graph set](concepts.md#c07), describing which kind of [output graph](concepts.md#c06) structure tends to appear under a stated [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23). It is a generalization made under the stated [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23), time window, classification rule and comparison conditions.

<note>

*Pattern tendency: The researcher's generalization of "which kind of structure appears repeatedly" seen in the output graph set. It is a generalization made under stated conditions.*

</note>

- It parallels the [output tendency](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c02) of the Individual paper: an [output tendency](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c02) generalizes the [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of one [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20), while a [pattern tendency](concepts.md#c08) generalizes the [output graph](concepts.md#c06) structures that recur in the [output graph set](concepts.md#c07).
- A [pattern tendency](concepts.md#c08) belongs to no [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) and is not a readout of any [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)'s [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06).
- When restricted to a single [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) and a single [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) type, where that [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) is an [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20), it returns to that [individual](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20)'s [output tendency](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c02).

<note>

*Pattern tendency corresponds to the output tendency of the Individual paper: one looks at one person's outputs, the other at output-graph structures that recur in the output graph set. It belongs to no one and is nobody's weight readout; narrowed to one person and one kind of output, it falls back to output tendency.*

</note>

### 3.4 Economic Mode

**[Economic mode](concepts.md#c09)**: A particular structure that, according to the [persistence criterion](concepts.md#c01) stated by the researcher (Premise 2), repeatedly appears across multiple [check windows](concepts.md#c03) of the [output graph set](concepts.md#c07).

<note>

*Economic mode: A particular structure that, according to the continuation standard stated by the researcher, repeatedly appears across multiple check windows.*

</note>

- **Identity depends on position, not on who**: structures in two [windows](concepts.md#c03) that are isomorphic under the stated equivalence count as the same [economic mode](concepts.md#c09). A change of participating [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) does not necessarily change identity.
- **Connected by [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)**: an [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) is a structure connected by [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10): there is [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) among the participating [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25), or between participating [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) and the [exterior](concepts.md#c04). Grouping many mutually unconnected [modes](concepts.md#c09) under one name (for example, the catering industry) is a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) of the researcher and is not one [economic mode](concepts.md#c09).
- **It may involve only a single [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)**, for example repeated [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) between one person and the [exterior](concepts.md#c04); it may also span [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25).
- **Not limited to markets**: no distinction between [production](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c01) and consumption, for-profit or not, with or without money. The name is only a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) of the researcher and is given no independent ontology, the same as the treatment of "[production](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c01)" in *Production Dynamics*.
- **Distinction from [pattern tendency](concepts.md#c08)**: a [pattern tendency](concepts.md#c08) is a cross-graph generalization with no identity and no [break](concepts.md#c10); an [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) is a particular tracked structure, with identity, and can undergo a [criterion break](concepts.md#c10).
- **Distinction from [resonance](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c09)**: [resonance](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c09) requires that multiple [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) repeatedly connect compatibly under the condition of [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10); an [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) carries an [antecedent](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) judgment and a [window](concepts.md#c03)-by-[window](concepts.md#c03) [persistence criterion](concepts.md#c01), and may involve only a single [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25).
- **Relation to [production](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c01)**: on the dimension of [production](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c01), the part of an [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) in which multiple [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) repeatedly connect compatibly, and either [arrive](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at one another or share a common source that [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at all of them, is the case described by the definition of [resonance](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c09).

<note>

*A few clarifications: the same mode is about positional structure, not who; when participants change, the mode does not necessarily change; it may involve only the repeated exchange between one person and the exterior; it is not limited to markets or to whether money exists; unlike a pattern tendency, an economic mode has identity and can break; unlike resonance, it requires judging antecedents and a continuation standard; on the dimension of production, the part of a mode in which multiple nodes repeatedly connect compatibly and arrive at one another or share a source that arrives at them is resonance. A mode is a structure connected by arrival; grouping many modes under one name (such as the catering industry) is only a distinction of the researcher.*

</note>

### 3.5 Criterion Break

**[Criterion break](concepts.md#c10)**: According to the [persistence criterion](concepts.md#c01) stated by the researcher, when a tracked structure (for example an [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) or a [population graph](concepts.md#c05)) fails to appear again within some [check window](concepts.md#c03), it is recorded as a [criterion break](concepts.md#c10). What counts as a [break](concepts.md#c10) is decided by the criterion.

<note>

*Criterion break: According to the continuation standard set by the researcher, if some economic mode fails to appear again in some window, it is recorded as a break.*

</note>

- The criterion is the researcher's definition; whether a [break](concepts.md#c10) occurs is a fact about that [window](concepts.md#c03). The two do not substitute for each other, with the same structure as [criterion exit](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09).
- The criteria of [criterion break](concepts.md#c10), [order collapse](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c15), [exit](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c43) and [criterion exit](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09) differ, and they do not imply one another: a [mode](concepts.md#c09) can [break](concepts.md#c10) while order has not undergone [order collapse](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c15), and a participant can [exit](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c43) while the [mode](concepts.md#c09) has not [broken](concepts.md#c10).

<note>

*The standard is the researcher's definition, and whether a break occurs is what actually happened in that window; the two do not replace each other. Break, order collapse, exit and criterion exit do not necessarily occur together: a mode can break while order stays intact, and a person can leave while the mode remains.*

</note>

### 3.6 Interface Node

**[Interface node](concepts.md#c11)**: A [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) in an [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) whose [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at the [exterior](concepts.md#c04).

<note>

*Interface node: Those nodes in an economic mode whose outputs go to the exterior.*

</note>

- Its type follows the upstream classification of [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25); it can be a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), a [signal container](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c26) or a [random source](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c27).
- The same [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) can have several kinds of [interface nodes](concepts.md#c11), and through different [interfaces](concepts.md#c11) the [exterior](concepts.md#c04) meets [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) sources of different character.

<note>

*An interface node can be a wo, a signal container or a random source. The same mode can have several kinds of interfaces, and what the exterior touches through different interfaces also differs.*

</note>

## 4. Reachable Subgraph: The Range of an Economic Mode

Following the definition of [reachable subgraph](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c07), this section speaks entirely of [records](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06) of what has **already [arrived](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)**. Beforehand, "whom one may possibly contact" belongs to the judgment of [antecedents](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) and does not belong to the [reachable subgraph](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c07).

<note>

*The reachable subgraphs in this section look only at records of what has already arrived. "Whom one might come into contact with" beforehand is a matter of antecedent judgment, not of reachable subgraph.*

</note>

### 4.1 The Node Range of an Economic Mode

**Conclusion 4.1**: Within some [check window](concepts.md#c03), if the [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of A in some [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) B, then A belongs to B's [reachable subgraph](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c07) in that [window](concepts.md#c03).

<note>

*Conclusion 4.1: In a given window, if A's output in the mode arrives at a wo B, A belongs to B's reachable subgraph in this window.*

</note>

(Derived from the definition of [reachable subgraph](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c07): the [presentations](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04) whose [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) has [arrived](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at some [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), together with the [edges](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c29) between them, constitute its [reachable subgraph](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c07); only what has already [arrived](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) is counted)

<note>

*The source is the definition of reachable subgraph: the objects whose outputs have reached some wo, together with the edges between them, make up its reachable subgraph.*

</note>

**Conclusion 4.2**: Within some [check window](concepts.md#c03), the participating [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) of a cross-[node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) are each the center of their own [reachable subgraph](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c07); participating [signal containers](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c26) and [random sources](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c27) count as lying within the [reachable subgraph](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c07) of the [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) they serve. The range of an [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) does not exceed the range reached by [arrivals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) that have occurred.

<note>

*Conclusion 4.2: In a cross-node economic mode, each participating wo is the center of its own reachable subgraph, and participating signal containers and random sources count as within the range of the wo they serve. Also, the range of a mode does not exceed the places already reached by arrivals.*

</note>

(Derived from Conclusion 4.1, Premise 6 and the definition of [reachable subgraph](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c07))

<note>

*This follows from Conclusion 4.1, together with Premise 6 and the definition of reachable subgraph.*

</note>

Explanation:
1. Every participating [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) of a cross-[node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) has an [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) with at least one other participating [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) or with the [exterior](concepts.md#c04); otherwise it is not in the same structure (the definition of [economic mode](concepts.md#c09)).
2. By Conclusion 4.1, a [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) that sends an [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) falls within the [reachable subgraph](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c07) of the receiving [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16); each participating [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) is the center of its own [reachable subgraph](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c07) (the center counts as included).
3. A [signal container](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c26) or [random source](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c27) is not a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) and has no [reachable subgraph](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c07) of its own; by Premise 6, a participating one counts as within the range of the [reachable subgraph](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c07) of the [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) it serves.

<note>

*Derivation: In a cross-node mode, every participant has at least one arrival with another participant or with the exterior, otherwise it is not in the same structure; one who sends falls in the reachable subgraph of the receiving wo, and each participating wo is the center of its own subgraph.*

</note>

Calibration: A shop can sell only to people it can reach. Within the time window, a person with no [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at all with the shop's [interface node](concepts.md#c11) is not in this [economic mode](concepts.md#c09).

<note>

*Example: A shop can sell only to people it can reach. During this period, a person with no arrival relation with the storefront at all is not counted as part of this economic mode.*

</note>

### 4.2 Reachable Is Not Participating

**Conclusion 4.3**: That a [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) is in a [reachable subgraph](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c07) does not imply that it has [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) with the central [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) on other dimensions.

<note>

*Conclusion 4.3: Being in a reachable subgraph only means having arrival on one dimension; it does not mean having it on other dimensions too.*

</note>

(Derived from the fact that a [reachable subgraph](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c07) must state its time window and that [arrivals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) are [recorded](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06) by dimension)

<note>

*Source: a reachable subgraph must state its time window, and arrivals are recorded by dimension.*

</note>

**Conclusion 4.4**: That a [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) is in a [reachable subgraph](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c07), even with [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) on the relevant dimension, does not imply that its [feasible set](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c11) contains a [path](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) whose [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) can be judged to be an [antecedent](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) needed by some [economic mode](concepts.md#c09).

<note>

*Conclusion 4.4: Being in the subgraph, even having arrival on the relevant dimension, does not mean the other party can produce output that can serve as an antecedent.*

</note>

(Derived from the definitions of [antecedent](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) and [feasible set](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c11); whether an [antecedent](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) is satisfied is a judgment the researcher makes about a [path](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07))

<note>

*Source: whether an antecedent is satisfied is a judgment the researcher makes about a path; it does not hold automatically.*

</note>

**Conclusion 4.5**: Even if some [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) can supply the corresponding [antecedent](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08), it does not follow that it will [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) in the next [check window](concepts.md#c03).

<note>

*Conclusion 4.5: Even if a node has the ability to supply an antecedent, that does not mean it will actually output in the next window.*

</note>

(Derived from the definition of [threshold](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c03): all [antecedents](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) being satisfied means only that the path is feasible, not that it will occur)

<note>

*The source is the definition of threshold: all conditions being satisfied only means "can go," not "will go."*

</note>

Taken together, on the stated [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) and dimension:

<note>

*Putting the preceding items together, the range gets smaller layer by layer.*

</note>

> [Nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) in the [reachable subgraph](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c07) ⊇ [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) with [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) on that dimension ⊇ [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) whose [antecedent](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) is judged able to be supplied ⊇ [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) actually [outputting](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) in the [mode](concepts.md#c09)

<note>

*The range from large to small: the nodes in the reachable subgraph; among them, those with arrival on this dimension; among them, those judged able to supply the antecedent; and finally those that actually output in the mode.*

</note>

Each step inward adds one more condition; the last layer can only wait for the [records](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06) afterward.

<note>

*Each step inward adds one more condition, and the last layer can only wait for the record afterward.*

</note>

Calibration: A person who knows a hundred people shows only that the first layer is large. How many of them do business with him, how many can supply what he needs, and how many in the end actually step forward are three other matters.

<note>

*Example: Knowing a hundred people shows only that the first layer is large. Who among them does business with you, who can produce what you need, and who actually steps forward are three other matters.*

</note>

Also (Conclusion 4.4): In Granovetter's study in Newton, Massachusetts, of 54 people who found jobs through acquaintances, only 16.7% had often met that acquaintance beforehand (at least once a week), 55.6% met occasionally, and 27.8% rarely (less than once a year). Frequent meeting does not imply being the one who supplies job information.

<note>

*Supplementary example: A study found that among people who found jobs through acquaintances, only a small portion had met them often beforehand. Meeting often does not mean being the one who provides job information.*

</note>

### 4.3 Changes in Arrival

**Conclusion 4.6**: Whether the [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) of an [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) occurs depends not only on that [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) but also on the [exterior](concepts.md#c04). So even when the [output tendencies](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c02) of all [individuals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c20) show no discernible change, the [reachable subgraph](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c07) may still change.

<note>

*Conclusion 4.6: Whether an output arrives depends not only on that output but also on the exterior. So even if everyone's output tendency has not changed, the reachable subgraph may still change.*

</note>

(Derived from Premise 7 and the definition of [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10): a [writing](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c29) counts as [arriving](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at some [presentation](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04) only when it lands on it as a [stimulus](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c30); whether it lands also depends on the [exterior](concepts.md#c04) (Premise 7))

<note>

*The sources are Premise 7 and the definition of arrival: a writing counts as arriving at an object only when it really lands on that object, and whether it lands also depends on the exterior.*

</note>

Explanation:
1. An [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)'s [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at another party is conditioned on the [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) occurring and being able to land on the other party (the definition of [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)).
2. Whether an [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) lands on the other party also depends on the other party's [exterior](concepts.md#c04) (Premise 7).
3. So when the [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) is unchanged, a change in the [exterior](concepts.md#c04) may still cause [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) to occur or not occur; a [reachable subgraph](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c07) counts only what has already [arrived](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10), so when the set of [presentations](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04) whose [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) has [arrived](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at the central [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) changes as a result, it changes accordingly.

<note>

*Derivation: For an output to arrive at another party it must be able to land there; whether it can land on the other party also depends on the other party's exterior (Premise 7); so if the output has not changed but the exterior has, arrival may appear or disappear.*

</note>

Calibration: A typhoon closes the port; the fishermen fish as usual, but the catch cannot reach the market in the city; the [reachable subgraph](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c07) between the fishermen and the city buyers shrank that week.

<note>

*Example: A typhoon closes the port, and the fishermen fish as usual, but the fish cannot reach the market; the reachable subgraph between the fishermen and the buyers shrank that week.*

</note>

Also: On 23 March 2021 the *Ever Given* ran aground in the Suez Canal and was not freed until the 29th; during that time more than 430 ships waited at both ends, and about nine billion US dollars of goods were blocked each day. The [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of sellers and buyers at both ends continued as before, but [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) did not occur.

<note>

*Supplementary example: When the Suez Canal was blocked, the outputs of buyers and sellers at both ends did not change, yet the goods could not arrive; arrival did not occur.*

</note>

**Conclusion 4.7**: If the [identity condition](concepts.md#c02) of an [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) requires certain [arrivals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) (lacking any one of them, it is not met), and these [arrivals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) no longer occur within some [check window](concepts.md#c03), the [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) undergoes a [criterion break](concepts.md#c10) in that [window](concepts.md#c03). Conversely, the occurrence of new [arrivals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) may cause a structure that had not appeared to appear.

<note>

*Conclusion 4.7: If a mode's identity condition requires certain arrivals (missing any one fails it), and these arrivals do not occur in some window, the mode breaks in that window; conversely, the occurrence of new arrivals may also make a structure that was not there appear.*

</note>

(Derived from the definitions of [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) and [criterion break](concepts.md#c10))

<note>

*This follows directly from the definitions of economic mode and criterion break.*

</note>

Calibration: Roads, shipping and the internet expand the range of [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10); blockades, wars and epidemics shrink it. The appearance and [break](concepts.md#c10) of an [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) can be caused entirely by the [exterior](concepts.md#c04), without anyone's tendency having to change first; but whether a new structure really appears still depends on the [records](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06).

<note>

*Example: Roads, shipping and the internet expand the range of arrival; blockades, wars and epidemics shrink it. The appearance or break of a mode can be caused entirely by the exterior, without anyone's tendency having to change first, but whether a new structure really appears still depends on the record.*

</note>

Also: When the Erie Canal opened in 1825, the freight cost from New York to Buffalo fell from 90 US dollars per ton to 4 dollars, and the trip shortened from three weeks to 8 days; the freight [path](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) between the Great Lakes region and New York, previously infeasible, thereby entered the [feasible set](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c11).

<note>

*Supplementary example: After the Erie Canal opened, freight costs fell sharply and the journey time shortened greatly; a freight path that could not be done before became feasible.*

</note>

## 5. The Formation of Economic Modes

### 5.1 One Cycle

Within one [check window](concepts.md#c03) of some [economic mode](concepts.md#c09), the following process can be described on the basis of the upstream (this is a description, not a new definition):

<note>

*What follows describes roughly how an economic mode operates within one check window. This is only a description, not a new definition.*

</note>

1. A participating [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28), and the [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) the researcher has specified for registration are [recorded](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06);
2. The [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) may [arrive](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at other [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25), at the [exterior](concepts.md#c04), or, through a [loop](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c31), at itself;
3. The [outcome feedback](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c05) of each participating [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), if it [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) back at it, enters its [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13);
4. This [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) participates in each [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16)'s [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) at the next [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09), and this step is not necessarily readable;
5. If these [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) have [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) in the next [check window](concepts.md#c03), it occurs under the [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) in which this [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) participates.

<note>

*Five steps: participants output, and the outputs the researcher has specified for registration are recorded; outputs may arrive at others, at the exterior, or back at oneself through a loop; each participating wo's outcome feedback, if it returns to it, enters its history; this history participates in its weight at the next moment (this step may not be visible); if these wos have output in the next window, it occurs under the weights in which this history participates.*

</note>

This corresponds to the definition of [derivation round](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c07): each [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) present goes through one cycle of [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28), [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) and entering [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13); this paper adds two steps, [outcome feedback](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c05) and its participation in [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06), and places the cycle on the [check window](concepts.md#c03) of an [economic mode](concepts.md#c09). This paper records the following: the process repeats [check window](concepts.md#c03) by [check window](concepts.md#c03); if no participating [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) in the next [check window](concepts.md#c03), the cycle stops at that [check window](concepts.md#c03); if a participating [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) again in a later [check window](concepts.md#c03), the cycle starts again; the cycle stopping is not the same as a [criterion break](concepts.md#c10), which is judged by the [persistence criterion](concepts.md#c01).

<note>

*This cycle corresponds to the definition of derivation round; this paper adds the two steps of outcome feedback and its participation in weight, and places it on the check window of an economic mode. This paper sets it up this way: the process repeats each window; if no one outputs in the next window, the cycle stops there; if someone outputs again in a later window, it starts again. The cycle stopping is not a break of the mode, which is judged by the persistence criterion.*

</note>

### 5.2 Participating Wos Each Have a Small Loop

**Conclusion 5.1**: In every [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) in which a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) participates, each participating [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) has its own [small loop](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c02).

<note>

*Conclusion 5.1: As long as a wo participates, each wo in the mode has its own small loop, that is, its output circles back to affect itself.*

</note>

(Derived from the definitions of [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28), [collapse](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c08), [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06), [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09), [involvement](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c11), [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10), [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), [loop](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c31) and [small loop](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c02): the [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) is the result of one of its own [collapses](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c08) ([output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)); the [collapse](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c08) result of a [presentation](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04) is a result at that [presentation](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04)'s next [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) that depends on [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) and has non-zero [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) ([collapse](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c08), [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09)), that is, a result that falls on it with positive [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) ([weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06)), so this [collapse](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c08) [involves](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c11) it ([involvement](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c11)); when the [collapse](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c08) [involves](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c11) it, the result [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at it ([arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)); whatever [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at it in its [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) participates in its [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) at the next [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) ([wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16)); so its [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) passes through itself and participates again in its own [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06), forming a [loop](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c31), that is, its [small loop](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c02))

<note>

*Reason: A wo's output is a result of its own; the result of its own collapse falls on it in the first place, that is, the collapse involves it, so the result returns to it and becomes part of its history, affecting its weight at the next moment, and that is a loop, namely its small loop.*

</note>

Explanation: A person satisfying oneself, interaction between two people and one-sided [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) are all included here. When a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) gives one-sided [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28), even if there is no response at all, "it did not happen to it" at its later [moments](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) is by the definition of [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) also an [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10). So what needs case-by-case judgment is not "whether there is a [loop](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c31)" but "which [loops](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c31) there are": only a [small loop](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c02), a [large loop](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c03) as well, or forming a [taiji](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c39).

<note>

*A wo's one-sided output also has a loop: even without a response, at its later moments, "it did not happen to it" counts as an arrival. So the question is not whether there is a loop but which loops there are: only a small loop, a large loop, or forming a taiji.*

</note>

Calibration: For a self-sufficient farming household, this year's harvest and farming experience return to next year's farming. A job application sent out without reply: the "no reply" also enters the applicant's [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13).

<note>

*Example: For a self-sufficient farming household, this year's harvest and experience return to next year's farming. A job application sent out with no reply: the "no reply" also enters the applicant's history.*

</note>

### 5.3 The Supply of Antecedents Comes from Others

**Conclusion 5.2**: If a [path](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) belongs to the [paths](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) referred to by Premise 1, and it is realized within some [check window](concepts.md#c03), then there must be the [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of some other [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) [arriving](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) that supplies its [antecedent](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08). Whether the supplier is counted into the same [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) (the [mode](concepts.md#c09) spans [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)) or counted as the [exterior](concepts.md#c04) of the [mode](concepts.md#c09) is a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) of the researcher.

<note>

*Conclusion 5.2: If a path belongs to the kind referred to by Premise 1, and it is really realized, then someone else's output must have arrived and supplied its condition; whether the supplier is counted into the same mode or counted as the exterior is a distinction of the researcher.*

</note>

(Derived from Premise 1, and from the definitions of [feasible set](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c11) and [antecedent](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08))

<note>

*Source: Premise 1, plus the definitions of feasible set and antecedent.*

</note>

Explanation:
1. A [path](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) being realized means it is feasible in that [window](concepts.md#c03) and every [antecedent](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) in its [threshold](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c03) is satisfied (the definition of [threshold](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c03)).
2. By Premise 1, at least one of those [antecedents](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) is satisfied only by the [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of another [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) [arriving](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10).
3. So the [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of some other [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) must have [arrived](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) (in that [window](concepts.md#c03), or earlier).

<note>

*Derivation: A path being realized means all antecedents are satisfied; by Premise 1, at least one of them can only be supplied by others; so someone else's output must have arrived, in that window or earlier.*

</note>

Calibration: One person cannot kill an elephant; it takes two or three people striking at the same time. For the [path](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) of "bringing down the elephant," each person's [antecedents](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) include others striking at the same time, so if the researcher counts the companions' strikes into the hunting [mode](concepts.md#c09), the hunting [mode](concepts.md#c09) spans [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25).

<note>

*Example: One person cannot bring down an elephant; it takes two or three people striking at the same time. The conditions of the "bring down the elephant" path include others striking at the same time, so if the researcher counts the companions into the hunting mode, the mode spans nodes.*

</note>

### 5.4 Correction and Prediction

**Conclusion 5.3**: The [outcome feedback](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c05) of each [check window](concepts.md#c03) may change the later [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) of the participating [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16); if these [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) have [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) that are not [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12) in the next [check window](concepts.md#c03), these [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) may differ accordingly; but the change has no preset direction and does not guarantee being more favorable to the continuation of the [mode](concepts.md#c09).

<note>

*Conclusion 5.3: The outcome feedback of each window may change the participants' later weight; if they have outputs that are not locked in in the next window, these outputs may differ accordingly. But the change has no fixed direction and does not guarantee being favorable to the continuation of the mode.*

</note>

(Derived from the definitions of [learning](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c04), [outcome feedback](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c05), [collapse](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c08), [lock-in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12) and [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28): [outcome feedback](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c05) is the later interaction in which a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16)'s later [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) back at itself; [learning](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c04) is the process in which [arriving](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) material may change later [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06), and is a result condition, does not point to a purpose, has no preset direction, and guarantees neither that something is learned nor that it is learned correctly; the result of a [collapse](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c08) depends on [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06), and an [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) is such a result; when something is not [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12) at some [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09), it is not the case that only one result of its next [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) has non-zero [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06))

<note>

*The source is the definitions of learning, outcome feedback, collapse, lock-in and output: outcome feedback is the later interaction in which a wo's later output comes back to itself; learning is the process in which arriving material may change later weight; it is a result, does not point to a purpose, has no preset direction, and guarantees neither that something is learned nor that it is learned correctly; the result of a collapse depends on weight, and an output is such a result; when something is not locked in at some moment, its next moment has more than one possible result.*

</note>

Calibration: A stall owner is complained about as too expensive; next week he may lower prices, or change to a different batch of customers, or simply not set up; lowering prices also does not guarantee that business gets better.

<note>

*Example: A stall owner is complained about as too expensive; he may lower prices, change customers, or simply not set up; lowering prices also does not guarantee that business gets better.*

## 6. Economic Modes Becoming Exterior

An [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) is not itself a [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25). Saying that it "becomes an [exterior](concepts.md#c04)" means that the [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) in the [mode](concepts.md#c09) [arrive](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) outside the [mode](concepts.md#c09) (see 0.6).

<note>

*An economic mode is not itself a node. Saying it "becomes an exterior" means that outputs of nodes in the mode arrive at nodes outside the mode.*

</note>

### 6.1 Two Routes

**Conclusion 6.1 (through an [interface node](concepts.md#c11))**: When the [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of an [interface node](concepts.md#c11) of an [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at a [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) outside the [mode](concepts.md#c09), these [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) become part of the [exterior](concepts.md#c04) of those [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25).

<note>

*Conclusion 6.1: When the output of the mode's interface node arrives at nodes outside the mode, these outputs become part of those nodes' exterior.*

</note>

(Derived from the definitions of [interface node](concepts.md#c11) and [exterior](concepts.md#c04))

<note>

*Source: the definitions of interface node and exterior.*

</note>

Calibration: A bank teller in town says "yes" or "no" to someone coming to borrow money; those words are the borrower's [exterior](concepts.md#c04).

<note>

*Example: A bank teller says "yes" or "no" to someone coming to borrow money; those words are the borrower's exterior.*

</note>

**Conclusion 6.2 (through the [natural environment](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c16))**: When the [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of a [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) in an [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at the [natural environment](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c16), and the changed part later [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at other [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), these [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) indirectly participate in the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) of those [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16); even if there is no [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) between [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) and [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) on the two sides.

<note>

*Conclusion 6.2: When the output of a node in the mode arrives at the natural environment, and the changed part later arrives at other wos, these outputs indirectly affect the weight of those wos, even if the two sides have never had direct contact.*

</note>

(Derived from the definitions of [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10), [natural environment](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c16) and [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16))

<note>

*Source: the definitions of arrival, natural environment and wo.*

</note>

Explanation:
1. A [presentation](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04) is any part of the [universe](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c02), and the parts of the [natural environment](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c16) are also [presentations](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04); the [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of a [mode](concepts.md#c09)'s [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) is the result of a [collapse](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c08), and if that [collapse](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c08) [involves](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c11) some part of the [natural environment](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c16), this [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at that part (the definitions of [presentation](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04), [natural environment](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c16), [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28), [involvement](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c11) and [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)).
2. The [natural environment](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c16) is the part that belongs to no [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) yet has [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) [arriving](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) (the definition of [natural environment](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c16)), so its changed part can [arrive](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at other [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25).
3. What [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at other [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) enters the [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) of those [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) and participates in their later [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) (the definition of [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16)).
4. In the chain of 1 to 3 there is no [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) from any [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) to a [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25).

<note>

*Derivation: The natural environment is also part of the universe, and if the collapse that an output results from involves it, the result arrives at it; the changed part of the natural environment can in turn arrive at other nodes; what arrives at a wo enters its history and affects later weight; in the whole chain there is no direct arrival from node to node.*

</note>

Calibration: Nitrogen fertilizer from farmland in the American Midwest flows with the Mississippi River into the sea, and a large hypoxic zone appears in the Gulf of Mexico every summer, changing the catch of the local shrimping industry. The farmers and the fishers have never been in contact with each other.

<note>

*Example: Nitrogen fertilizer from Midwestern farmland flows with the river into the sea, a hypoxic zone appears in the Gulf of Mexico in summer, and the catch of the local shrimping industry changes. The farmers and fishers have never been in contact.*

</note>

### 6.2 Influence Does Not Require Participation

**Conclusion 6.3**: Even if a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) does not participate in some [economic mode](concepts.md#c09), as long as the [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) in the [mode](concepts.md#c09) [arrive](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at it by any route, these [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) participate in its later [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06).

<note>

*Conclusion 6.3: Even if a wo does not participate in a mode, as long as the outputs of nodes in the mode arrive at it in any way, these outputs affect its later weight.*

</note>

(Derived from the definitions of [edge](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c29) and [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16): the [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of one [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) participates in the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) of another [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) ([edge](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c29)); the entire [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) that [has arrived](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) participates in its [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) at the next [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) ([wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16)). This paper adds: the routes of [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) include the two of Conclusions 6.1 and 6.2)

<note>

*Source: the definition of edge (an output participates in another node's weight) and the definition of wo (the entire history that has arrived affects its weight at the next moment). This paper adds: the routes of arrival include the two of Conclusions 6.1 and 6.2.*

</note>

Calibration: When a bank opens in town, people who do not open accounts there are also affected, for example neighbors starting to borrow money to do business, and land prices changing. Participants are only a small part of those affected.

<note>

*Example: When a bank opens in town, people who do not open accounts are also affected, for example neighbors starting to borrow money to do business, and land prices changing. Participants are only a small part of those affected.*

</note>

### 6.3 Types of Interface

**Conclusion 6.4**: An [exterior](concepts.md#c04) [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) meets [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) sources of different character through different types of [interface node](concepts.md#c11):

<note>

*Conclusion 6.4: Through different kinds of interface nodes, the exterior meets sources of different character.*

</note>

- **Through a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16)**: the [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) comes from a [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) whose [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) changes with [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13), so the same request may get different responses; if both are a [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) and each one's [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) [arrive](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at the other in a stated dimension, they can accumulate a [dedicated history](../paper_ae79e938_76e1_4bf9_b2ab_995c03e16c9e/concepts.md#c05) and may form [tacit coordination](../paper_ae79e938_76e1_4bf9_b2ab_995c03e16c9e/concepts.md#c06);
- **Through a [signal container](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c26)**: if a [locked-in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12) [signal container](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c26) lists [templates](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c14), they do not change by themselves and provide a fixed point, obtainable without first having [resonance](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c09); a container is not a [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19), so it cannot form a [dedicated history](../paper_ae79e938_76e1_4bf9_b2ab_995c03e16c9e/concepts.md#c05) or [tacit coordination](../paper_ae79e938_76e1_4bf9_b2ab_995c03e16c9e/concepts.md#c06) with it;
- **Through a [random source](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c27)**: when the [random source](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c27) is not [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12), only the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) of its own result can be spoken of; the [random source](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c27) is not a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), hence not a [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19), so no [dedicated history](../paper_ae79e938_76e1_4bf9_b2ab_995c03e16c9e/concepts.md#c05) or [tacit coordination](../paper_ae79e938_76e1_4bf9_b2ab_995c03e16c9e/concepts.md#c06) can be formed with it.

<note>

*Three kinds of interface: a wo, whose response may differ by history, and if both sides are a "self" and each one's outputs arrive at the other in a stated dimension, a dedicated history can accumulate and tacit coordination may form; a signal container, whose templates, if it lists any, are fixed and can be obtained without first resonating, but a container is not a "self" and cannot form a dedicated history or tacit coordination with it; a random source, whose own result, when it is not locked in, can be spoken of only in terms of weight; it is not a wo and so not a "self," and no dedicated history or tacit coordination can be formed with it.*

</note>

(Derived from the definition of [interface node](concepts.md#c11), and from the definitions of [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19), [signal container](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c26), [random source](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c27), [dedicated history](../paper_ae79e938_76e1_4bf9_b2ab_995c03e16c9e/concepts.md#c05) and [tacit coordination](../paper_ae79e938_76e1_4bf9_b2ab_995c03e16c9e/concepts.md#c06))

<note>

*Source: the definition of interface node, plus the definitions of wo, self, signal container, random source, dedicated history and tacit coordination.*

</note>

Calibration: The same bank is two different kinds of [exterior](concepts.md#c04) to a teller and to an ATM: the teller may be accommodating today and not tomorrow; the ATM follows the rules every time, but will not remember you either.

<note>

*Example: The same bank: the teller may be accommodating today and not tomorrow; the ATM follows the rules every time and will not remember you. To you they are two different exteriors.*

</note>

### 6.4 Other Dimensions of Participants

**Conclusion 6.5**: When the [outcome feedback](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c05) of an [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at a participating [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), what it takes part in is its entire [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) distribution, not limited to the dimension delimited by the researcher. So that [outcome feedback](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c05) may participate in its [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) on other dimensions.

<note>

*Conclusion 6.5: When the mode's outcome feedback reaches a person, what it takes part in is his entire weight distribution, not limited to the dimension the researcher drew; so the outcome feedback may affect his outputs on other dimensions.*

</note>

(Derived from the definition of [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), the definition of [outcome feedback](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c05) and the definition of [own segment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c17))

<note>

*Source: the definition of wo, the definition of outcome feedback and the definition of "own segment."*

</note>

Explanation:
1. The [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) of a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) depends on the entire [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) that has [arrived](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at it (the definition of [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16)).
2. The [own segment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c17) is determined by dependence and not by any [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) (the definition of [own segment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c17)); the dimension is the researcher's [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18).
3. So a piece of [outcome feedback](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c05) participates in the entire [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) distribution and is not limited by the dimension the researcher draws.

<note>

*Derivation: A wo's weight depends on the entire history that has arrived at it; the "own segment" is determined by dependence, not by any distinction, while the dimension is the researcher's distinction; so a piece of outcome feedback affects the entire weight distribution and is not limited by the dimension the researcher draws.*

</note>

Calibration: Working at a bank changes more than the [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) at work; household finances and spending habits may change too, or no change may be visible.

<note>

*Example: Working at a bank changes more than the outputs at work; household finances and spending habits may change too, or no change may be visible.*

</note>

### 6.5 The Direction of Influence

**Conclusion 6.6**: After the [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of a [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) in the [mode](concepts.md#c09) [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at an [exterior](concepts.md#c04) [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25), if the [exterior](concepts.md#c04) [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) is a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) or [random source](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c27) and is not [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12) at the [moment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) just before its next [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28), then its next [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) cannot be uniquely derived beforehand, and what can be derived is only the [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) of each result; unless all results with nonzero [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) point in the same direction, the direction of influence cannot be derived either.

<note>

*Conclusion 6.6: After the output of a node in the mode arrives at an exterior node, if that node is a wo or random source and is not locked in at the moment just before its next output, its next output cannot be uniquely derived beforehand; only the weight of each result can be derived; unless the possible results all point the same way, not even the direction can be derived.*

</note>

(Derived from the definitions of the [Law of the Black Box](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c25) and [random source](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c27): when not [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12), only [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) can be discussed, not results)

<note>

*Source: the definitions of the Law of the Black Box and random source: when not locked in, only the weight can be derived, not the result.*

</note>

Calibration: A convenience store opens at the street corner; some people start eating late-night snacks, some carry on as before, and some therefore close their own grocery stores; before the opening, no one can say who will change how.

<note>

*Example: A convenience store opens at the street corner; some people start eating late-night snacks, some carry on as before, and some close their grocery stores; before the opening, no one can say who will change how.*

</note>

## 7. Economic Modes as Each Other's Exterior

### 7.1 A Cross-Mode Loop Forms When Output Returns to Itself through Another Mode

**Conclusion 7.1**: If the [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of a participating [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) in [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) A [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at a participating [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) of B, and the later [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of that [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) in B, or of other [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) in B connected to it by [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10), in turn [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at that [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) of A, and none of these [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) belongs to both A and B, then A and B are part of each other's [exterior](concepts.md#c04); the [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of that [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) passes through B, returns to its own [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) and participates in its own later [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06). Under these conditions, A and B together form a cross-[mode](concepts.md#c09) [loop](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c31).

<note>

*Conclusion 7.1: If the output of a wo in mode A arrives at a wo in B, and the later output of that wo (or of other wos in B connected to it) in turn returns to that wo of A, and none of these wos belongs to both modes, the two modes are part of each other's exterior; that wo's output passes through B, returns to its own history and affects its own later weight. Under these conditions, together they form a cross-mode loop.*

</note>

(Derived from Conclusions 6.1, 6.2 and 6.3, and from the definitions of [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), [loop](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c31) and [outcome feedback](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c05))

<note>

*Source: Conclusions 6.1, 6.2 and 6.3, plus the definitions of wo, loop and outcome feedback: the consequences brought back after a wo's output return to that wo itself.*

</note>

Calibration: A bank lends to a merchant, and the merchant's revenue flows back to the bank. The bank and the merchant are each a [mode](concepts.md#c09), and together they form a larger [loop](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c31).

<note>

*Example: A bank lends to a merchant, and the merchant's revenue returns to the bank. The bank and the merchant are each a mode, and together they form a larger loop.*

</note>

### 7.2 Relations of Being Each Other's Exterior

If there is [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) between A and B, the researcher may, under a stated dimension, time window and compatibility criterion, identify the following relations according to the upstream definitions:

<note>

*If there is arrival between two modes, the researcher may, under stated conditions, identify the following three relations according to the upstream definitions.*

</note>

- **[Resonance](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c09)**: the [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of the participating [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) of A and B repeatedly connect compatibly.
- **[Taiji](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c39)**: A and B each contain a [positive feedback](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c32) [loop](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c31) in some direction, in opposite directions, [coupled](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c36) to each other and [diverging](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c38) within the [coupling](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c36). By Conclusion 5.1, in any [mode](concepts.md#c09) in which a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) participates each [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) has a [small loop](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c02), so it must still be determined whether there is a [positive feedback](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c32) [loop](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c31) in it, whether the directions are opposite, and whether they [diverge](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c38).
- **[Mismatch](../paper_ae79e938_76e1_4bf9_b2ab_995c03e16c9e/concepts.md#c08)**: adjacent [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) between the [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) concerned are incompatible under the stated criterion.

<note>

*Three relations: resonance (outputs repeatedly connect compatibly), taiji (each contains a positive feedback loop, in opposite directions, coupled to each other and diverging), mismatch (adjacent outputs are incompatible). In a mode with a wo, each wo has a small loop, but whether it is positive feedback, whether the directions are opposite and whether they diverge still has to be determined case by case.*

</note>

**Conclusion 7.2**: [Resonance](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c09) and [taiji](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c39) can coexist, so the two are not mutually exclusive; relations other than [resonance](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c09), [taiji](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c39) and [mismatch](../paper_ae79e938_76e1_4bf9_b2ab_995c03e16c9e/concepts.md#c08) are not excluded by this paper.

<note>

*Conclusion 7.2: Resonance and taiji can hold at the same time, so they do not exclude each other; relations beyond these three are not excluded by this paper.*

</note>

(Derived from the definitions of [resonance](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c09) and [taiji](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c39): [resonance](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c09) requires repeated compatible connection under the condition of [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10), while [taiji](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c39) requires [positive feedback](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c32) [loops](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c31) in opposite directions to be [coupled](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c36) and [diverge](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c38); the two criteria differ, so a [taiji](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c39) with opposite directions may still connect compatibly in sequence)

<note>

*Source: Resonance requires that outputs repeatedly connect compatibly under the condition of arrival, while taiji requires opposite directions and divergence; the two standards differ, so a taiji with opposite directions may still connect compatibly in the output sequence.*

</note>

Calibration: Two rival chain stores fight a price war that grows fiercer, and this is [taiji](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c39); at the same time, their trucks stagger their deliveries on the same road every day, connecting compatibly on the dimension of traffic.

<note>

*Example: Two rival chain stores fight a price war that grows fiercer, and this is taiji; at the same time, their trucks stagger their deliveries on the same road every day, which is compatible connection on the dimension of traffic.*

</note>

Also: In 1975 Pepsi launched the "Pepsi Challenge" blind taste test; on 23 April 1985 Coca-Cola announced a changed formula and introduced "New Coke", and on 11 July of the same year, under strong consumer backlash, announced the return of the original formula. The [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of each side took the other as reference; if the researcher judges that the two each contain [positive feedback](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c32) [loops](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c31) in opposite directions that [diverge](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c38) within the [coupling](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c36), this meets the criterion of [taiji](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c39), which requires the dimension and the data to be stated separately.

<note>

*Supplementary example: The competition between Coca-Cola and Pepsi. If the researcher judges that each side contains a positive feedback loop in opposite directions, coupled and diverging, it meets the standard of taiji, but the dimension and the data must be stated separately.*

</note>

### 7.3 The Supply and Order of Antecedents

**Conclusion 7.3**: If an [antecedent](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) of some [path](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) of A can be satisfied only by the [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of a [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) in B [arriving](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10), then that [path](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) is feasible only after the corresponding [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of B has [arrived](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10).

<note>

*Conclusion 7.3: If a condition needed by one of A's paths can be satisfied only by an output from a node of B, then that path is feasible only after B's corresponding output has arrived.*

</note>

(Derived from the definition of [threshold](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c03) and Premise 3: if any [antecedent](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) is not satisfied, the [path](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) is not feasible in that round; an [antecedent](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) can be satisfied by the [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) of an [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) (Premise 3), so when an [antecedent](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) can be supplied only by the [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of a [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) in B, the [path](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) is feasible only after the corresponding [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) has [arrived](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10). This paper adds: the order can cross [modes](concepts.md#c09))

<note>

*Source: the definition of threshold (if any condition is not satisfied, the path is not feasible) and Premise 3 (a condition can be satisfied by an output arriving). This paper adds: the order relation can cross modes.*

</note>

**Conclusion 7.4**: So if an [antecedent](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) of some [path](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) of A can be supplied only by the [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of a [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) in B, and A's [identity condition](concepts.md#c02) requires this [path](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) and allows no substitute, then A can appear only after B's corresponding [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) has [arrived](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10); from the supply relations of [antecedents](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08), a partial order can be drawn between an [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) and the [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) that supply it. But the [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) of B's corresponding [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) does not imply that A will appear.

<note>

*Conclusion 7.4: So if a condition of one of A's paths can only be supplied by B, and A must take that path, A can appear only after B's corresponding output has arrived, and a partial order can be drawn between a mode and the outputs that supply it; but B's output arriving does not mean A will certainly appear.*

</note>

(Derived from Conclusion 7.3 and the definition of [threshold](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c03): satisfied [antecedents](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) mean only feasibility, not that it will occur; for the second half see also Conclusion 4.5)

<note>

*Source: Conclusion 7.3, plus the definition of threshold (satisfied conditions mean only that it can go, not that it will go).*

</note>

Explanation:
1. If A's [identity condition](concepts.md#c02) requires the [path](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) above (allowing no other [path](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) in its place), then for A to appear that [path](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) must be realized, so the corresponding [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of B must first [arrive](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10).
2. This gives only a partial order: where there is no supply relation, no order can be drawn from supply relations.

<note>

*Derivation: If A's identity condition requires that path and allows no other path in its place, then for A to appear the path must be realized, and B's output must arrive first; but only a partial order can be drawn, and where there is no supply relation none can be drawn from supply relations.*

</note>

Calibration: Only with cold-chain transport can a sashimi shop appear inland; but a city that has a cold chain does not necessarily have anyone opening a sashimi shop.

<note>

*Example: Only with cold-chain transport can a sashimi shop appear inland; but a city that has a cold chain does not necessarily have anyone opening a sashimi shop.*

</note>

Also: On 15 February 1882 the refrigerated ship *Dunedin* left Port Chalmers, New Zealand, carrying about five thousand frozen sheep carcasses, and arrived in London in late May; the frozen meat trade began there and later became the core of New Zealand's twentieth-century economy. A sheep-farming [mode](concepts.md#c09) whose output is frozen-mutton export, and whose [identity condition](concepts.md#c02) allows no other [path](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) in its place, could appear only after refrigerated transport.

<note>

*Supplementary example: In 1882 a refrigerated ship carried mutton from New Zealand to London, and the frozen meat trade began. A sheep-farming mode whose output is frozen-mutton export, with no other path allowed in its place, could appear only after refrigerated transport.*

</note>

### 7.4 The Chain of Supply Interruption

**Conclusion 7.5**: If the [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of a [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) in B, through [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10), is judged to be an [antecedent](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) of some [path](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) of A, and that [antecedent](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) stays satisfied only while this [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) keeps [arriving](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10), then when that [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) no longer [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10), that [antecedent](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) is no longer supplied by B; if there is no substitute source, the corresponding [path](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) of A is not feasible.

<note>

*Conclusion 7.5: If the output of a node in B is judged to be the antecedent of one of A's paths, and that antecedent holds only while the output keeps arriving, then when that output no longer arrives, the antecedent is no longer supplied by B; if there is no substitute source, that path of A is not feasible.*

</note>

## 8. Reproductive Modes

**[Reproductive mode](concepts.md#c12)**: An [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) whose output is [offspring](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c15). A reproductive event need not by itself constitute a [reproductive mode](concepts.md#c12): only when the researcher, by a [persistence criterion](concepts.md#c01), tracks the structure it belongs to as a recurring [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) is that structure a [reproductive mode](concepts.md#c12).

### 11.1 Gold Mining and Supply in the Mining Region (1848 to 1856)

Note on materials: What was read is the Wikipedia entries "California gold rush" and "Women in the California gold rush", and a National Park Service page on buried ships in San Francisco, with the relevant passages extracted by a tool, not the original files. Head counts are mostly estimates, and the sources do not fully agree.

<note>

*Note on materials for the first case: What was read is pages from Wikipedia and the National Park Service, with passages extracted by a tool, not the original files; head counts are mostly estimates, and the sources do not necessarily agree.*

</note>

1. **[Cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)**: The scope is the California mining region and San Francisco, with the year as the [check window](concepts.md#c03). The [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) are gold seekers, merchants, restaurants, hotels, tailors, ships and crews. The tracked structure (hereafter the gold-rush structure): gold seekers obtain gold dust → the gold dust [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at merchants → supplies, food, lodging and clothing [arrive](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at the gold seekers → the gold seekers dig again.
2. **Initial state**: Before 1848, San Francisco had about a thousand people, and the [records](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06) contain no gold-rush structure.
3. **New [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)**: On 24 January 1848, Marshall found gold dust in the millrace at Sutter's Mill, which for everyone was an [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) from the [exterior](concepts.md#c04). Wherever the news [arrived](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10), people came from there: about six thousand people by the end of 1848, about ninety thousand in 1849, and at least three hundred thousand by 1855; in 1852 alone more than twenty thousand Chinese landed in San Francisco.
4. **The structure that appeared**:
   - The gold-rush structure reappeared year after year from 1848. Gold seekers came and went in great numbers and the participating [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) were replaced almost every year, but this positional structure did not change.
   - Gold seekers selling gold dust, eating, lodging and buying clothes each needed the [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of other [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25), so this case counts those [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) into the gold-rush structure, and the structure spans [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) from the first [window](concepts.md#c03).
   - Sam Brannan bought up prospecting supplies in San Francisco and resold them; in 1852 Wells Fargo began handling exchange and delivery in California; in 1853 Levi Strauss came to San Francisco to deal in cloth and dry goods.
   - Crews and passengers went ashore to mine for gold; hundreds of ships lay unmanned in the harbor, and some were converted into warehouses, taverns, hotels and even prisons.
5. **Criterion and [break](concepts.md#c10)**:
   - The mining method began with the pan, then the rocker box and the long tom, and later the diversion of water; around 1850 most of the easily obtained gold dust had been mined out; by 1855 only medium-sized and large groups made any gain.
   - If the researcher cuts out the segment of "mining" alone and states the [identity condition](concepts.md#c02) on the panning method, then this cut-out structure underwent a [criterion break](concepts.md#c10) between about 1850 and 1855; if it is stated on the positional structure of the gold-rush structure as a whole, then the mining method changed but the gold-rush structure did not [break](concepts.md#c10).
   - If the criterion of the shipping [mode](concepts.md#c09) is stated on the [path](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) of crews sailing the ships, it underwent a [criterion break](concepts.md#c10) in the harbor, because the [antecedent](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) of crews was no longer satisfied.
6. **What it shows**:
   - Identity looks at position, not at who: with the participating [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) all replaced, the gold-rush structure can still be judged the same one (3.4).
   - Premise 1 held from the start: for gold seekers' food, lodging, clothing and the sale of gold dust, the [antecedents](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) could be satisfied only by the [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of other [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) [arriving](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10), so these [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) had to be supplied by other [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) (Conclusion 5.2); this case counts them into the gold-rush structure, so the structure spans [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25). There is no Robinson Crusoe style of gold mining.
   - "Solo gold mining" and "group gold mining" are the researcher's [distinctions](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) of the mining segment, not two [modes](concepts.md#c09) each independent in the [output graph set](concepts.md#c07); depending on which segment the criterion is stated on, and whether it is stated on the [path](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) or on the result, whether there is a [break](concepts.md#c10) may differ (Conclusion 5.9).
   - The [paths](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) of merchants, exchange and cloth could appear only after the gold seekers, if the researcher sets their [antecedents](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) as satisfiable only by the gold seekers' [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) (Conclusions 7.3, 7.4).
   - When crews left their ships, the [antecedent](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) of the shipping [mode](concepts.md#c09) was no longer supplied by the original [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25), forming a chain (Conclusion 7.5); when abandoned ships were turned into warehouses, the [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of the shipping [mode](concepts.md#c09) became the [antecedents](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) of other [paths](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) (Conclusion 5.8).
   - Reproduction: In 1850 women made up about 4.5% of the non-indigenous population; about 10% in 1852; in 1870 the male-to-female ratio was about 100 to 38; only the 1950 census came close to parity. The early [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) came almost entirely from outside, so the reproductive events supplying [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) were outside the [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23), and only later did reproductive events inside the [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23) gradually increase (Conclusion 8.2).
7. **What can be said and what cannot be derived**:
   - Can be said: in these [records](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06), the positional structure of the gold-rush structure reappears year after year while participants are replaced in great numbers; every item of the gold seekers' livelihood came from other [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25); the mining method changed; ships were put to other uses.
   - Cannot be derived: why any gold seeker came or left; why the gold-rush structure formed here and not elsewhere; whether a given person would stay the next year; whether such accounts as "getting rich" or "failing" are true.

<note>

*First case (gold mining and supply): The cut is the California mining region and San Francisco, with the year as the window. The tracked structure is gold seekers digging gold, selling it to merchants, and digging again with the supplies they get. After the news spread, people poured in from everywhere; participants were replaced every year, yet the structure reappeared year after year; every item of livelihood depended on others' supply, so it necessarily spans nodes; the businesses of merchants, exchange and cloth are later paths; crews leaving their ships broke the shipping mode; the early nodes came almost entirely from outside. What can be said is that the structure reappeared year after year while participants were replaced in great numbers; what cannot be derived is individuals' motives and fates.*

</note>

### 11.2 The Mining Region and Its Exterior (1848 to 1910)

Note on materials: What was read is the Wikipedia entries "Hydraulic mining", "Woodruff v. North Bloomfield Gravel Mining Company" and "California gold rush", extracted by a tool, not the original files. There are very few first-hand records from the Indigenous side, so what follows writes only the [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) and the consequences that can be [recorded](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06).

<note>

*Note on materials for the second case: What was read is Wikipedia entries, extracted by a tool; there are very few first-hand records from the Indigenous side, so only arrival and consequences that can be recorded are written here.*

</note>

1. **[Cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)**: The [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) fall into four categories: the [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) taking part in the mining region's [modes](concepts.md#c09); the [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) engaged in agriculture and shipping in the downstream Sacramento Valley; California's Indigenous groups; foreign miners. The [natural environment](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c16) includes rivers and land. The [check window](concepts.md#c03) is the year.
2. **Initial state**: Before the gold rush, California had about fifteen thousand residents of European descent and of Mexican-Californian descent; the [modes](concepts.md#c09) of the Indigenous groups already existed.
3. **New [arrivals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)**:
   - In 1853, Matteson used high-pressure water jets near Nevada City to wash gold-bearing gravel. This [path](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) required bringing water from high ground, with pipes and nozzles; by the early 1860s it already required large organizations and large sums of capital.
   - Sediment, rubble and heavy metals were discharged into rivers, raising the riverbeds, causing man-made floods, destroying large tracts of farmland in the valley, flooding towns, and also obstructing steamboat navigation.
   - [Arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) between the mining region and the Indigenous peoples: killing, expulsion, and changes to rivers and land. Madley estimates that between 1846 and 1873 at least 9,400 to 16,000 California Indigenous people were killed by non-Indigenous people, in more than 370 massacres.
   - A tax on foreign miners: twenty US dollars per month.
4. **The structure that appeared**:
   - The [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of the mining [modes](concepts.md#c09) [arrived](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at wheat farmers through the river, and the farmers' lawsuit in turn [arrived](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at the mining region: the suit was filed in 1882, and on 7 January 1884 Judge Sawyer ruled that the mines could no longer discharge sediment into the river channels.
   - The [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of the mining region [arrived](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at the Indigenous groups, and the [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of the Indigenous groups also [arrived](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at the mining region; the two sides were each other's [exterior](concepts.md#c04).
5. **Criterion and [break](concepts.md#c10)**:
   - "Hydraulic mining with sediment discharged into river channels" underwent a [criterion break](concepts.md#c10) after 1884; "hydraulic mining" continued on a smaller scale, changed after 1893 to adding debris dams, and Malakoff Diggins closed only in 1910.
   - The [modes](concepts.md#c09) of many Indigenous groups underwent a [criterion break](concepts.md#c10).
6. **What it shows**:
   - Only after surface gold was mined out and water and capital were in place did the [path](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) of hydraulic mining enter the [feasible set](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c11) (Conclusion 5.7).
   - Wheat farmers and the mines did not necessarily have direct [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10), yet the mines' [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) changed them through the river (Conclusion 6.2); influence does not require participation (Conclusion 6.3).
   - To the mining region the Indigenous peoples were [exterior](concepts.md#c04), and to the Indigenous peoples the mining region was [exterior](concepts.md#c04); the two formed a cross-[mode](concepts.md#c09) [loop](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c31) (Conclusion 7.1). In this [loop](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c31), the [modes](concepts.md#c09) of many Indigenous groups underwent a [criterion break](concepts.md#c10) while the mining region's [modes](concepts.md#c09) continued; under the compatibility criterion, the relation between the two is [mismatch](../paper_ae79e938_76e1_4bf9_b2ab_995c03e16c9e/concepts.md#c08) (7.2).
   - The foreign miners' tax is the [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of a [signal container](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c26), and when it [arrived](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at the foreign miners its content did not change with the person (Conclusion 6.4).
   - In this case, the three criteria give different times of [break](concepts.md#c10): 1884, 1893 and 1910 correspond to different [identity conditions](concepts.md#c02) (3.5, Conclusion 5.9).
7. **What can be said and what cannot be derived**:
   - Can be said: the mining region's [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) [arrived](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) downstream through the river; there was a great deal of [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) between the mining region and the Indigenous peoples, after which the [modes](concepts.md#c09) of many Indigenous groups no longer appeared; after the ruling, the structure of discharging sediment into rivers no longer appeared.
   - Cannot be derived: the motives of either side; whether the mining region acted "in order to" drive out the Indigenous peoples; what the Indigenous groups would have become without the gold rush; whether the ruling was "correct".

<note>

*Second case (the mining region and its exterior): After hydraulic mining appeared, sediment was discharged into river channels and affected downstream agriculture and shipping; farmers sued, and in 1884 the ruling prohibited sediment discharge; there was a great deal of arrival between the mining region and the Indigenous peoples, the modes of many Indigenous groups broke, and the mining region's modes continued. With different criteria, the year of the break also differs. What can be said is these arrivals and results; what cannot be derived is the motive of either side.*

</note>

## 13. The General Proposition

Under a stated [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23), the [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of biological humans can be [recorded](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c06) as an [output graph set](concepts.md#c07), recorded on a [population graph](concepts.md#c05). The structures in it that the researcher tracks by a [persistence criterion](concepts.md#c01) and that appear repeatedly are [economic modes](concepts.md#c09); in every [mode](concepts.md#c09) in which a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) participates, each [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) has a [small loop](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c02); if some [path](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) is among the [paths](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c07) referred to by Premise 1 and is realized within some [check window](concepts.md#c03), then the [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of some other [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) must supply its [antecedent](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08), and whether that is counted into the [mode](concepts.md#c09) is decided by the researcher's [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18). The [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) in a [mode](concepts.md#c09), through [interface nodes](concepts.md#c11) and the [natural environment](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c16), can become the [exterior](concepts.md#c04) of other [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25), and influence does not require participation. [Economic modes](concepts.md#c09) that [arrive](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at each other and share no participating [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) are each other's [exterior](concepts.md#c04); when the [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) of some [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) returns to it through a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) in another [mode](concepts.md#c09), this forms a cross-[mode](concepts.md#c09) [loop](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c31); among them [resonance](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c09), [taiji](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c39) and [mismatch](../paper_ae79e938_76e1_4bf9_b2ab_995c03e16c9e/concepts.md#c08) can be identified by criteria, and interruptions of supply are transmitted in a chain as judged through [antecedents](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08). The supply of [antecedents](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) gives a partial order between a [mode](concepts.md#c09) and the [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) that supply it; if every biological human [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) will undergo [criterion exit](../paper_96f10d75_23d0_441f_94c7_ab87ca28d284/concepts.md#c09), then for every [mode](concepts.md#c09) that takes biological humans as [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25), its biological human [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) ultimately come from reproductive events; when these events are tracked as a [reproductive mode](concepts.md#c12) (an [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) that produces [offspring](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c15)), if the [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) it supplies is interrupted, the interruption can be transmitted along the supply relations of [antecedents](../paper_81952268_47b1_4d8c_9605_270c42646693/concepts.md#c08) to the [modes](concepts.md#c09) that depend on these [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25).

<note>

*General proposition (first paragraph): Record the outputs of biological humans as an output graph set, and the structures that the researcher tracks and that appear repeatedly are economic modes; modes that arrive at each other and share no participants are each other's exterior, and resonance, taiji and mismatch can be identified; the supply of antecedents gives a partial order between a mode and the outputs that supply it; if biological human nodes all exit, then for every mode that takes biological humans as nodes, its biological human nodes ultimately come from reproductive events; when these are tracked as a reproductive mode and it is interrupted, the interruption can be transmitted along antecedent relations to the modes that depend on these nodes.*

</note>

The formation of an [economic mode](concepts.md#c09) can only be identified afterward; its continuation and specific influence, when the criterion or the [exterior](concepts.md#c04) [nodes](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) depend on [wos](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) or [random sources](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c27) that are not [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12) (under the conditions of Conclusions 5.4 and 6.6), likewise cannot be uniquely derived beforehand and can only be judged [window](concepts.md#c03) by [window](concepts.md#c03); the [economic modes](concepts.md#c09) observed are all ones that so far have not undergone a [criterion break](concepts.md#c10). Ownership, institutions, price, money, organizations, markets and the like can be derived only by introducing [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18); this paper has not yet introduced their premises, and they are left for later work that derives them with this paper as the base layer.

<note>

*General proposition (second paragraph): How a mode forms can only be identified afterward; its continuation and specific influence, when the criterion or the exterior depends on wos or random sources that are not locked in (under the conditions of Conclusions 5.4 and 6.6), likewise cannot be uniquely derived beforehand and can only be judged window by window; what we see are all ones that have not yet broken; ownership, institutions, price, money and the like can be derived only after distinction is introduced, and are left for later.*

</note>
