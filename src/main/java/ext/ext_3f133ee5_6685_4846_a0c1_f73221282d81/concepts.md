## public 債務超過一千美元 / Debt Over 1000 Dollars

- 定義：該網頁文章所述：林肯與〔Berry〕合夥的店垮了之後，林肯承擔了剩餘的債務，合計超過一千美元（文章換算為今日約兩萬七千美元）；林肯戲稱這筆債為「國債」，花了許多年才還清。
- Definition: The web article states that after the store Lincoln ran with Berry failed, Lincoln assumed the remaining debts, totaling more than 1,000 dollars (about 27,000 dollars in today's valuation by the article's conversion); he jokingly called it his "national debt" and took many years to repay it.
