## public 一八四八年還清債務 / Debt Paid Off in 1848

- 定義：該網頁所述：直到一八四八年，林肯任美國聯邦眾議員時，才終於還清他稱為「國債」的債務。
- Definition: The web page states that as late as 1848, while serving as a United States Congressman, Lincoln was finally able to pay off what he called "the national debt".

<a id="c02"></a>
## public 一八三三年五月七日任命為郵務員 / Appointed Postmaster on May 7 1833

- 定義：該網頁所述：林肯於一八三三年五月七日獲任命為新薩勒姆的郵務員。
- Definition: The web page states that Lincoln received an appointment as postmaster of New Salem on May 7, 1833.
