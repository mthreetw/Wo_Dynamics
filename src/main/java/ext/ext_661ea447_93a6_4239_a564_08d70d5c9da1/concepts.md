## public 一八三六年九月十九日取得執業執照 / License to Practice Law on September 19 1836

- 定義：該傳記條目所述：林肯於一八三六年九月十九日取得執業執照。
- Definition: The biographical entry states that Lincoln received his license to practice law on September 19, 1836.

<a id="c02"></a>
## public 一八五八年七場辯論 / Seven Debates in 1858

- 定義：該傳記條目所述：一八五八年八月二十一日至十月十五日，林肯與〔Douglas〕在伊利諾州各地進行七場辯論；林肯雖輸掉該年選舉，辯論卻帶來全國知名度，並為一八六〇年的共和黨提名鋪路。
- Definition: The biographical entry states that between August 21 and October 15, 1858, Lincoln and Douglas held a series of seven debates across Illinois; although Lincoln lost the 1858 election, the debates brought him national recognition and set the stage for his 1860 Republican nomination.

<a id="c03"></a>
## public 一八六五年四月十四日遇刺 / Shot on April 14 1865

- 定義：該傳記條目所述：一八六五年四月十四日，林肯被〔John Wilkes Booth〕開槍擊中。
- Definition: The biographical entry states that on April 14, 1865 Lincoln was shot by John Wilkes Booth.
