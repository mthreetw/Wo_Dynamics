## public 一八三六年九月九日取得執業執照 / License to Practice Law on September 9 1836

- 定義：該網頁所述：一八三六年三月二十四日，桑加蒙郡巡迴法院證明林肯品行良好；同年九月九日，伊利諾州最高法院審查林肯，發給他在全州所有法院執業的執照。
- Definition: The web page states that on March 24, 1836 the Sangamon County Circuit Court certified Lincoln as a person of good moral character, and on September 9, 1836 the Illinois Supreme Court examined him and issued a license to practice law in all courts of the state.
