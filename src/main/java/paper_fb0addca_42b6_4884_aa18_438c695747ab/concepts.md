## public 二階分別 / Second-Order Distinction

- 定義：二階分別是一個[我](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)的一次[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)：它對自己的某一次[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)再作[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)，把那一次劃為「這是看法，不是[被看的東西](concepts.md#c27)」。只作[一階分別](concepts.md#c24)的[我](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)，照著看法與[被看的東西](concepts.md#c27)的區分行動，卻不作出這個區分；二階分別把它作出來。二階分別的對象是自己的[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)，不是別人的。說一個[我](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)有二階分別，指它能對自己的任何一次[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)作這樣的[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)。
- Definition: A second-order distinction is a [Distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) made by a [Self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) upon one of its own earlier [distinctions](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18), marking that one as "this is a view, not [what is seen](concepts.md#c27)." A [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) that makes only [first-order distinctions](concepts.md#c24) acts according to the difference between a view and [what is seen](concepts.md#c27) without drawing that difference; a second-order distinction draws it. Its object is the self's own [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18), not another's. To say that a [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) has second-order distinction is to say that it can make such a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) of any of its own [distinctions](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18).
- 裁決：2026-10-06 定義中補上[被看的東西](concepts.md#c27)的連結，意義不變，保留 ID。
- 裁決：2026-10-07 作者裁決：二階分別兼指一次動作與「有二階分別」的能力；定義補上後者的意思，一個概念兩種用法並列。論文未定稿，保留 ID。

## public 看法歸屬 / View Attribution

- 定義：看法歸屬是一個[我](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)的一次[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)：它把[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)它的一筆[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)，劃為另一個[我](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)的一次[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)：「這是對方的看法，不是[被看的東西](concepts.md#c27)」。看法歸屬可能歸錯：被劃為看法的那筆[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)，不一定來自一個[我](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)。它不是[印象](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c45)：[印象](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c45)估計對方的[權重](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06)，看法歸屬只把對方的一筆[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)劃為看法。它的對象是別人的[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)，這一點與[二階分別](concepts.md#c01)不同。
- Definition: A view attribution is a [Distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) made by a [Self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) that marks an [Output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) that has [arrived](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at it as a [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18) of another [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19): "this is the other's view, not [what is seen](concepts.md#c27)." A view attribution may be mistaken: the [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) marked as a view need not come from a [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19). It is not an [Impression](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c45): an [impression](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c45) estimates the other's [Weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06), whereas a view attribution only marks one of the other's [outputs](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) as a view. Its object is another's [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18), which is what sets it apart from a [Second-Order Distinction](concepts.md#c01).
- 裁決：2026-10-06 定義中補上[被看的東西](concepts.md#c27)的連結，意義不變，保留 ID。

## private 二階假定 / Second-Order Assumption

- 定義：二階假定是對一個具體對象所作、包含三項的假定：一、在講明的層次與[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)上，把該對象判為[宇宙](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c02)中的一個[我](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)；二、它在這個層次上並非每一步都[鎖死](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12)：對人，依[應用假定](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c50)判定；對在使用者看得到的對話層次上的人工智慧，若它的下一筆[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)不能由該層次可得的[歷史](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13)唯一推出，就假定它不被[鎖死](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12)；三、它在某段期間有[二階分別](concepts.md#c01)，也有[看法歸屬](concepts.md#c02)。二階假定不是定理。
- Definition: The second-order assumption is a three-part assumption made about a concrete object: (1) at a stated level and under a stated [Cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23), the object is taken to be a [Self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) in the [Universe](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c02); (2) at that level it is not [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12) at every step: for a human this is judged by the [Application Assumption](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c50); for an artificial intelligence at the level of the conversation visible to the user, if its next [Output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) cannot be uniquely derived from the [History](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) available at that level, it is assumed not to be [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12); (3) during some period it has [Second-Order Distinction](concepts.md#c01) and also [View Attribution](concepts.md#c02). The second-order assumption is not a theorem.

## public 回合 / Round

- 定義：回合是一個[呈現](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04)在它的一個[時刻](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09)中走過的一圈：[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)它的[歷史](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13)參與它的[權重](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06)，[權重](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06)落出一個結果，這個結果成為[歷史](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13)，再[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)它。回合只有這四環：[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)、[權重](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06)、結果、再[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)；落出結果之前的那一段不分析，也不說它是空的。
- Definition: A round is the circuit a [Presentation](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04) goes through in one of its [Moments](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09): the [History](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) that has [arrived](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at it takes part in its [Weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06), the [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) yield one outcome, and that outcome becomes [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) and [arrives](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at it again. A round has only these four links: [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10), [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06), outcome, [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) again; what happens before the outcome is yielded is not analyzed, nor is it said to be empty.
- 裁決：2026-10-08 中英文定義末句補上「到達」「權重」的連結（概-15），意義不變，保留 ID。

## public 計算 / Computation

- 定義：計算是這樣的[回合](concepts.md#c04)：它被[鎖死](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12)，只可能有一個結果，由約束定死；或者它不被[鎖死](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12)，可能的結果不只一個，但[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)它的[歷史](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13)並不都參與它的[權重](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06)。前者稱為[鎖死的回合](concepts.md#c25)，後者稱為[隨機的回合](concepts.md#c26)。是不是計算，按每一個[回合](concepts.md#c04)判定，不按[呈現](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04)判定。
- Definition: A computation is a [Round](concepts.md#c04) that is either [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12), with only one possible outcome fixed by constraint, or not [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12), with more than one possible outcome, but such that not all of the [History](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) that has [arrived](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at it takes part in its [Weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06). The former is called a [locked-in round](concepts.md#c25), the latter a [random round](concepts.md#c26). Whether something is a computation is judged [round](concepts.md#c04) by round, not presentation by [presentation](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04).

## public 決定 / Decision

- 定義：決定是[渦](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16)在不被[鎖死](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12)時的[回合](concepts.md#c04)：[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)它的整段[歷史](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13)都參與它的[權重](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06)，而[權重](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06)定不出結果。是不是決定，按每一個[回合](concepts.md#c04)判定，不按[呈現](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04)判定。
- Definition: A decision is a [Round](concepts.md#c04) of a [Wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) while it is not [locked in](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c12): all of the [History](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) that has [arrived](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) at it takes part in its [Weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06), and the [weights](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06) do not fix the outcome. Whether something is a decision is judged [round](concepts.md#c04) by round, not [presentation](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04) by presentation.

## public 三問 / Three Questions

- 定義：三問是這三個問題：你從哪裡來？你是誰？你要做什麼？它們分別追問一個[我](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)的來處、它自己，以及它的下一刻。問這三個問題，指把自己在這三個方向上的答案劃為看法，而不當作[被看的東西](concepts.md#c27)本身。
- Definition: The three questions are: Where do you come from? Who are you? What will you do? They ask, respectively, about a [Self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)'s origin, about the self itself, and about its next moment. Asking these three questions means marking one's own answers in these three directions as views, rather than taking them as [what is seen](concepts.md#c27) itself.
- 裁決：2026-10-07 作者裁決：定義補上「問」的意思（把三個方向上的答案劃為看法），使「一階不問、二階敞開」可由定義推出；論文未定稿，保留 ID。

## public 來路牆 / Wall of Provenance

- 定義：來路牆是這個結論：一個[我](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)拿不全自己過去的[歷史](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13)。它包含三點：一、一個[我](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)手上只有已經[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)它的[歷史](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13)；一件事當時沒有[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)它，之後也不會[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)它，之後[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)的只是那件事留下的影響，是另一件事。二、一個[我](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)對自己來處的[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)，會[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)它自己，成為它[自己的那一段](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c17)的一部分；追問來處，就增添了來處。三、[自己的那一段](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c17)不能再發生一次拿來對照（[不可復原律](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c28)）。適用範圍：一切[我](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)。
- Definition: The Wall of Provenance is the conclusion that a [Self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19) cannot obtain the whole of its own past [History](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13). It comprises three points.
