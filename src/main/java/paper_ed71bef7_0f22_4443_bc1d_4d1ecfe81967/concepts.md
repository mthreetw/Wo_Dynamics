## public 經驗前提 / Empirical Premise

- 定義：經驗前提是由個案或既有測試得來、可以被新的個案或測試修改的前提。其中以[經驗禁止](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c51)的形式寫出的，只寫必要條件；另一種是對既有測試結果的登記，或由觀察歸納的概括，不是必要條件。
- Definition: An empirical premise is a premise drawn from cases or from existing tests, which new cases or tests can revise. Those written in the form of an [empirical prohibition](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c51) state only necessary conditions. The other kind registers the results of existing tests, or is a generalization induced from observations, and is not a necessary condition.
- 裁決：2026-11-11 與上游經驗禁止不是同一個概念：經驗前提一至三的形式是經驗禁止，經驗前提四不是；另立新概念，定義連到經驗禁止。
- 裁決：2026-11-11 新增經驗前提五（由觀察歸納的概括），定義改為涵蓋「對既有測試結果的登記，或由觀察歸納的概括」。
- 裁決：2026-11-13 全篇掃查指出定義重述上游經驗禁止的定義；刪去重述，改由連結承擔。

## private 理論極限 / Theoretical Limit

- 定義：理論極限是不因工程改善而改變的那一層限制，也就是[流變律](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c24)、[黑箱律](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c25)、[損耗律](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c26)與[不可復原律](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c28)。
- Definition: The theoretical limit is the layer of limits that does not change with engineering improvements, namely the [law of flux](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c24), the [law of the black box](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c25), the [law of attrition](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c26) and the [law of non-restoration](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c28).
- 裁決：2026-11-11 登記為獨立概念，修飾子為 private：它只以三層對照表中的一列界定。

- Definition: The engineering limit is the layer of limits that may change with engineering improvements, namely reception and the range measured by the [engineering capacities](concepts.md#c12).

## public 自我配置假定 / Self-Configuration Assumption

- 定義：自我配置假定是在講明的[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)下，對切出的某個[渦](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16)所作的假定：假定它以[分別](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18)定義自己。它不是定理，從外部確認[不被保證](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c02)。該[渦](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16)缺少[自他區辨](concepts.md#c48)或[跨時刻歸併](concepts.md#c49)所需的某項工程條件（留下自己[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)的副本並與隨後的[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)對照；跨[時刻](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09)保留，並把不同[時刻](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09)自己造成的[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)[壓縮](concepts.md#c31)為同一類），或找到機制證明它沒有任何[回合](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c04)是[決定](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c06)時，這項假定對它不成立。換一種[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)，這項假定可以落在不同的[渦](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16)上，也可以同時落在巢狀的[渦](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16)上。
- Definition: The self-configuration assumption is an assumption made, under a stated [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23), about some [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16) that has been cut out: it is assumed to define itself by [distinction](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c18). It is not a theorem, and its confirmation from outside is [not guaranteed](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c02). The assumption fails for that wo when it lacks one of the engineering conditions needed for [self-other discrimination](concepts.md#c48) or [cross-moment lumping](concepts.md#c49) (keeping a copy of its own [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28) and comparing it with the [arrivals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) that follow; retaining across [moments](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09), and [compressing](concepts.md#c31) the [arrivals](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) it caused at different [moments](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c09) into one kind), or when a mechanism is found showing that none of its [rounds](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c04) is a [decision](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c06). Under another cut, the assumption may fall on a different wo, or on nested wo at the same time.
- 裁決：2026-11-13 稽核指出定義寫「缺少這項假定的某項必要條件」，不讀論文無法得知是哪些條件；改為寫明是自他區辨或跨時刻歸併所需的工程條件。

## public 有損的推廣 / Generalized Lossiness

- 定義：有損的推廣是這個結論：一筆[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)一個[呈現](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04)的，不是另一個[渦](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16)[自己的那一段](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c17)的全部，因為後者一直追溯到[宇宙初態](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c14)，包括許多次[寫入](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c29)與[坍塌](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c08)的結果；這不依賴接收者是不是[我](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)。[有損](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c13)就一筆[到達](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10)、[被看的東西](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c27)是另一個[渦](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16)而言，是它落在[我](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19)身上的情形。
- Definition: Generalized lossiness is this conclusion: what one [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) brings to a [presentation](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c04) is not the whole of another [Wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16)'s [own segment](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c17), because the latter reaches back to [the initial state of the universe](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c14), including the results of many [writings](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c29) and [collapses](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c08); this does not depend on whether the receiver is a [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19). [Lossiness](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c13) for one [arrival](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c10) and when [what is seen](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c27) is another [Wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), is the case where it falls on a [self](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c19).
- 裁決：2026-11-12 稽核指出原定義對任何呈現、任何筆數的到達都說「不是全部」，來源只撐到一筆到達、對方是渦；作者裁定照推得出的範圍收窄。
- 裁決：2026-11-13 稽核指出定義只寫一筆到達是一次寫入中對它而言的那一部分，漏了論文並列的「某一次坍塌的結果」；依論文一之六的列舉補上。
- 裁決：2026-11-13 全篇掃查指出定義的前兩個分句重述上游刺激、到達、自己的那一段的定義；定義收為結論本身，推導留在論文一之六。

## private 定義隔離與更名不變性 / Definitional Isolation and Renaming Invariance

- 定義：定義隔離與更名不變性是這項記帳約定：[幻覺構件](../paper_08383b0a_1167_4efb_9b89_dc8a71afd5ff/concepts.md#c01)只依明示定義、適用條件、組合規則與可登記判準取得理論意義，自然語言名稱不帶入善惡、目的、因果、大小或心理內容。
- Definition: Definitional isolation and renaming invariance is this bookkeeping convention: an [illusory construct](../paper_08383b0a_1167_4efb_9b89_dc8a71afd5ff/concepts.md#c01) acquires theoretical meaning only through its explicit definition, conditions of application, rules of combination and registrable criteria; its natural-language name carries in no good or evil, purpose, causation, magnitude or psychological content.
- 裁決：2026-11-11 登記為獨立概念，修飾子為 private：它是記帳約定。

## public 構造 / Structure

- 定義：構造是載體上可使用、可改變的參數、連結、神經結構、動態狀態等。衡量它的指標是[構造能力](concepts.md#c17)。構造不是[權重](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06)。
- Definition: Structure is the usable and changeable parameters, connections, neural structures, dynamic states and the like on a carrier. The indicator that measures it is [structure capacity](concepts.md#c17). Structure is not [weight](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c06).

## public 運算 / Operation

- 定義：運算是以一個或多個[表示](concepts.md#c34)為輸入、落出一個[表示](concepts.md#c34)的映射，例如變換、組合、比較、推進。說一個[節點](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25)作運算，指研究者把它的[回合](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c04)讀作運算。衡量它的指標是[運算能力](concepts.md#c14)。
- Definition: An operation is a mapping that takes one or more [representations](concepts.md#c34) as input and yields a [representation](concepts.md#c34), such as transformation, combination, comparison or advancing. To say that a [node](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c25) performs an operation is to say that a researcher reads its [round](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c04) as an operation. The indicator that measures it is [operation capacity](concepts.md#c14).
- 裁決：2026-11-13 全篇掃查指出本條說運算就是變換，運算能力卻把變換與組合、比較、推進並列；作者裁定依數學與計算理論的通行用法，改為以表示為輸入的映射。

## private 目前的人工智慧 / Current Artificial Intelligence

- 定義：目前的人工智慧，是在邏輯狀態這個[切法](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23)下，以圖靈機為設計基底、每一[回合](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c04)不是[鎖死的回合](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c25)就是[隨機的回合](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c26)的數位人工智慧。
- Definition: Current artificial intelligence is digital artificial intelligence designed on the basis of the Turing machine, every one of whose [rounds](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c04), under the [cut](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c23) of logical state, is either a [locked-in round](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c25) or a [random round](../paper_fb0addca_42b6_4884_aa18_438c695747ab/concepts.md#c26).

## public 工程能力 / Engineering Capacity

- 定義：工程能力是用來衡量載體作某類動作或某類保留能到什麼程度的指標，有[容納能力](concepts.md#c13)、[運算能力](concepts.md#c14)、[記憶能力](concepts.md#c15)、[壓縮能力](concepts.md#c16)與[構造能力](concepts.md#c17)五項。它們不是[渦](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16)的必要且充分條件，也不窮盡[渦](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16)的能力。
- Definition: An engineering capacity is an indicator used to measure to what degree a carrier can perform some kind of action or some kind of retention. There are five: [containment capacity](concepts.md#c13), [operation capacity](concepts.md#c14), [memory capacity](concepts.md#c15), [compression capacity](concepts.md#c16) and [structure capacity](concepts.md#c17). They are not necessary and sufficient conditions of a [wo](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c16), nor do they exhaust the capacities of a wo.

<a id="c13"></a>
## public 容納能力 / Containment Capacity

- 定義：容納能力是[工程能力](concepts.md#c12)的一項：在既定結構中，能區分、表示並同時處理多少輸入差異及其關係。
- Definition: Containment capacity is one of the [engineering capacities](concepts.md#c12): how many input differences and their relations can be distinguished, represented and handled at once within a given structure.

<a id="c14"></a>
## public 運算能力 / Operation Capacity

- 定義：運算能力是[工程能力](concepts.md#c12)的一項：在有限資源與時間內，能對[表示](concepts.md#c34)作多少、多快的[運算](concepts.md#c08)：變換、組合、比較、推進。
- Definition: Operation capacity is one of the [engineering capacities](concepts.md#c12): how many [operations](concepts.md#c08), and how fast, can be performed on a [representation](concepts.md#c34) with limited resources and time: transforming, combining, comparing, advancing.

<a id="c15"></a>
## public 記憶能力 / Memory Capacity

- 定義：記憶能力是[工程能力](concepts.md#c12)的一項：能保留多少先前的輸入、狀態或[運算](concepts.md#c08)結果，並在後續取用。
- Definition: Memory capacity is one of the [engineering capacities](concepts.md#c12): how much of earlier inputs, states or [operation](concepts.md#c08) results can be retained and later retrieved.

<a id="c16"></a>
## public 壓縮能力 / Compression Capacity

- 定義：壓縮能力是[工程能力](concepts.md#c12)的一項：以有限資源重新表示資訊時，能保住多少指定用途所需的差異；它衡量的是[壓縮](concepts.md#c31)。
- Definition: Compression capacity is one of the [engineering capacities](concepts.md#c12): how many of the differences needed for a specified use can be kept when information is re-represented with limited resources. It measures to what degree [compression](concepts.md#c31) can be carried out.

<a id="c17"></a>
## public 構造能力 / Structure Capacity

- 定義：構造能力是[工程能力](concepts.md#c12)的一項：既有[構造](concepts.md#c07)參與[輸出](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28)形成的程度；有[學習](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c04)時，[構造](concepts.md#c07)隨[歷史](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13)與回饋能改變多少。
- Definition: Structure capacity is one of the [engineering capacities](concepts.md#c12): the degree to which existing [structure](concepts.md#c07) takes part in forming [output](../paper_b9df3b68_23ee_4839_965b_ed0e6b283ee6/concepts.md#c28); and, where there is [learning](../paper_2ccbf179_58cf_4851_bd72_5fa886e4b2c3/concepts.md#c04), how much structure can change with [history](../paper_37b3643a_dc40_4463_8b36_4f4594acd986/concepts.md#c13) and feedback.

## public 功能 / Function
