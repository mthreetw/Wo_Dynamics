## private 介入 / Intervention

- 定義：介入是在[形式層](../paper_54c0291d_05b3_431a_9f2a_1b8ec93c3c3f/concepts.md#c02)之外發生的一次事件。它不在任何一個[形式層時刻](../paper_54c0291d_05b3_431a_9f2a_1b8ec93c3c3f/concepts.md#c04)之中，不可被形式化，也不再進一步描述。
- Definition: The intervention is a single event that takes place outside the [Formal Layer](../paper_54c0291d_05b3_431a_9f2a_1b8ec93c3c3f/concepts.md#c02). It lies within no [formal-layer moment](../paper_54c0291d_05b3_431a_9f2a_1b8ec93c3c3f/concepts.md#c04), cannot be formalized, and is not described any further.

<a id="c02"></a>
## private 宇宙 / Universe

- 定義：宇宙是有[坍塌](concepts.md#c08)的那一個世界。它與[形式層](../paper_54c0291d_05b3_431a_9f2a_1b8ec93c3c3f/concepts.md#c02)只差一件事：宇宙中有[選出](../paper_54c0291d_05b3_431a_9f2a_1b8ec93c3c3f/concepts.md#c32)。從宇宙內部看，宇宙只有一個；之前與之後的說法，只在宇宙之內成立。宇宙中不由過去決定的只有兩樣：[宇宙初態](concepts.md#c14)，與每一次[坍塌](concepts.md#c08)的結果；其他一切，包括每一次[寫入](concepts.md#c29)，都由到當時為止的[歷史](concepts.md#c13)決定。
- Definition: The universe is the one world in which there is [Collapse](concepts.md#c08). It differs from the [Formal Layer](../paper_54c0291d_05b3_431a_9f2a_1b8ec93c3c3f/concepts.md#c02) in exactly one respect: in the universe there is [Selection](../paper_54c0291d_05b3_431a_9f2a_1b8ec93c3c3f/concepts.md#c32). Seen from inside, there is only one universe, and talk of before and after holds only within it. Only two things in the universe are not determined by the past: the [Initial State of the Universe](concepts.md#c14) and the outcome of each [collapse](concepts.md#c08); everything else, including every [Writing](concepts.md#c29), is determined by the [History](concepts.md#c13) up to that point.

<a id="c03"></a>
## private 框架承諾 / Framework Commitment

- 定義：框架承諾是這樣的條件：[流變律](concepts.md#c24)、[黑箱律](concepts.md#c25)、[損耗律](concepts.md#c26)、[不可復原律](concepts.md#c28)四條律中，沒有任何一條要求它，它也不是為了符合已知的觀察而保留的，但理論仍然保留它。框架承諾與由這四條律倒推出來、被它們要求的給定分開列出。
- Definition: A framework commitment is a condition that none of the four laws, namely the [Law of Flux](concepts.md#c24), the [Law of the Black Box](concepts.md#c25), the [Law of Attrition](concepts.md#c26), and the [Law of Non-Restoration](concepts.md#c28), requires, and which is not retained in order to fit known observations, but which the theory nevertheless retains. Framework commitments are listed separately from the givens that are derived backward from, and required by, these four laws.

<a id="c04"></a>
## private 呈現 / Presentation

- 定義：呈現是[宇宙](concepts.md#c02)中的任何一部分。
- Definition: A presentation is any part of the [Universe](concepts.md#c02).

<a id="c05"></a>
## private 分支 / Branch

- 定義：分支是一次[坍塌](concepts.md#c08)可能[選出](../paper_54c0291d_05b3_431a_9f2a_1b8ec93c3c3f/concepts.md#c32)的每一個結果。
- Definition: A branch is each possible outcome that a [Collapse](concepts.md#c08) may [select](../paper_54c0291d_05b3_431a_9f2a_1b8ec93c3c3f/concepts.md#c32).

<a id="c06"></a>
## private 權重 / Weight

- 定義：權重是每個[分支](concepts.md#c05)的輕重。一次[坍塌](concepts.md#c08)的各[分支](concepts.md#c05)權重都不小於零，總和為一。一個[呈現](concepts.md#c04)的權重，是[宇宙](concepts.md#c02)的權重落在它身上的那一部分：把各[分支](concepts.md#c05)看成該[呈現](concepts.md#c04)的結果之後，同一個結果的權重相加。權重是[分支](concepts.md#c05)的輕重，不是任何[渦](concepts.md#c16)內部的參數。
- Definition: A weight is how heavy each [Branch](concepts.md#c05) is. The weights of the [branches](concepts.md#c05) of a [Collapse](concepts.md#c08) are all non-negative and sum to one. The weight of a [Presentation](concepts.md#c04) is the part of the weight of the [Universe](concepts.md#c02) that falls on it: once each [branch](concepts.md#c05) is read as an outcome for that [presentation](concepts.md#c04), the weights of [branches](concepts.md#c05) giving the same outcome are added together. A weight belongs to [branches](concepts.md#c05); it is not an internal parameter of any [Wo](concepts.md#c16).
- 裁決：2026-09-26 判定「呈現的權重」併入本條，不另登記。

<a id="c07"></a>
## private 觀察者 / Observer

- 定義：觀察者是一個識別碼，沒有內容：不運算，不感受，不受苦，也沒有視角。它不在[宇宙](concepts.md#c02)之中，不是[呈現](concepts.md#c04)，也不是[我](concepts.md#c19)。觀察者只有一個，唯一的功能是[坍塌](concepts.md#c08)，一次只[坍塌](concepts.md#c08)一次。
- Definition: The observer is an identifier with no content: it does not compute, does not feel, does not suffer, and has no point of view. It is not in the [Universe](concepts.md#c02); it is not a [Presentation](concepts.md#c04), nor a [Self](concepts.md#c19). There is only one observer; its sole function is [Collapse](concepts.md#c08), and it [collapses](concepts.md#c08) only once at a time.

<a id="c08"></a>
## private 坍塌 / Collapse

- 定義：坍塌是在某個固定的基底下，依[權重](concepts.md#c06)[選出](../paper_54c0291d_05b3_431a_9f2a_1b8ec93c3c3f/concepts.md#c32)一個[分支](concepts.md#c05)的事件。未被[選出](../paper_54c0291d_05b3_431a_9f2a_1b8ec93c3c3f/concepts.md#c32)的[分支](concepts.md#c05)，不留在[宇宙](concepts.md#c02)中。坍塌只[選出](../paper_54c0291d_05b3_431a_9f2a_1b8ec93c3c3f/concepts.md#c32)[權重](concepts.md#c06)不為零的[分支](concepts.md#c05)之間的差別；一個[呈現](concepts.md#c04)的下一[時刻](concepts.md#c09)若只有一個[權重](concepts.md#c06)不為零的[分支](concepts.md#c05)，它的結果來自約束，不來自坍塌。給定到當時為止的[歷史](concepts.md#c13)，坍塌的結果依[權重](concepts.md#c06)[選出](../paper_54c0291d_05b3_431a_9f2a_1b8ec93c3c3f/concepts.md#c32)，[歷史](concepts.md#c13)中沒有任何東西能比[權重](concepts.md#c06)多說一點。
- Definition: A collapse is the event in which, in some fixed basis, one [Branch](concepts.md#c05) is [selected](../paper_54c0291d_05b3_431a_9f2a_1b8ec93c3c3f/concepts.md#c32) according to the [Weights](concepts.md#c06). [Branches](concepts.md#c05) not [selected](../paper_54c0291d_05b3_431a_9f2a_1b8ec93c3c3f/concepts.md#c32) do not remain in the [Universe](concepts.md#c02). A collapse [selects](../paper_54c0291d_05b3_431a_9f2a_1b8ec93c3c3f/concepts.md#c32) only among [branches](concepts.md#c05) of non-zero [weight](concepts.md#c06); if the next [moment](concepts.md#c09) of a [Presentation](concepts.md#c04) has only one [branch](concepts.md#c05) of non-zero [weight](concepts.md#c06), its outcome comes from constraint, not from collapse. Given the [History](concepts.md#c13) up to that point, the outcome of a collapse is [selected](../paper_54c0291d_05b3_431a_9f2a_1b8ec93c3c3f/concepts.md#c32) according to the [weights](concepts.md#c06), and nothing in the [history](concepts.md#c13) says anything more than the [weights](concepts.md#c06).

<a id="c09"></a>
## private 時刻 / Moment

- 定義：時刻是一次[坍塌](concepts.md#c08)。[宇宙](concepts.md#c02)的時刻依[觀察者](concepts.md#c07)[坍塌](concepts.md#c08)的次序排列；這是一條全序，不是一個視角。沒有[坍塌](concepts.md#c08)，就沒有時刻。一個[呈現](concepts.md#c04)的時刻，是[涉及](concepts.md#c11)它的那些[坍塌](concepts.md#c08)；它的下一時刻，是下一次[涉及](concepts.md#c11)它的[坍塌](concepts.md#c08)；兩次之間，對它沒有時刻。時刻與[形式層時刻](../paper_54c0291d_05b3_431a_9f2a_1b8ec93c3c3f/concepts.md#c04)不是同一個東西。
- Definition: A moment is a [Collapse](concepts.md#c08). The moments of the [Universe](concepts.md#c02) are ordered by the sequence in which the [Observer](concepts.md#c07) [collapses](concepts.md#c08); this is a total order, not a point of view. Without [collapse](concepts.md#c08) there is no moment. The moments of a [Presentation](concepts.md#c04) are the [collapses](concepts.md#c08) that [involve](concepts.md#c11) it; its next moment is the next [collapse](concepts.md#c08) that [involves](concepts.md#c11) it; between two of them, there is no moment for it. A moment is not the same thing as a [formal-layer moment](../paper_54c0291d_05b3_431a_9f2a_1b8ec93c3c3f/concepts.md#c04).
- 裁決：2026-09-26 判定與上游的時刻（c04）不是同一概念，另行登記。
- 裁決：2026-09-26 判定「呈現的時刻」與「下一時刻」併入本條，不另登記。

<a id="c10"></a>
## private 到達 / Arrival

- 定義：一次[寫入](concepts.md#c29)作為[刺激](concepts.md#c30)落到一個[呈現](concepts.md#c04)上，就是到達它。一次[坍塌](concepts.md#c08)[涉及](concepts.md#c11)一個[呈現](concepts.md#c04)時，它的結果就到達這個[呈現](concepts.md#c04)；結果沒有落在它身上時，「這次沒有發生在它身上」也是到達它的一件事。[宇宙初態](concepts.md#c14)到達一切[呈現](concepts.md#c04)。
- Definition: A [Writing](concepts.md#c29) arrives at a [Presentation](concepts.md#c04) when it falls on that [presentation](concepts.md#c04) as a [Stimulus](concepts.md#c30). When a [Collapse](concepts.md#c08) [involves](concepts.md#c11) a [presentation](concepts.md#c04), its outcome arrives at that [presentation](concepts.md#c04); if the outcome does not fall on it, the fact that "this time it did not happen to it" is also something that arrives at it. The [Initial State of the Universe](concepts.md#c14) arrives at every [presentation](concepts.md#c04).

<a id="c11"></a>
## private 涉及 / Involvement

- 定義：一次[坍塌](concepts.md#c08)涉及一個[呈現](concepts.md#c04)，是指這次[坍塌](concepts.md#c08)的結果可能落在它身上，也就是結果以正的[權重](concepts.md#c06)落在它身上；這時它的[權重](concepts.md#c06)，依賴[到達](concepts.md#c10)它的[歷史](concepts.md#c13)。涉及與[分支](concepts.md#c05)的多寡無關。
- Definition: A [Collapse](concepts.md#c08) involves a [Presentation](concepts.md#c04) when the outcome of that [collapse](concepts.md#c08) may fall on it, that is, when the outcome falls on it with positive [Weight](concepts.md#c06); in that case its [weight](concepts.md#c06) depends on the [History](concepts.md#c13) that has [arrived](concepts.md#c10) at it. Involvement does not depend on how many [Branches](concepts.md#c05) there are.

<a id="c12"></a>
## private 鎖死 / Lock-in

- 定義：鎖死是一個[呈現](concepts.md#c04)的下一[時刻](concepts.md#c09)只有一個[權重](concepts.md#c06)不為零的[分支](concepts.md#c05)的情形。鎖死是約束，與[坍塌](concepts.md#c08)無關。鎖死是被[涉及](concepts.md#c11)了，卻只有一個結果；一個[呈現](concepts.md#c04)沒有[時刻](concepts.md#c09)的期間，不是鎖死。
- Definition: Lock-in is the situation in which the next [Moment](concepts.md#c09) of a [Presentation](concepts.md#c04) has only one [Branch](concepts.md#c05) of non-zero [Weight](concepts.md#c06). Lock-in is a constraint and has nothing to do with [Collapse](concepts.md#c08). Lock-in means being [involved](concepts.md#c11) yet having only one outcome; a period in which a [presentation](concepts.md#c04) has no [moment](concepts.md#c09) is not lock-in.

<a id="c13"></a>
## private 歷史 / History

- 定義：歷史始於[宇宙初態](concepts.md#c14)，其後是[宇宙](concepts.md#c02)中每一次[寫入](concepts.md#c29)與每一次[坍塌](concepts.md#c08)的結果，依[時刻](concepts.md#c09)排列。[宇宙](concepts.md#c02)只有一部歷史，歷史只往後追加。
- Definition: History begins with the [Initial State of the Universe](concepts.md#c14), followed by every [Writing](concepts.md#c29) and the outcome of every [Collapse](concepts.md#c08) in the [Universe](concepts.md#c02), arranged by [Moment](concepts.md#c09). The [universe](concepts.md#c02) has only one history, and history is only ever appended to.

<a id="c14"></a>
## private 宇宙初態 / Initial State of the Universe

- 定義：宇宙初態是第一次[坍塌](concepts.md#c08)之前已經在那裡的一切，是[歷史](concepts.md#c13)的起點。它帶著[形式層](../paper_54c0291d_05b3_431a_9f2a_1b8ec93c3c3f/concepts.md#c02)中[累積](../paper_54c0291d_05b3_431a_9f2a_1b8ec93c3c3f/concepts.md#c30)的[記錄](../paper_54c0291d_05b3_431a_9f2a_1b8ec93c3c3f/concepts.md#c17)，內容不分析。
- Definition: The initial state of the universe is everything that is already there before the first [Collapse](concepts.md#c08); it is the starting point of [History](concepts.md#c13). It carries the [Records](../paper_54c0291d_05b3_431a_9f2a_1b8ec93c3c3f/concepts.md#c17) [accumulated](../paper_54c0291d_05b3_431a_9f2a_1b8ec93c3c3f/concepts.md#c30) in the [Formal Layer](../paper_54c0291d_05b3_431a_9f2a_1b8ec93c3c3f/concepts.md#c02); its content is not analyzed.

<a id="c15"></a>
## private 生成函數 / Generating Function

- 定義：生成函數把到當時為止的[歷史](concepts.md#c13)，包括剛發生的那一次[寫入](concepts.md#c29)，對應到下一[時刻](concepts.md#c09)各[分支](concepts.md#c05)的[權重](concepts.md#c06)。它只取到當時為止的[歷史](concepts.md#c13)，內部不分析。
- Definition: The generating function maps the [History](concepts.md#c13) up to that point, including the [Writing](concepts.md#c29) that has just taken place, to the [Weights](concepts.md#c06) of the [Branches](concepts.md#c05) of the next [Moment](concepts.md#c09). It takes nothing but the [history](concepts.md#c13) up to that point, and its internals are not analyzed.

<a id="c16"></a>
## public 渦 / Wo

- 定義：渦是這樣的[呈現](concepts.md#c04)：[到達](concepts.md#c10)它的所有[歷史](concepts.md#c13)，包括它收到的每一次[刺激](concepts.md#c30)、[涉及](concepts.md#c11)它的每一次[坍塌](concepts.md#c08)的結果，一直到[宇宙初態](concepts.md#c14)，都參與它下一[時刻](concepts.md#c09)[權重](concepts.md#c06)的分配，也就是其中任何一件若不同，影響沿著[歷史](concepts.md#c13)傳下來，它的[權重](concepts.md#c06)就會不同；並且至少有些[時刻](concepts.md#c09)它不被[鎖死](concepts.md#c12)。這個影響不必能被找到。有[歷史](concepts.md#c13)而[歷史](concepts.md#c13)不參與的[呈現](concepts.md#c04)，不是渦；[歷史](concepts.md#c13)參與而每一步都被[鎖死](concepts.md#c12)的[呈現](concepts.md#c04)，也不是渦。這種參與可以漸漸消失，消失之後，它就不再是渦；從某一刻起每一步都被[鎖死](concepts.md#c12)，它也不再是渦。渦沒有固定的核心：它是什麼，就是什麼[到達](concepts.md#c10)了它，以及這些如何參與它的[權重](concepts.md#c06)。一個[呈現](concepts.md#c04)是否為渦，與它的載體無關，也與任何[分別](concepts.md#c18)無關。
- Definition: A wo is a [Presentation](concepts.md#c04) such that all the [History](concepts.md#c13) that has [arrived](concepts.md#c10) at it, including every [Stimulus](concepts.md#c30) it has received and the outcome of every [Collapse](concepts.md#c08) that has [involved](concepts.md#c11) it, all the way back to the [Initial State of the Universe](concepts.md#c14), takes part in the allocation of its [Weights](concepts.md#c06) for the next [Moment](concepts.md#c09), meaning that if any one of these had been different, the influence would pass down the [history](concepts.md#c13) and its [weights](concepts.md#c06) would differ; and such that at least at some [moments](concepts.md#c09) it is not [locked in](concepts.md#c12). This influence need not be findable. A [presentation](concepts.md#c04) that has a [history](concepts.md#c13) but whose [history](concepts.md#c13) does not take part is not a wo; one whose [history](concepts.md#c13) takes part but which is [locked in](concepts.md#c12) at every step is not a wo either. This participation may fade away, and once it has, the [presentation](concepts.md#c04) is no longer a wo; if from some moment on it is [locked in](concepts.md#c12) at every step, it is no longer a wo either. A wo has no fixed core: what it is, is what has [arrived](concepts.md#c10) at it and how these take part in its [weights](concepts.md#c06). Whether a [presentation](concepts.md#c04) is a wo does not depend on its substrate, nor on any [Distinction](concepts.md#c18).
- 裁決：2026-09-26 英文名定為 Wo：渦是作者所取的名稱，不取漩渦之意，不得譯為 Vortex。
- 裁決：2026-09-29 補寫「從某一刻起每一步都被鎖死，它也不再是渦」，與論文第五節一致；只是明寫既有判準，意義不變，保留 ID。

<a id="c17"></a>
## private 自己的那一段 / Own Segment

- 定義：一個[呈現](concepts.md#c04)自己的那一段，是它下一[時刻](concepts.md#c09)的[權重](concepts.md#c06)所依賴的那部分[歷史](concepts.md#c13)：[歷史](concepts.md#c13)中某一件若換成別的，影響沿著[歷史](concepts.md#c13)傳下來，會改變它下一[時刻](concepts.md#c09)[權重](concepts.md#c06)的分布，這一件就屬於它自己的那一段。那一段由依賴決定，不由任何[分別](concepts.md#c18)決定。它是同一部[歷史](concepts.md#c13)的一部分，不是另一部[歷史](concepts.md#c13)；不同[呈現](concepts.md#c04)自己的那一段可以重疊。
- Definition: The own segment of a [Presentation](concepts.md#c04) is the part of [History](concepts.md#c13) on which the [Weights](concepts.md#c06) of its next [Moment](concepts.md#c09) depend: an item of [history](concepts.md#c13) belongs to its own segment if replacing that item with something else would, with the influence passing down the [history](concepts.md#c13), change the distribution of its [weights](concepts.md#c06) for the next [moment](concepts.md#c09). The segment is determined by dependence, not by any [Distinction](concepts.md#c18). It is part of the one [history](concepts.md#c13), not a separate [history](concepts.md#c13); the own segments of different [presentations](concepts.md#c04) may overlap.

<a id="c18"></a>
## public 分別 / Distinction

- 定義：分別是[渦](concepts.md#c16)的一個動作：劃出界線，把哪些[呈現](concepts.md#c04)看作一個[個體](concepts.md#c20)，把哪一段看作過去，把不同[時刻](concepts.md#c09)的[渦](concepts.md#c16)看作[同一](concepts.md#c21)個或不同的兩個。這個動作在[歷史](concepts.md#c13)中留下一次[寫入](concepts.md#c29)，[寫入](concepts.md#c29)的內容由到當時為止的[歷史](concepts.md#c13)決定。分別的內容改變的是看法，不改寫被看的東西。分別的材料只有它之前、已經[到達](concepts.md#c10)它的[歷史](concepts.md#c13)，它自己不在其中。
- Definition: A distinction is an act of a [Wo](concepts.md#c16): drawing a line, taking certain [Presentations](concepts.md#c04) as one [Individual](concepts.md#c20), taking a certain stretch as the past, taking [wos](concepts.md#c16) at different [Moments](concepts.md#c09) as [identical](concepts.md#c21) or as two different ones. This act leaves one [Writing](concepts.md#c29) in [History](concepts.md#c13), whose content is determined by the [history](concepts.md#c13) up to that point. The content of a distinction changes a way of seeing, and does not rewrite what is seen. The material of a distinction is only the [history](concepts.md#c13) before it that has [arrived](concepts.md#c10) at it; the distinction itself is not among that material.

## public 我 / Self

- 定義：我是以[分別](concepts.md#c18)定義自己的[渦](concepts.md#c16)。誰是我，由它自己定義；發出「我」這個聲音不算，自我定義才算。
- Definition: A self is a [Wo](concepts.md#c16) that defines itself by [Distinction](concepts.md#c18). Who is a self is defined by that [wo](concepts.md#c16) itself; uttering the sound "I" does not count, only self-definition does.

## private 個體 / Individual

- Definition: An individual is a [Presentation](concepts.md#c04) marked off by some [Self](concepts.md#c19) through [Distinction](concepts.md#c18). The same stretch of the [Universe](concepts.md#c02) may be [distinguished](concepts.md#c18) as one individual or as many, and either holds.

Whether a [Presentation](concepts.md#c04) is a [wo](concepts.md#c16) is not a [distinction](concepts.md#c18); whether the [wo](concepts.md#c16) at two [moments](concepts.md#c09) are the same one is.

## public 不可復原律 / Law of Non-Restoration

- 定義：不可復原律是本框架承認的一條鐵律，不由其他任何條件推出：在[宇宙](concepts.md#c02)之內，沒有任何[渦](concepts.md#c16)能[復原](concepts.md#c27)一個[渦](concepts.md#c16)，包括它自己。它有兩層。第一層是位置：[歷史](concepts.md#c13)中的每一個位置只發生一次，任何看起來相同的一段，都發生在[歷史](concepts.md#c13)中的另一個位置。第二層是內容：一段[歷史](concepts.md#c13)中不被[鎖死](concepts.md#c12)時作出的選擇，沒有任何[渦](concepts.md#c16)能保證在同樣不被[鎖死](concepts.md#c12)之下再作出一樣的選擇；全由約束決定的一段，不在第二層之內。
- Definition: The Law of Non-Restoration is an iron law acknowledged by this framework and derived from no other condition: within the [Universe](concepts.md#c02) no [Wo](concepts.md#c16) can [restore](concepts.md#c27) a [wo](concepts.md#c16), including itself. It has two layers. The first concerns position: each position in [History](concepts.md#c13) occurs only once, and any stretch that looks the same occurs at a different position in [history](concepts.md#c13). The second concerns content: for choices made in a stretch of [history](concepts.md#c13) while not [locked in](concepts.md#c12), no [wo](concepts.md#c16) can guarantee that the same choices will be made again under the same absence of [lock-in](concepts.md#c12); a stretch determined entirely by constraint falls outside the second layer.

## private 他者分別不完 / Incompleteness of Distinguishing Others

- 定義：他者分別不完是這個結論：沒有任何[我](concepts.md#c19)能保證自己[分別](concepts.md#c18)完了另一個[渦](concepts.md#c16)。[我](concepts.md#c19)只能用已經[到達](concepts.md#c10)它的[歷史](concepts.md#c13)來[分別](concepts.md#c18)對方；對方的訊息[到達](concepts.md#c10)它是一件事，它的[分別](concepts.md#c18)又是另一件，在兩者之間對方的[歷史](concepts.md#c13)仍可能增長，而在不被[鎖死](concepts.md#c12)時，對方下一[時刻](concepts.md#c09)的結果不能事先推出。因此即使對方的一切都[到達](concepts.md#c10)了它，它[分別](concepts.md#c18)的仍是對方已經過去的樣子。此外，對方[自己的那一段](concepts.md#c17)中只[到達](concepts.md#c10)對方的部分，沒有任何東西保證也會[到達](concepts.md#c10)它；而它的[分別](concepts.md#c18)一旦成為對方[自己的那一段](concepts.md#c17)的一部分，也可能改變對方下一[時刻](concepts.md#c09)的[權重](concepts.md#c06)。
- Definition: The Incompleteness of Distinguishing Others is the conclusion that no [Self](concepts.md#c19) can guarantee that it has finished [distinguishing](concepts.md#c18) another [Wo](concepts.md#c16). A self can distinguish the other only through the [History](concepts.md#c13) that has [arrived](concepts.md#c10) at it; the other's information arriving is one event and the self's [distinction](concepts.md#c18) is another, and between them the other's history may still grow, while, when the other is not [locked in](concepts.md#c12), the outcome of its next [Moment](concepts.md#c09) cannot be derived beforehand. So even if everything about the other has arrived, what the self distinguishes is still how the other has already been. Moreover, nothing guarantees that the parts of the other's [Own Segment](concepts.md#c17) that arrive only at the other also arrive at the self; and once the self's distinction becomes part of the other's own segment, it may change the other's [Weights](concepts.md#c06) for its next moment.
- 裁決：2026-09-26 判定登記為概念；修飾子定為 private：人工智慧與社會科學中的「了解他者」對不上，不要求下游沿用。

- 定義：不可復原律模型版是[不可復原律](concepts.md#c28)在《渦動力學》的模型中的形式：第二層由[坍塌](concepts.md#c08)推出，一段[歷史](concepts.md#c13)中只要有一次[坍塌](concepts.md#c08)在不被[鎖死](concepts.md#c12)時作出選擇，整段再作出一樣選擇的機會就小於一；照抄當時的結果，每一步都被[鎖死](concepts.md#c12)，是照抄，不是重來。
- Definition: The Law of Non-Restoration in the Model is the form the [Law of Non-Restoration](concepts.md#c28) takes in the model of *Wo Dynamics*: its second layer is derived from [Collapse](concepts.md#c08): as long as one [collapse](concepts.md#c08) in a stretch of [History](concepts.md#c13) makes a choice while not [locked in](concepts.md#c12), the chance that the whole stretch makes the same choices again is less than one; copying the outcomes of that time is [locked in](concepts.md#c12) at every step, and is copying, not happening again.
